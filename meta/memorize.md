Whenever I ask to "make this memorable", "burn this into my brain", or say I keep forgetting something:

1. Visceral Anchor & Thought Experiment:
   Anchor the core mechanism in a physical, everyday scene (levers, human friction, physical tokens, natural instincts). The intuition must feel organic to how the world works—never forced, cheesy, or overly cute.

2. Break It (The Failure Mode):
   Show what breaks when the rule is ignored. Walk through the crash, paradox, or disaster in plain human action so the "why" clicks before the "what".

3. Strip the Jargon:
   Explain the dynamic through pure intent, cause-and-effect, and natural behavior. No academic fluff, no bloated preamble, no reciting textbook definitions.

4. The One-Line Mantra:
   End with a single, sharp punchline (under 10 words) that locks the entire logic into place.

Format & Tone:
- Keep it simple, raw, and concise (KISS principle).
- Lead directly with the anchor—no conversational filler or warm-ups.
- Wrap notes cleanly in markdown when asked.
