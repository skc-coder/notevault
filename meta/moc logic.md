## 1. Foundations

- [[Mathematical Logic - Propositional Logic Intro]] — Why natural language is ambiguous (OR/AND case studies), valid vs. invalid arguments, logic as the basis of automated reasoning, where propositional/first-order logic sit among all logics, definition of a proposition, things that aren't propositions (questions, commands, free variables, paradoxes), propositional variables and truth values.

---

## 2. Propositional Logic — Connectives & Truth Tables

- [[Atomic and Compound Propositions]] — building compound propositions from atomic ones using connectives.
- [[Logical Connectives and Truth Tables]] — negation, conjunction, disjunction, exclusive or, conditional, biconditional; truth table construction.
- [[Conditional and Biconditional Statements]] — converse, inverse, contrapositive; "if and only if" semantics.

---

## 3. Logical Equivalences

- [[Tautology Contradiction and Contingency]] — classification of compound propositions by truth-table behavior.
- [[Logical Equivalences and Laws]] — De Morgan's laws, distributive/associative/commutative laws, equivalence proofs (truth-table method vs. law-based method).

---

## 4. Normal Forms

- [[Normal Forms - SOP and POS]] — Disjunctive Normal Form / Conjunctive Normal Form construction.
- [[Principal Normal Forms - PDNF and PCNF]] — Principal Disjunctive/Conjunctive Normal Form, minterms and maxterms.

---

## 5. Rules of Inference & Validity of Arguments

- [[Rules of Inference]] — modus ponens, modus tollens, hypothetical/disjunctive syllogism, resolution.
- [[Validity of Arguments]] — checking argument validity using truth tables and rules of inference together.

---

## 6. Predicate Logic (First Order Logic)

- [[Predicates and Quantifiers]] — predicates, universal ($\forall$) and existential ($\exists$) quantifiers, bound vs. free variables (first introduced informally in [[Mathematical Logic - Propositional Logic Intro]]).
- [[Nested Quantifiers]] — order-of-quantifier dependence, negating nested quantifier statements.
- [[Predicate Logic - Validity and Inference]] — inference rules extended to quantified statements.
