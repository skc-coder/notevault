## 1. Percentage to M

### Step 1: Reduce the Percentage to Base Fraction $\frac{a}{b}$
Convert the standalone numerical percentage into its lowest fractional equivalent:
$$\pm \Delta\% = \frac{x}{100} = \pm\frac{a}{b}$$

### Step 2: Form the Multiplier ($M$)

  $$M = 1 \pm \frac{a}{b} = \frac{b \pm a}{b}$$
  
---

## 2. M to percentage

Inspect the fraction $M = \frac{\text{Numerator}}{\text{Denominator}}$:

* **If $M > 1$ (Increase):**
  $$\Delta\% = (M - 1) \times 100\% = \left(\frac{\text{Numerator} - \text{Denominator}}{\text{Denominator}}\right) \times 100\%$$
  * *Example:* $M = \frac{7}{5} \implies \frac{7 - 5}{5} = +\frac{2}{5} = \mathbf{+40\%}$

* **If $M < 1$ (Decrease):**
  $$\Delta\% = (1 - M) \times 100\% = \left(\frac{\text{Denominator} - \text{Numerator}}{\text{Denominator}}\right) \times 100\%$$
  * *Example:* $M = \frac{7}{8} \implies \frac{8 - 7}{8} = -\frac{1}{8} = \mathbf{-12.5\%}$
