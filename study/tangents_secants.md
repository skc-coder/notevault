# Tangents, Secants & Power of a Point

## 1. Tangent Properties & Power of a Point

### Tangent-Radius Perpendicularity
A tangent at any point of a circle is perpendicular to the radius through the point of contact.
$$OP \perp PT$$

### Tangents from an External Point
1. The lengths of two tangents drawn from an external point $P$ to a circle are equal:
   $$PA = PB$$
2. The line segment joining the external point to the centre bisects the angle between the tangents and the angle between the radii:
   $$\angle APO = \angle BPO, \quad \angle AOP = \angle BOP$$

### Intersecting Chords & Secant Theorems (Power of a Point)
1. **Internal Chord Intersection**:
   If two chords $AB$ and $CD$ intersect inside a circle at point $P$:
   $$PA \cdot PB = PC \cdot PD$$
2. **External Secant Intersection**:
   If two secants $P-A-B$ and $P-C-D$ intersect outside a circle at point $P$:
   $$PA \cdot PB = PC \cdot PD$$
3. **Secant-Tangent Theorem**:
   If a tangent $PT$ and a secant $P-A-B$ are drawn from an external point $P$:
   $$PT^2 = PA \cdot PB$$

### Alternate Segment Theorem
The angle between a tangent and a chord drawn through the point of contact is equal to the angle subtended by the chord in the alternate segment.
$$\angle BAT = \angle BCA$$

### Common Tangents to Two Circles
For two circles with radii $r_1, r_2$ and distance between centres $d$:
- **Direct Common Tangent (DCT)**:
  $$L_{\text{DCT}} = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 - r_2)^2}$$
- **Transverse Common Tangent (TCT)**:
  $$L_{\text{TCT}} = \sqrt{d^2 - (r_1 + r_2)^2}$$
