# Congruence of Triangles

## 1. Concept of Congruence

Two triangles $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta PQR$ are **congruent** ($\Delta ABC \cong \Delta PQR$) if they are identical in shape and size. All corresponding angles and corresponding sides are equal:
- $\angle A = \angle P, \quad \angle B = \angle Q, \quad \angle C = \angle R$
- $AB = PQ, \quad BC = QR, \quad CA = RP$

Corresponding parts of congruent triangles are equal (**CPCTC**).

---

## 2. Criteria for Congruence

### 1. SAS (Side-Angle-Side) Criteria
Two triangles are congruent if two sides and the **included angle** of one triangle are equal to two sides and the included angle of another triangle.
$$\text{If } AB = PQ, \quad \angle B = \angle Q, \quad BC = QR \implies \Delta ABC \cong \Delta PQR$$

### 2. ASA / AAS (Angle-Side-Angle / Angle-Angle-Side) Criteria
- **ASA**: Two angles and the **included side** are equal.
- **AAS**: Two angles and any **non-included corresponding side** are equal.

### 3. SSS (Side-Side-Side) Criteria
Two triangles are congruent if all three corresponding sides are equal ($AB = PQ, BC = QR, CA = RP$).

### 4. RHS (Right angle-Hypotenuse-Side) Criteria
Two right-angled triangles are congruent if the hypotenuse and one side of one triangle are equal to the hypotenuse and corresponding side of the other.

---

## 3. Important Theorems & Properties

1. **Isosceles Triangle Base Angle Theorem**:
   - In $\Delta ABC$, if $AB = AC$, then $\angle B = \angle C$.
   - Conversely, if $\angle B = \angle C$, then $AB = AC$.
2. **Altitude & Bisector in Isosceles Triangle**:
   - The median, angle bisector from vertical vertex, and altitude to the base of an isosceles triangle coincide.
3. **Mid-point Theorem**:
   - The line segment joining the midpoints of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and half of its length:
     $$DE \parallel BC \quad \text{and} \quad DE = \frac{1}{2} BC$$
