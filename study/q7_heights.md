# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q7

## Question Statement

An aeroplane flying horizontally $1\text{ km}$ above the ground is observed at an elevation of $60^\circ$. After $10\text{ s}$, its elevation is observed to be $30^\circ$, the uniform speed of the aeroplane in $\text{km/h}$ is

- (a) $\frac{240}{\sqrt{3}}$
- (b) $240\sqrt{3}$
- (c) $240$
- (d) $260\sqrt{3}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Position Analysis
Let height of flight $h = 1\text{ km}$.
- Initial horizontal distance from observer to plane $d_1$:
  $$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{h}{d_1} \implies \sqrt{3} = \frac{1}{d_1} \implies d_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\text{ km}$$
- Final horizontal distance after $10\text{ seconds}$ $d_2$:
  $$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{h}{d_2} \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{1}{d_2} \implies d_2 = \sqrt{3}\text{ km}$$

### 2. Distance Traveled in 10 Seconds
$$D = d_2 - d_1 = \sqrt{3} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{3 - 1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\text{ km}$$

### 3. Speed Calculation
- Time interval $t = 10\text{ seconds} = \frac{10}{3600}\text{ h} = \frac{1}{360}\text{ h}$.
- Speed $v = \frac{D}{t}$:
  $$v = \frac{2/\sqrt{3}}{1/360} = \frac{720}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{720\sqrt{3}}{3} = 240\sqrt{3}\text{ km/h}$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(b) $240\sqrt{3}$**
