# CDS Practice Question 28.6

## Question
The mean deviation about the mean for the data: 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 14 is:
- (a) 4.00
- (b) 3.25
- (c) 2.75
- (d) 2.40

---

## Detailed Solution & Derivation

### 1. Compute Arithmetic Mean ($\bar{x}$)
$$n = 8$$
$$\bar{x} = \frac{3 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 8 + 10 + 11 + 14}{8} = \frac{64}{8} = 8$$

### 2. Compute Absolute Deviations $|x_i - \bar{x}|$
- $|3 - 8| = 5$
- $|5 - 8| = 3$
- $|6 - 8| = 2$
- $|7 - 8| = 1$
- $|8 - 8| = 0$
- $|10 - 8| = 2$
- $|11 - 8| = 3$
- $|14 - 8| = 6$

$$\sum |x_i - \bar{x}| = 5 + 3 + 2 + 1 + 0 + 2 + 3 + 6 = 22$$

### 3. Mean Deviation Formula
$$MD(\bar{x}) = \frac{\sum |x_i - \bar{x}|}{n} = \frac{22}{8} = 2.75$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(c) 2.75**
