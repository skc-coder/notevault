# CDS Pathfinder Q11.1

## Problem Statement

By selling an article for ₹247.50, Sonu gets a profit of $12.5\%$. What is the cost price of the article?

A. ₹220  
B. ₹205  
C. ₹210  
D. ₹200  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. Fractional Conversion
- Given profit percentage $P\% = 12.5\% = \frac{1}{8}$.
- This implies for every 8 units of Cost Price ($\text{CP}$), the Profit ($P$) is 1 unit.
- Thus, the Selling Price ($\text{SP}$) in ratio terms is:
  $$\text{SP} = \text{CP} + P = 8 + 1 = 9 \text{ units}$$

### 2. Equation & Value Substitution
- We are given $\text{SP} = \text{Rs. }247.50$:
  $$9 \text{ units} = 247.50$$
  $$1 \text{ unit} = \frac{247.50}{9} = 27.50$$

### 3. Cost Price Calculation
- Cost Price corresponds to 8 units:
  $$\text{CP} = 8 \times 27.50 = \text{Rs. }220$$

---

## Correct Answer
**A. ₹220**
