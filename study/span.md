 $\text{Span}\{\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_p\}$ is the collection of all vectors that can be written in the form
$$
c_1\mathbf{v}_1 + c_2\mathbf{v}_2 + \cdots + c_p\mathbf{v}_p
$$
with $c_1, \dots, c_p$ scalars.

 $\text{Span}\{\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_p\}$  is also called subset of $\mathbb{R}^n$ spanned (or generated) by** $\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_p$. 

---
Finding span is just [solving linear equations](solving%20linear%20equations.md) or solving [Ax=b](Ax=b.md)
