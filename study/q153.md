# CDS Percentage Q153

## Question
A man spends $75\%$ of his income. If his income increases by $20\%$ and he increases his expenditure by $10\%$, by what percentage does his savings increase?
- (a) $25\%$
- (b) $50\%$
- (c) $75\%$
- (d) $10\%$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Assign initial baseline values
Let initial Income $I_0 = 100$.
- Initial Expenditure $E_0 = 75\%$ of $100 = 75$.
- Initial Savings $S_0 = I_0 - E_0 = 100 - 75 = 25$.

### Step 2: Calculate new adjusted values
- New Income $I_1 = 100 + 20\% \text{ of } 100 = 120$.
- New Expenditure $E_1 = 75 + 10\% \text{ of } 75 = 75 + 7.5 = 82.5$.
- New Savings $S_1 = I_1 - E_1 = 120 - 82.5 = 37.5$.

### Step 3: Calculate percentage increase in savings
Absolute increase in savings:
$$\Delta S = S_1 - S_0 = 37.5 - 25 = 12.5$$

Percentage increase in savings:
$$\text{Percentage Increase} = \frac{\Delta S}{S_0} \times 100\% = \frac{12.5}{25} \times 100\% = \frac{1}{2} \times 100\% = 50\%$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(b) $50\%$**
