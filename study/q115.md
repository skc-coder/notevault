# Question 115: Product of Terms of a GP with Known Middle Term

The 5th term of a Geometric Progression is 3. What is the product of its first 9 terms?

(a) $3^5$  
(b) $3^9$  
(c) $3^{10}$  
(d) Cannot be determined  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Shortcut / Key Invariant:**  
> In a GP with $2n+1$ terms, the product of all terms is equal to the **middle term raised to the power of the total number of terms**:
> $$\text{Product} = (T_{\text{middle}})^{\text{total terms}}$$
> 
> **Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
> - Total terms = 9 (odd number).
> - Middle term position = $\frac{9+1}{2} = 5\text{th term}$.
> - Given $T_5 = 3$.
> - Product $P = (T_1 \cdot T_9)(T_2 \cdot T_8)(T_3 \cdot T_7)(T_4 \cdot T_6) \cdot T_5$
> - Since $T_1 \cdot T_9 = T_5^2$, $T_2 \cdot T_8 = T_5^2$, etc.:
>   $$P = (T_5^2)^4 \cdot T_5 = T_5^9 = 3^9$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) $3^9$**
