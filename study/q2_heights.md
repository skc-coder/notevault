# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q2

## Question Statement

From a light house the angles of depression of two ships on opposite sides of the light house are observed to be $30^\circ$ and $45^\circ$. If the height of light house is $h$, then what is the distance between the ships?

- (a) $(\sqrt{3} + 1)h$
- (b) $(\sqrt{3} - 1)h$
- (c) $\sqrt{3}h$
- (d) $\left(1 + \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}\right)h$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Geometric Setup
Let the lighthouse be $AB = h$. Let the two ships be positioned at $C$ and $D$ on opposite sides of the base $B$ of the lighthouse along a single straight line.

### 2. Individual Right Triangles
- On side 1 (Ship $C$, angle of elevation = $45^\circ$):
  $$\tan 45^\circ = \frac{AB}{BC} \implies 1 = \frac{h}{BC} \implies BC = h$$
- On side 2 (Ship $D$, angle of elevation = $30^\circ$):
  $$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{AB}{BD} \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{h}{BD} \implies BD = h\sqrt{3}$$

### 3. Total Distance Between Ships
Since ships lie on opposite sides of the vertical tower:
$$\text{Distance } CD = BC + BD = h + h\sqrt{3} = h(\sqrt{3} + 1)$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(a) $(\sqrt{3} + 1)h$**
