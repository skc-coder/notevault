# CDS Pathfinder Ch 4 Ex 8

## Question
The vulgar fraction of $0.12\overline{36}$ is:
- (a) $\frac{120}{823}$
- (b) $\frac{102}{825}$
- (c) $\frac{93}{825}$
- (d) None of these

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

1. **Identify decimal structure**:
   - $0.12\overline{36}$ is a **mixed recurring decimal**.
   - Non-repeating digits after decimal ($m = 2$): $12$
   - Repeating digits ($n = 2$): $36$

2. **Apply Theorem 2 (Mixed Recurring Decimal Conversion)**:
   $$\text{Fraction} = \frac{(\text{Full Number}) - (\text{Non-repeating Part})}{99\dots900\dots0}$$
   - Numerator:
     $$1236 - 12 = 1224$$
   - Denominator ($2$ nines followed by $2$ zeroes):
     $$9900$$

3. **Simplify fraction**:
   $$\frac{1224}{9900}$$
   - Divide numerator and denominator by $12$:
     $$\frac{1224 \div 12}{9900 \div 12} = \frac{102}{825}$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(b) $\frac{102}{825}$**
