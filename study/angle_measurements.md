# Angle Measurements & Clocks

## Theory & Intuition

Angle measurement is the foundation of trigonometry. An angle is formed by rotating a ray about its endpoint (vertex) from an initial position to a terminal position.

### Systems of Measurement
1. **Sexagesimal System (Degree Measure)**:
   - $1 \text{ Right Angle} = 90^\circ$
   - $1^\circ = 60' \text{ (minutes)}$
   - $1' = 60'' \text{ (seconds)}$

2. **Circular System (Radian Measure)**:
   - A radian is the angle subtended at the center of a circle by an arc whose length is equal to the radius of the circle.
   - Written as $1^c$.
   - **Conversion Formula**:
     $$\pi \text{ radians} = 180^\circ$$
     $$1^c = \left(\frac{180}{\pi}\right)^\circ \approx 57^\circ 17' 45''$$
     $$1^\circ = \frac{\pi}{180} \text{ radians} \approx 0.01746 \text{ rad}$$

### Circular Arc & Sector Length
- For a circle of radius $r$ and arc length $l$ subtending an angle $\theta$ (in **radians**) at the center:
  $$l = r \theta \implies \theta = \frac{l}{r}$$
- **Area of Sector**:
  $$A = \frac{1}{2} r^2 \theta = \frac{1}{2} l r$$

### Clock Angle Principles
- **Hour Hand Rotation**:
  - Rotates $360^\circ$ in 12 hours ($720 \text{ minutes}$).
  - Speed of Hour Hand = $\frac{360^\circ}{720 \text{ min}} = 0.5^\circ/\text{min}$ ($30^\circ/\text{hour}$).
- **Minute Hand Rotation**:
  - Rotates $360^\circ$ in 60 minutes.
  - Speed of Minute Hand = $\frac{360^\circ}{60 \text{ min}} = 6^\circ/\text{min}$.
- **Relative Speed**:
  $$\text{Relative Speed} = 6^\circ - 0.5^\circ = 5.5^\circ/\text{min} = \left(\frac{11}{2}\right)^\circ/\text{min}$$
- **Angle between Clock Hands at Hour $H$ and Minute $M$**:
  $$\theta = |30H - 5.5M|^\circ$$
