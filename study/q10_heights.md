# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q10

## Question Statement

From a window ($h\text{ m}$ high above the ground) of a house in a street, the angle of elevation and depression of the top and the foot of another house on the opposite side of the street are $\theta$ and $\phi$, respectively. Then, the height of the opposite house is

- (a) $h \tan \theta \cot \phi$
- (b) $h [1 + \tan \theta \cot \phi]$
- (c) $h [1 + \cot \theta \tan \phi]$
- (d) $h \cot \theta \tan \phi$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Width of Street
Let street width be $w$. From window of height $h$:
- Angle of depression to foot of opposite house $= \phi$:
  $$\tan \phi = \frac{h}{w} \implies w = \frac{h}{\tan \phi} = h \cot \phi$$

### 2. Height Above Window Level
- Angle of elevation to top of opposite house $= \theta$:
  Let height of opposite house above window line be $h'$.
  $$\tan \theta = \frac{h'}{w} \implies h' = w \tan \theta = (h \cot \phi) \tan \theta = h \tan \theta \cot \phi$$

### 3. Total Height of Opposite House
$$H = h + h' = h + h \tan \theta \cot \phi = h [1 + \tan \theta \cot \phi]$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(b) $h [1 + \tan \theta \cot \phi]$**
