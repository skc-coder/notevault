# CDS Pathfinder Q11.5

## Problem Statement

Harish sold two scooters, each for ₹24,000. If he makes $20\%$ profit on the first and $15\%$ loss on the second, what is his gain or loss percent in the whole transaction?

A. Loss of $20\frac{20}{41}\%$  
B. Loss of $\frac{20}{41}\%$  
C. Gain of $\frac{20}{41}\%$  
D. Loss of $\frac{32}{73}\%$  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. General Formula for Asymmetric Equal Selling Price
- For profit $x = +20\%$ and loss $y = -15\%$ with equal selling prices:
  $$\text{Net } \% = \frac{100(x + y) + 2xy}{(100 + x) + (100 + y)}$$

### 2. Parameter Substitution
- Numerator:
  $$100(20 - 15) + 2(20)(-15) = 100(5) - 600 = 500 - 600 = -100$$

- Denominator:
  $$(100 + 20) + (100 - 15) = 120 + 85 = 205$$

### 3. Net Ratio Evaluation
- Substituting:
  $$\text{Net } \% = \frac{-100}{205} = -\frac{20}{41}\%$$
- The negative sign indicates a net **loss of $\frac{20}{41}\%$**.

---

## Correct Answer
**B. Loss of 20/41%**
