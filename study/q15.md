# Question 15: Wilson's Theorem Companion (12! + 2 mod 13)

What is the remainder when $12! + 2$ is divided by 13?

(a) 0  
(b) 1  
(c) 2  
(d) 12  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> 1. **Apply Wilson's Theorem for prime $p = 13$**:
>    $$(p-1)! \equiv -1 \pmod p \implies 12! \equiv -1 \pmod{13}$$
> 2. **Substitute into expression**:
>    $$12! + 2 \equiv -1 + 2 = \mathbf{1 \pmod{13}}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 1**
