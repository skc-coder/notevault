# Vectors in $\mathbb{R}^n$

$\mathbb{R}^n$ (read "r-n") is the set of all ordered $n$-tuples of real numbers, typically written as $n \times 1$ column matrices:

$$
\mathbf{u} = \begin{bmatrix}
u_1 \\
u_2 \\
\vdots \\
u_n
\end{bmatrix}
$$

- The **zero vector** $\mathbf{0}$ has all entries equal to $0$. Its dimension is clear from context.
