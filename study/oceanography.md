# Oceanography, Ocean Currents and Coral Reefs

Comprehensive, exhaustive master concept notes on Ocean Floor Relief, Marine Salinity, Tides, World Ocean Currents Circulation, and Coral Reef Ecology synthesized from Pages 229-251 of the Physical Geography PDF.

---

## Theory, Intuition & Key Concepts

### 1. Ocean Floor Relief & Topography

The ocean basin is divided into **4 Major Relief Divisions**:

1. **Continental Shelf**:
   - Gently sloping seaward extension of continental landmass ($\text{gradient } 1^\circ \text{ or less}$).
   - Average depth $\approx 120-150 \text{ m}$. Wide shelves found off Siberia (Arctic Ocean - $1,500 \text{ km}$ wide).
   - **Economic Importance**: Rich fishing grounds (Grand Banks, Dogger Bank) and petroleum reserves (Bombay High).

2. **Continental Slope**:
   - Connects continental shelf to ocean basin ($\text{gradient } 2^\circ - 5^\circ$).
   - Depth ranges from $200 \text{ m}$ to $3,000 \text{ m}$. Deep submarine canyons are carved here.

3. **Continental Rise**:
   - Thick apron of sediments at the base of slope ($\text{gradient } 0.5^\circ - 1^\circ$).

4. **Abyssal Plain (Deep Sea Plain)**:
   - Flattest and smoothest regions on Earth covering $>40\%$ of ocean floor. Depth $3,000 - 6,000 \text{ m}$.

5. **Oceanic Deeps / Trenches**:
   - V-shaped steep-sided narrow depressions at subduction zones.
   - **Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep)**: Deepest point in Pacific Ocean ($11,034 \text{ m}$).
   - **Puerto Rico Trench**: Deepest point in Atlantic Ocean ($8,376 \text{ m}$).
   - **Java / Sunda Trench**: Deepest point in Indian Ocean ($7,450 \text{ m}$).

### 2. Ocean Salinity & Temperature

#### A. Ocean Salinity
- Average ocean salinity is **$35 \text{ ppt}$** ($35\%_0$ or $35 \text{ grams of salt per } 1,000 \text{ g}$ of water).
- **Dominant Salts in Sea Water**:
  $$\text{Sodium Chloride } (NaCl \approx 77.7\%) > \text{Magnesium Chloride } (MgCl_2 \approx 10.9\%) > \text{Magnesium Sulfate } (MgSO_4 \approx 4.7\%)$$
- **Highest Salinity Water Bodies**:
  1. **Lake Van (Turkey)**: $330 \text{ ppt}$
  2. **Dead Sea (Israel/Jordan)**: $238 \text{ ppt}$
  3. **Great Salt Lake (Utah, USA)**: $220 \text{ ppt}$
  4. **Red Sea**: $41 \text{ ppt}$ (High evaporation, low inflow).

### 3. World Ocean Currents Circulation

#### A. Atlantic Ocean Currents
- **Warm Currents**:
  - **Gulf Stream**: Warm current flowing NE along East Coast of USA towards Europe (keeps European ports ice-free!).
  - **North Atlantic Drift**, **Florida Current**, **Brazil Current**, **Equatorial Current**.
- **Cold Currents**:
  - **Labrador Current**: Cold current flowing south from Arctic; meets Gulf Stream at **Newfoundland** (creates heavy fog & rich fishing grounds at Grand Banks).
  - **Canary Current**: Cold current off NW Africa (creates Sahara Desert).
  - **Benguela Current**: Cold current off SW Africa (creates Namib & Kalahari Deserts).
  - **Falkland Current**: Cold current off S. America.

#### B. Pacific Ocean Currents
- **Warm Currents**:
  - **Kuroshio (Japan) Current**: Warm current flowing north along Japan.
  - **East Australian Current**, **North & South Equatorial Currents**.
- **Cold Currents**:
  - **Oyashio (Kamchatka) Current**: Cold current meeting Kuroshio near Japan (rich fishing ground).
  - **Humboldt / Peru Current**: Cold current flowing north along Peru/Chile (causes **Atacama Desert** and triggers **El Niño** when replaced by warm water).
  - **California Current**: Cold current off USA West Coast.

#### C. Indian Ocean Currents
- Reverses direction seasonally due to the **Monsoon Winds**!
- **Warm**: Mozambique Current, Agulhas Current.
- **Cold**: West Australian Current (causes Great Australian Desert).

### 4. Coral Reefs & Atolls

#### A. Optimal Conditions for Coral Growth
1. **Warm Sea Temperature**: $20^\circ\text{C} - 21^\circ\text{C}$ (Tropical waters between $30^\circ\text{N} - 30^\circ\text{S}$).
2. **Shallow Depth**: $<50 \text{ meters}$ (requires sunlight for zooxanthellae algae photosynthesis).
3. **Salinity**: Normal marine salinity ($27 - 35 \text{ ppt}$).
4. **Clear, Silt-Free Water**: Muddy/fresh water suffocates polyps (no corals near river mouths!).

#### B. Three Types of Coral Reefs
1. **Fringing Reef**: Attached directly to the coastline (e.g., Andaman Islands).
2. **Barrier Reef**: Separated from coast by a deep wide lagoon (e.g., **Great Barrier Reef, Australia** — $2,000 \text{ km}$ long).
3. **Atoll**: Ring-shaped coral reef enclosing a central lagoon (e.g., **Lakshadweep Islands**, Maldives).
