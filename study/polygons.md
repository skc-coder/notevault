# Polygons & Regular Polygons

## 1. General Polygon Properties ($n$ sides)
- **Sum of Interior Angles**:
  $$S_{\text{int}} = (n - 2) \times 180^\circ$$
- **Sum of Exterior Angles**:
  $$S_{\text{ext}} = 360^\circ$$
- **Number of Diagonals**:
  $$D = \frac{n(n - 3)}{2}$$

## 2. Regular Polygon Properties ($n$ equal sides $a$)
- **Each Exterior Angle**:
  $$\theta_{\text{ext}} = \frac{360^\circ}{n}$$
- **Each Interior Angle**:
  $$\theta_{\text{int}} = 180^\circ - \theta_{\text{ext}} = \frac{(n - 2) \times 180^\circ}{n}$$
- **Interior & Exterior Complementarity**:
  $$\theta_{\text{int}} + \theta_{\text{ext}} = 180^\circ$$

## 3. Specific Regular Polygon Areas
- **Equilateral Triangle ($n=3$)**:
  $$\text{Area} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} a^2$$
- **Square ($n=4$)**:
  $$\text{Area} = a^2$$
- **Regular Pentagon ($n=5$)**:
  $$\text{Area} = \frac{5}{4} a^2 \cot\left(\frac{\pi}{5}\right) = \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{4} a^2 \text{ (CDS approx)}$$
- **Regular Hexagon ($n=6$)**:
  - Composed of 6 congruent equilateral triangles of side $a$.
  $$\text{Area} = 6 \times \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} a^2\right) = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} a^2$$
  - Main diagonal length $= 2a$.
- **Regular Octagon ($n=8$)**:
  $$\text{Area} = 2(1 + \sqrt{2}) a^2$$
