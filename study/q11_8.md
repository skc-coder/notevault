# CDS Pathfinder Q11.8

## Problem Statement

What price should a seller mark on a watch purchased at ₹380 so that after giving a $5\%$ discount, there is a $25\%$ profit?

A. ₹400  
B. ₹450  
C. ₹500  
D. ₹600  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. Invariant Relation between MP and CP
- Standard ratio relation:
  $$\frac{\text{MP}}{\text{CP}} = \frac{100 + P\%}{100 - D\%}$$

### 2. Parameter Substitution
- $\text{CP} = \text{Rs. }380$
- Target Profit $P\% = 25\%$
- Discount $D\% = 5\%$

- Substituting into formula:
  $$\frac{\text{MP}}{380} = \frac{100 + 25}{100 - 5} = \frac{125}{95}$$

### 3. Solving for Marked Price
  $$\text{MP} = 380 \times \frac{125}{95}$$
- Simplifying $\frac{380}{95} = 4$:
  $$\text{MP} = 4 \times 125 = \text{Rs. }500$$

---

## Correct Answer
**C. ₹500**
