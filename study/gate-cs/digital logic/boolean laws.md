## Absorption Law
$$
a + ab = a \quad \text{and} \quad a(a + \text{anything}) = a
$$

and

$$
{A + \overline{A}B} = A + B
$$

## Consensus Property
$$
ab + \overline{a}c + bc = ab + \overline{a}c
$$


## reverse distributive law 
$$
(P + Q)(P + R) = P + QR
$$

This is a standard Boolean identity derived from distributive property.
