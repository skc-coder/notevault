# Variation 31: Solution Evaporation and Solute Concentration Maintenance

## Problem Statement
In a liquid solution of volume $V$ containing solute concentration $c_1\%$, water is evaporated until solute concentration rises to $c_2\%$. Prove that volume of evaporated water is $V \left(1 - \frac{c_1}{c_2}\right)$.

---

## Proof & Theoretical Intuition

### 1. Mass Conservation of Solute
The total mass of solute before evaporation equals total mass of solute after evaporation:
$$V \cdot \frac{c_1}{100} = V' \cdot \frac{c_2}{100} \implies V' = V \left(\frac{c_1}{c_2}\right)$$
The volume of evaporated water $\Delta V$ is:
$$\Delta V = V - V' = V - V \left(\frac{c_1}{c_2}\right) = V \left(1 - \frac{c_1}{c_2}\right)$$
