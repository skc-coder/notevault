# CDS Pathfinder Ch 5 Q2

## Question

Find the least four-digit natural number that is a perfect square.

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

- Step 1: Identify the smallest four-digit natural number:
  The smallest four-digit number is $1000$.

- Step 2: Test adjacent integer squares around $\sqrt{1000}$:
  - $30^2 = 900$ (3 digits)
  - $31^2 = 961$ (3 digits)
  - $32^2 = 1024$ (4 digits)

- Step 3: Verify that $32^2 = 1024$ is the smallest four-digit perfect square:
  Since $31^2 = 961 < 1000 < 1024 = 32^2$, the immediate smallest integer whose square yields 4 digits is $32$.

$$\text{Smallest 4-digit perfect square} = 32^2 = 1024$$

---

## Key Takeaway

For bounding problems involving small/large $d$-digit perfect squares, evaluate integer roots around $\sqrt{10^{d-1}}$ using long division or known square tables.
