# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q8

## Question Statement

A person of height $2\text{ m}$ wants to get a fruit which is on a pole of height $\frac{10}{3}\text{ m}$. If he stands at a distance of $\frac{4}{3}\text{ m}$ from the foot of the pole, then the angle at which he should throw the stone, so that it hits the fruit is

- (a) $60^\circ$
- (b) $45^\circ$
- (c) $90^\circ$
- (d) $30^\circ$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Relative Height
- Height of fruit on pole $= \frac{10}{3}\text{ m}$.
- Height of person's eyes/hand level $= 2\text{ m}$.
- Relative vertical elevation needed $h_{rel}$:
  $$h_{rel} = \frac{10}{3} - 2 = \frac{10 - 6}{3} = \frac{4}{3}\text{ m}$$

### 2. Distance & Angle
- Horizontal distance to pole $d = \frac{4}{3}\text{ m}$.
- Angle of trajectory elevation $\theta$:
  $$\tan \theta = \frac{h_{rel}}{d} = \frac{4/3}{4/3} = 1$$
  $$\theta = 45^\circ$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(b) $45^\circ$**
