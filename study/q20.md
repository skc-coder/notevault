# Question 20: Greatest Common Divisor with Same Remainder 17 (CDS 2012 II)

What is the greatest number that divides 13850 and 17030 and leaves a remainder 17?

(a) 477  
(b) 159  
(c) 107  
(d) 87  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Step 1: Subtract remainder 17 from both numbers
> - $13850 - 17 = \mathbf{13833}$
> - $17030 - 17 = \mathbf{17013}$
> 
> The required divisor $D = \operatorname{HCF}(13833, 17013)$.
> 
> ### Step 2: Difference Trick
> $$\text{Difference} = 17013 - 13833 = \mathbf{3180}$$
> 
> Factor 3180 to find candidate divisors:
> $$3180 = 20 \times 159$$
> 
> Since 13833 and 17013 are odd, 20 is ruled out.  
> Testing option 159:
> - $13833 = 159 \times 87$
> - $17013 = 159 \times 107$
> 
> $\implies D = \mathbf{159}$.
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 159**
