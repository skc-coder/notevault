# CDS Percentage Q154

## Question
$1\text{ L}$ of water is evaporated from $6\text{ L}$ of a solution containing $4\%$ sugar. What is the percentage of sugar in the remaining solution?
- (a) $4\%$
- (b) $5\%$
- (c) $4\frac{4}{5}\%$
- (d) $\frac{4}{5}\%$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Calculate mass of pure sugar in original solution
Total initial solution volume $V_1 = 6\text{ L}$.
Sugar concentration $= 4\%$.
Amount of pure sugar:
$$\text{Sugar} = 6 \times \frac{4}{100} = 0.24\text{ L}$$

### Step 2: Determine new solution volume after evaporation
When $1\text{ L}$ of water evaporates, sugar does NOT evaporate.
New total solution volume $V_2$:
$$V_2 = 6\text{ L} - 1\text{ L} = 5\text{ L}$$

### Step 3: Calculate new sugar percentage concentration
$$\text{New Concentration \%} = \frac{\text{Pure Sugar}}{V_2} \times 100\%$$
$$\text{New Concentration \%} = \frac{0.24}{5} \times 100\% = 0.24 \times 20 = 4.8\%$$

Convert $4.8\%$ to mixed fraction:
$$4.8 = 4 + 0.8 = 4 + \frac{8}{10} = 4\frac{4}{5}\%$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(c) $4\frac{4}{5}\%$**
