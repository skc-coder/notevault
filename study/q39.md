# CDS Practice Question 39

## Question Statement
The efficiency of $P$ is twice that of $Q$, whereas the efficiency of $P$ and $Q$ together is three times that of $R$. If $P, Q,$ and $R$ work together on a job, in what ratio should they share their earnings?

- (a) $2 : 1 : 1$
- (b) $4 : 2 : 1$
- (c) $4 : 3 : 2$
- (d) $4 : 2 : 3$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

1. **Relate Efficiencies of $P$ and $Q$**:
   Given efficiency $E_P = 2 E_Q$.
   Let $E_Q = 1\text{ unit/day}$.
   Then $E_P = 2\text{ units/day}$.

2. **Relate Combined Efficiency of $(P + Q)$ to $R$**:
   Given $E_P + E_Q = 3 E_R$.
   Substitute $E_P = 2$ and $E_Q = 1$:
   $$2 + 1 = 3 E_R \implies 3 = 3 E_R \implies E_R = 1\text{ unit/day}$$

3. **Determine Wage Sharing Ratio**:
   Since all three work together for the same amount of time, wages are distributed strictly in proportion to their individual daily work rates (efficiencies):
   $$\text{Wage Ratio } P : Q : R = E_P : E_Q : E_R = 2 : 1 : 1$$

- **Correct Option**: **(a) 2 : 1 : 1**
