# Heat and Thermodynamics

Thermal expansion, heat transfer mechanisms, thermometry, thermodynamic laws, and relative humidity.

---

## 1. Thermometry & Temperature Scales

Temperature is the quantitative measure of average kinetic energy of molecules.

- **Scale Interconversion Formula**:
  $$\frac{C}{5} = \frac{F - 32}{9} = \frac{R}{4} = \frac{K - 273.15}{5}$$
- **Key Benchmarks**:
  - Absolute Zero: $0\text{ K} = -273.15^\circ\text{C}$
  - Water Freezing: $0^\circ\text{C} = 32^\circ\text{F} = 273.15\text{ K}$
  - Normal Body Temp: $37^\circ\text{C} = 98.6^\circ\text{F} = 310.15\text{ K}$
  - Intersection Point: $-40^\circ\text{C} = -40^\circ\text{F}$

---

## 2. Specific & Latent Heat

- **Sensible Heat Capacity ($Q$)**:
  $$Q = m c \Delta T \quad (c_{\text{water}} = 1\text{ cal/g}^\circ\text{C} = 4186\text{ J/kg}\cdot\text{K})$$
- **Latent Heat ($L$)**: Heat absorbed/released during phase change at constant temperature:
  $$Q = m L$$
  - Latent Heat of Fusion of Ice: $L_f = 80\text{ cal/g} \approx 3.36 \times 10^5\text{ J/kg}$
  - Latent Heat of Vaporization of Water: $L_v = 540\text{ cal/g} \approx 2.26 \times 10^6\text{ J/kg}$

---

## 3. Laws of Thermodynamics

1. **Zeroth Law**: Defines thermal equilibrium and temperature concept.
2. **First Law (Energy Conservation)**:
   $$\Delta Q = \Delta U + W$$
3. **Second Law (Entropy & Direction)**: Heat cannot spontaneously flow from a colder to a hotter body without work input (Clausius statement). Engine efficiency cannot be 100% (Kelvin-Planck statement).

---

## 4. Hygrometry & Relative Humidity

- **Relative Humidity (RH)**: Ratio of actual water vapor pressure to saturated vapor pressure at given temperature:
  $$\text{RH} = \frac{\text{Actual Water Vapor Amount}}{\text{Saturated Water Vapor Amount at same } T} \times 100\%$$
- **Dew Point**: Temperature at which air becomes saturated with existing moisture.
