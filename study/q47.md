# CDS Practice Question 47

## Question
A sum was put at simple interest at a certain rate for 2 years. Had it been put at 3% higher rate, it would have fetched ₹72 more. The sum is:
- (a) ₹1,200
- (b) ₹1,600
- (c) ₹1,900
- (d) ₹1,400

---

## Detailed Solution & Proof

- Let principal sum be $P$ and original rate be $R\%$.
- Time period $T = 2\text{ years}$.

- Difference in simple interest produced by rate increment $\Delta R = 3\%$:
  $$\Delta SI = \frac{P \cdot \Delta R \cdot T}{100}$$

- Substitute given values $\Delta SI = 72$, $\Delta R = 3\%$, $T = 2$:
  $$72 = \frac{P \cdot 3 \cdot 2}{100}$$
  $$72 = \frac{6P}{100}$$

- Solve for $P$:
  $$P = \frac{72 \times 100}{6} = \text{Rs. }1,200$$

**Correct Option**: **(a) ₹1,200**
