# CDS PYQ 2013 II Q33: Variable Power Trap in Polynomial LCM

## Question Text

The LCM of $P(x) = x^3 - x^2 - 2x$ and $Q(x) = x^3 + x^2$ is:

**Options:**
- (a) $x^3 - x^2 - 2x$
- (b) $x^2 + x$
- (c) $x^4 - x^3 - 2x^2$
- (d) $x - 2$

---

## Detailed Mathematical Solution

### Step 1: Factorize Both Polynomials

Factorize $P(x)$:
$$P(x) = x^3 - x^2 - 2x$$
$$P(x) = x(x^2 - x - 2)$$
Split middle term $-x = -2x + x$:
$$P(x) = x(x - 2)(x + 1)$$

Factorize $Q(x)$:
$$Q(x) = x^3 + x^2$$
Factor out $x^2$:
$$Q(x) = x^2(x + 1)$$

---

### Step 2: Compute LCM

Take the **highest exponent of every prime algebraic factor**:
- Highest power of $x$: $x^2$
- Highest power of $(x + 1)$: $(x + 1)^1$
- Highest power of $(x - 2)$: $(x - 2)^1$

$$\operatorname{LCM}(P(x), Q(x)) = x^2(x + 1)(x - 2)$$

Expand the factors:
$$\operatorname{LCM}(P(x), Q(x)) = x^2(x^2 - x - 2)$$
$$\operatorname{LCM}(P(x), Q(x)) = x^4 - x^3 - 2x^2$$

**Correct Answer**: **(c) $x^4 - x^3 - 2x^2$**

---

## Critical Trap Analysis: Why Option (a) Fails

> [!WARNING]
> **Common Exam Trap (Option (a) vs Option (c))**:
> - Option (a) is $x^3 - x^2 - 2x = x(x - 2)(x + 1)$.
> - Even though Option (a) is divisible by $P(x)$, **it is NOT divisible by $Q(x) = x^2(x + 1)$** because Option (a) only has $x^1$ instead of the required degree $x^2$.
> - For a polynomial $L(x)$ to be a valid LCM, every factor of $Q(x)$ must divide $L(x)$ without remainder. Since $x^2 \nmid x^1$, Option (a) cannot be the LCM!

---

## Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy
Always inspect the exponent of monomial terms ($x^k$)! The LCM must contain $x^{\max(\text{powers})}$. Here $Q(x)$ has $x^2$, so the LCM must contain at least $x^2$.
