# CDS Practice Question 28.1

## Question
In a class of 50 students, 10 have failed and their average marks are 28. The total marks obtained by the entire class are 2800. The average marks of those who have passed are:
- (a) 43
- (b) 53
- (c) 63
- (d) 70

---

## Detailed Solution & Derivation

### 1. Step-by-Step Evaluation

- **Total Class Strength**:
  $$N = 50$$

- **Failed Students**:
  $$n_1 = 10, \quad \bar{x}_1 = 28$$

- **Total Marks of Failed Students**:
  $$\text{Sum}_1 = n_1 \cdot \bar{x}_1 = 10 \times 28 = 280$$

- **Passed Students Count**:
  $$n_2 = N - n_1 = 50 - 10 = 40$$

- **Total Marks of Entire Class**:
  $$\text{Sum}_{\text{total}} = 2800$$

- **Total Marks of Passed Students**:
  $$\text{Sum}_2 = \text{Sum}_{\text{total}} - \text{Sum}_1 = 2800 - 280 = 2520$$

- **Average Marks of Passed Students ($\bar{x}_2$)**:
  $$\bar{x}_2 = \frac{\text{Sum}_2}{n_2} = \frac{2520}{40} = 63$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(c) 63**
