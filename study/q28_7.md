# CDS Practice Question 28.7

## Question
In a pie diagram showing a family's monthly expenditure on items A (30%), B (25%), C (20%), D (15%), and E (10%):
1. What is the central angle for item A?
2. If total monthly income is `₹ 3000`, what is the expenditure on item C?

- (a) 108°, ₹ 600
- (b) 90°, ₹ 500
- (c) 108°, ₹ 500
- (d) 120°, ₹ 600

---

## Detailed Solution & Derivation

### 1. Central Angle for Item A
- Total pie angle $= 360^\circ$ corresponding to $100\%$.
- Percentage of item A $= 30\%$.

$$\theta_A = 30\% \text{ of } 360^\circ = \frac{30}{100} \times 360^\circ = 108^\circ$$

### 2. Expenditure on Item C
- Total income $= \text{\text{Rs. } } 3000$.
- Percentage of item C $= 20\%$.

$$\text{Expenditure}_C = 20\% \text{ of } 3000 = \frac{20}{100} \times 3000 = \text{\text{Rs. } } 600$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(a) 108°, ₹ 600**
