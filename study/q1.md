# Q1: First Commander-in-Chief of Indian Army

## Question
Who was the first Indian Commander-in-Chief of the independent Indian Army?

- (a) Field Marshal S.H.F.J. Manekshaw
- (b) General K.M. Cariappa
- (c) General K.S. Thimayya
- (d) Air Marshal Subroto Mukherjee

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Historical Context & Appointment
On **15th January 1949**, General (later Field Marshal) **Kodandera Madappa Cariappa** took over as the first Indian Commander-in-Chief of the Indian Army from General Sir Francis Bucher, the last British Commander-in-Chief.

### Step 2: Key Milestones
- To commemorate this historical handover, **15th January** is celebrated every year as **Indian Army Day**.
- General Cariappa was later conferred the rank of **Field Marshal** in 1986.

**Correct Answer**: **(b) General K.M. Cariappa**
