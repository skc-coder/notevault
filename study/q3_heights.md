# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q3

## Question Statement

The angle of elevation of the top of a tower from the bottom of a building is twice that from its top. What is the height of the building, if the height of the tower is $75\text{ m}$ and the angle of elevation of the top of the tower from the bottom of the building is $60^\circ$?

- (a) $25\text{ m}$
- (b) $37.5\text{ m}$
- (c) $50\text{ m}$
- (d) $60\text{ m}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Identify Angles & Setup
- Let tower height $TQ = 75\text{ m}$.
- Let building height be $h$.
- Angle of elevation of top of tower from bottom of building is given as $60^\circ$.
- Angle of elevation of top of tower from top of building is half of $60^\circ$, which is $30^\circ$.

### 2. Distance Between Bases
In the right triangle at the bottom of the building:
$$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{\text{Height of Tower}}{\text{Distance } d} = \frac{75}{d}$$
$$\sqrt{3} = \frac{75}{d} \implies d = \frac{75}{\sqrt{3}} = 25\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$

### 3. Right Triangle at Top of Building
At the top of the building (height $h$), the remaining height to the top of the tower is $75 - h$:
$$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{75 - h}{d}$$
$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{75 - h}{25\sqrt{3}}$$
- Cancel $\sqrt{3}$ on both sides:
  $$1 = \frac{75 - h}{25} \implies 25 = 75 - h \implies h = 50\text{ m}$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(c) $50\text{ m}$**
