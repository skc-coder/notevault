# Q2: National Calendar Foundation

## Question
The National Calendar of India based on the Saka Era was adopted on which date, and which is its first month?

- (a) 26th January 1950; Magha
- (b) 22nd March 1957; Chaitra
- (c) 15th August 1947; Vaisakha
- (d) 22nd July 1947; Phalguna

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Adoption & Structure
The National Calendar of India is based on the **Saka Era** (which began in 78 AD). It was officially adopted on **22nd March, 1957** (1 Chaitra 1879 Saka) along with the Gregorian calendar for official government notifications and broadcasts.

### Step 2: Calendar Properties
- The first month of the Saka calendar is **Chaitra**.
- A normal year consists of 365 days. 1st Chaitra corresponds to **22nd March** in a normal year and **21st March** in a leap year.

**Correct Answer**: **(b) 22nd March 1957; Chaitra**
