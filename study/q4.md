# Q4: Param Vir Chakra First Recipient

## Question
Who was the first recipient of India's highest wartime gallantry decoration, the Param Vir Chakra?

- (a) Major Somnath Sharma
- (b) Lance Naik Karam Singh
- (c) Captain Vikram Batra
- (d) Major Shaitan Singh

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Historical Action
**Major Somnath Sharma** of the 1st Battalion, Kumaon Regiment, was posthumously awarded the first **Param Vir Chakra** for his heroic resistance against raiders at Badgam during the 1947 Indo-Pakistani War.

### Step 2: Key Facts
- Major Somnath Sharma held off the enemy forces near Srinagar airfield despite being outnumbered and critically injured.
- The Param Vir Chakra is India's highest military award for wartime bravery.

**Correct Answer**: **(a) Major Somnath Sharma**
