# Q9: Tamil Nadu Coromandel Coast Koeppen Symbol (As)

## Question Statement
According to Köppen's climatic classification, the climate of the Coromandel / Tamil Nadu coast of India is designated by which of the following symbols?

- **(a)** Amw
- **(b)** **As**
- **(c)** Cwg
- **(d)** Aw

---

## Solution & Intuition

**Correct Answer: (b) As**

### Key Concept Breakdown
- **As Symbol**: Represents a **Tropical Climate with a Dry Summer ($s = \text{summer dry}$)**.
- **Why Tamil Nadu is As**:
  - During the Southwest Monsoon (June to September), the Western Ghats block moisture, placing the Coromandel Coast in a rain-shadow zone (dry summer).
  - Maximum rainfall occurs during **Winter (October to December)** from the **Retreating North-East Monsoon** picking up moisture over the Bay of Bengal!
