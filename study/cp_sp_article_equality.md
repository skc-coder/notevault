# Article Count CP-SP Equivalence

## Theory & Mathematical Formulation

When problem statements specify equality between cost price of $n$ articles and selling price of $m$ articles:

$$n \cdot \text{CP} = m \cdot \text{SP}$$

### 1. Ratio Derivation

- Rearranging the algebraic relation:
  $$\frac{\text{SP}}{\text{CP}} = \frac{n}{m}$$

### 2. Profit / Loss Criteria

- **If $n > m$** (Cost price of more items equals selling price of fewer items):
  - $\text{SP} > \text{CP} \Rightarrow$ **Profit**
  $$\text{Profit } \% = \left( \frac{n - m}{m} \right) \times 100\%$$

- **If $m > n$** (Cost price of fewer items equals selling price of more items):
  - $\text{CP} > \text{SP} \Rightarrow$ **Loss**
  $$\text{Loss } \% = \left( \frac{m - n}{m} \right) \times 100\%$$

### 3. Unit Cost Technique

- Assume cost price of 1 article = $\$1$.
- Cost price of $m$ articles = $\$m$.
- Selling price of $m$ articles = Cost price of $n$ articles = $\$n$.
- Profit on $m$ articles = $\$n - \$m$.
- Profit $\% = \frac{n - m}{m} \times 100\%$.
