# Variation 1: Multi-Statement Planetary Accretion & Nebular Hypotheses

## Tricky Novel Variation Statement

Consider the following statements regarding the origin of the Solar System and planetary classification:

1. In Laplace's revision of the Nebular Hypothesis, planets were formed from material ejected by a companion wandering binary star.
2. Terrestrial planets retained higher concentrations of lighter gaseous elements (Hydrogen and Helium) compared to Jovian planets because of their strong gravitational attraction.
3. The density of Saturn is less than that of water, meaning it would theoretically float in an ocean.
4. Venus exhibits retrograde (clockwise) rotation on its axis, causing the Sun to rise in the west and set in the east.

Which of the statements given above are **CORRECT**?

- **(a)** 1 and 2 only
- **(b)** 3 and 4 only
- **(c)** 1, 3, and 4 only
- **(d)** 2, 3, and 4 only

---

## Detailed Solution & Trap Analysis

**Correct Option: (b) 3 and 4 only**

### Statement Breakdown:
1. **Statement 1 is INCORRECT**:
   - Laplace's Nebular Hypothesis (1796) proposed that planets formed from rings shed by a single slowly rotating cooling nebular cloud.
   - The *wandering companion star* hypothesis was proposed by **Chamberlin & Moulton** (Planetesimal Hypothesis) and **Jeans & Jeffreys** (Tidal Theory), NOT Laplace!
2. **Statement 2 is INCORRECT**:
   - Terrestrial (inner) planets lost their lighter gases (H & He) due to high intense solar winds near the Sun and their smaller gravity. Jovian (outer) planets retained heavy gaseous atmospheres because solar winds were weak at large distances and their mass/gravity was immense.
3. **Statement 3 is CORRECT**:
   - Saturn's average density is $\approx 0.69 \text{ g/cm}^3$, which is less than water ($1.0 \text{ g/cm}^3$).
4. **Statement 4 is CORRECT**:
   - Venus (along with Uranus) rotates retrograde (clockwise, East to West), so the Sun rises in the West.
