# CDS Practice Question 44

## Question
The sum required to earn a monthly interest of ₹400 at 10% per annum simple interest is:
- (a) ₹2,000
- (b) ₹12,000
- (c) ₹24,000
- (d) ₹48,000

---

## Detailed Solution & Proof

- Identify given parameters:
  - Monthly interest $SI = \text{Rs. }400$
  - Annual Rate $R = 10\%$ per annum
  - Time period $T = 1\text{ month} = \frac{1}{12}\text{ years}$

- Apply standard Simple Interest formula:
  $$SI = \frac{P \cdot R \cdot T}{100}$$

- Substitute known values:
  $$400 = \frac{P \cdot 10 \cdot \frac{1}{12}}{100}$$

- Solve for Principal $P$:
  $$400 = \frac{P}{120}$$
  $$P = 400 \times 120 = \text{Rs. }48,000$$

**Correct Option**: **(d) ₹48,000**
