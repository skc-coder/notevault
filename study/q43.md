# CDS Practice Question 43

## Question Statement
Two pipes $A$ and $B$ can fill a tank in $60\text{ min}$ and $75\text{ min}$, respectively. There is also an outlet $C$. If $A, B,$ and $C$ are opened together, then the tank is full in $50\text{ min}$. How much time will be taken by $C$ to empty the full tank?

- (a) $100\text{ min}$
- (b) $110\text{ min}$
- (c) $120\text{ min}$
- (d) $125\text{ min}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

1. **Assign Pipe Filling and Emptying Rates**:
   - Rate of Pipe $A$: $R_A = +\frac{1}{60}\text{ tank/min}$
   - Rate of Pipe $B$: $R_B = +\frac{1}{75}\text{ tank/min}$
   - Rate of Outlet $C$: $R_C = -\frac{1}{c}\text{ tank/min}$ (where $c$ is time for $C$ alone to empty tank)

2. **Formulate Combined Rate Equation**:
   When $A, B, C$ open together, tank fills in $50\text{ min}$:
   $$R_{A+B-C} = \frac{1}{60} + \frac{1}{75} - \frac{1}{c} = \frac{1}{50}$$

3. **Solve for Emptying Rate $\frac{1}{c}$**:
   $$\frac{1}{c} = \frac{1}{60} + \frac{1}{75} - \frac{1}{50}$$
   Find LCM of $(60, 75, 50) = 300$:
   $$\frac{1}{c} = \frac{5 + 4 - 6}{300} = \frac{3}{300} = \frac{1}{100}$$

4. **Invert to Find Time $c$**:
   $$c = 100\text{ minutes}$$

- **Correct Option**: **(a) 100 min**
