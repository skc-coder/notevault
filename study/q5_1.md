# CDS Pathfinder Ch 5 Q1

## Question

Evaluate the square root:
$$\sqrt{\frac{0.324 \times 0.64 \times 129.6}{0.729 \times 1.024 \times 36}}$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

- Step 1: Count decimal places in numerator and denominator:
  - Numerator decimal places: $3 + 2 + 1 = 6$.
  - Denominator decimal places: $3 + 3 + 0 = 6$.
  - Since total decimal places match ($6 = 6$), multiply by $10^6$ to clear all decimals:
    $$\frac{0.324 \times 0.64 \times 129.6}{0.729 \times 1.024 \times 36} = \frac{324 \times 64 \times 1296}{729 \times 1024 \times 36}$$

- Step 2: Express numbers as perfect squares or prime powers:
  - $324 = 18^2$
  - $64 = 8^2$
  - $1296 = 36^2$
  - $729 = 27^2$
  - $1024 = 32^2$
  - $36 = 6^2$

- Step 3: Compute the square root by extracting bases:
  $$\sqrt{\frac{324 \times 64 \times 1296}{729 \times 1024 \times 36}} = \frac{18 \times 8 \times 36}{27 \times 32 \times 6}$$

- Step 4: Simplify numerator and denominator:
  - Numerator: $18 \times 8 \times 36 = 5184$.
  - Denominator: $27 \times 32 \times 6 = 5184$.
  $$\frac{5184}{5184} = 1$$

---

## Key Takeaway

Always match total decimal places in fractional expressions involving square roots first. When total numerator decimals equal total denominator decimals, all decimal points can be removed cleanly.
