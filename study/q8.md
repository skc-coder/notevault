# Kuroshio & Ocean Current Circulation (CAPF 2024)

## Question Statement (CAPF 2024)
Match Ocean Current with its location: Kuroshio Current, Humboldt Current, Canary Current, Gulf Stream.

Kuroshio -> Warm Pacific Humboldt -> Cold Pacific (Peru) Canary -> Cold Atlantic Gulf Stream -> Warm Atlantic

---

## Solution & Intuition

**Correct Answer:** Kuroshio is a warm current in the North Pacific Ocean.

### Key Concept Breakdown
Kuroshio Current flows northward along Japan (warm). Humboldt (Peru) Current flows along South America (cold). Gulf Stream flows along North America to Western Europe (warm).
