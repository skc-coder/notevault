# Variation 30: Income Expenditure Savings Shift and Percentage Net Growth

## Problem Statement
Given an individual saving $s\%$ of income $I$. If income grows by $i\%$ and expenditure grows by $e\%$, analyze the percentage growth in savings $S$.

---

## Proof & Theoretical Intuition

### 1. Weighted Growth Identity
Let initial Income $= 100$.
- Expenditure $E = 100 - s$.
- Savings $S = s$.
- Absolute income increment $\Delta I = 100 \times \frac{i}{100} = i$.
- Absolute expenditure increment $\Delta E = (100 - s) \times \frac{e}{100}$.
- Absolute savings increment $\Delta S = \Delta I - \Delta E = i - \frac{(100 - s)e}{100}$.
- Percentage growth in savings:
  $$\text{Savings Growth \%} = \frac{\Delta S}{S} \times 100\% = \frac{i - \frac{(100 - s)e}{100}}{s} \times 100\% = \frac{100i - (100 - s)e}{s}\%$$
