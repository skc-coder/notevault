# Sphere, Hemisphere & Spherical Shell

## 1. Sphere

A 3D solid generated by revolving a circle about any of its diameters. Every point on its boundary is equidistant ($r$) from its centre.

- **Volume ($V$)**:
  $$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$$
- **Surface Area ($S$)**:
  $$S = 4\pi r^2$$

---

## 2. Hemisphere

A plane passing through the centre of a sphere cuts it into two equal halves called **Hemispheres**.

- **Volume ($V$)**:
  $$V = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3$$
- **Curved Surface Area ($\text{CSA}$)**:
  $$\text{CSA} = 2\pi r^2$$
- **Total Surface Area ($\text{TSA}$)**:
  $$\text{TSA} = \text{CSA} + \text{Flat Circular Base Area} = 2\pi r^2 + \pi r^2 = 3\pi r^2$$

---

## 3. Spherical Shell (Hollow Sphere)

A solid bounded by two concentric spheres of outer radius $R$ and inner radius $r$.

- **Thickness of Shell ($t$)**:
  $$t = R - r$$
- **Volume of Solid Material ($V$)**:
  $$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi (R^3 - r^3)$$
- **Outer Surface Area**:
  $$S_{\text{outer}} = 4\pi R^2$$
- **Inner Surface Area**:
  $$S_{\text{inner}} = 4\pi r^2$$
- **Total Surface Area ($\text{TSA}$)**:
  $$\text{TSA} = 4\pi (R^2 + r^2)$$

---

## 4. Solid Submersion & Displacement Principle

When a solid body of volume $V_{\text{solid}}$ is completely submerged into a liquid contained in a cylinder of radius $R$:
$$\text{Volume of Displaced Liquid} = \text{Volume of Solid}$$
$$\pi R^2 \cdot \Delta h = V_{\text{solid}}$$
$$\Delta h = \frac{V_{\text{solid}}}{\pi R^2}$$

---

## 5. Recasting / Melting Equality

When $N$ small spherical drops of radius $r$ melt together to form a large sphere of radius $R$:
$$N \times \left(\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3\right) = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3 \implies R = r \cdot N^{1/3}$$
Ratio of Total Initial Surface Area to Final Surface Area:
$$\frac{S_{\text{initial}}}{S_{\text{final}}} = \frac{N \cdot 4\pi r^2}{4\pi R^2} = \frac{N r^2}{(r N^{1/3})^2} = N^{1/3}$$

---
