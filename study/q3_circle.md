# Q3: Alternate Segment Theorem & Angle Calculation

## Question
In the given circle, $PQ$ is a tangent to the circle at point $A$. $AB$ is a chord such that $\angle BAQ = 55^\circ$. If $C$ is a point on the major arc $AB$, what is the measure of $\angle ACB$?

- (a) $55^\circ$
- (b) $65^\circ$
- (c) $125^\circ$
- (d) $35^\circ$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: State Alternate Segment Theorem
The angle between a tangent ($PQ$) and a chord ($AB$) through the point of contact ($A$) is equal to the angle subtended by the chord in the alternate segment.

### Step 2: Apply Angle Relation
The angle in the alternate segment subtended by chord $AB$ at point $C$ on the major arc is $\angle ACB$.
$$\angle ACB = \angle BAQ$$

Given $\angle BAQ = 55^\circ$:
$$\angle ACB = 55^\circ$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(a) $55^\circ$**
