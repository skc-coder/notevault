# Basic Profit and Loss Invariants

## Theory & Mathematical Formulation

### Core Definitions & Invariants

1. **Cost Price ($\text{CP}$)**:
   - The effective total expenditure incurred to acquire an item.
   - Includes purchase price plus overhead costs (freight, repair, transport, damage):
     $$\text{CP}_{\text{total}} = \text{CP}_{\text{base}} + \text{Overhead}$$

2. **Selling Price ($\text{SP}$)**:
   - The final price at which the article is sold to a customer.

3. **Profit & Loss Criteria**:
   - Profit occurs strictly when $\text{SP} > \text{CP}$:
     $$\text{Profit} = \text{SP} - \text{CP}$$
   - Loss occurs strictly when $\text{CP} > \text{SP}$:
     $$\text{Loss} = \text{CP} - \text{SP}$$

4. **Base Reference Rule**:
   - Unless explicitly stated otherwise, profit percentage ($P\%$) and loss percentage ($L\%$) are **always calculated relative to Cost Price ($\text{CP}$)** as the denominator:
     $$P\% = \left( \frac{\text{SP} - \text{CP}}{\text{CP}} \right) \times 100\%$$
     $$L\% = \left( \frac{\text{CP} - \text{SP}}{\text{CP}} \right) \times 100\%$$

5. **Direct Multipliers**:
   - Selling price given profit $r\%$:
     $$\text{SP} = \text{CP} \times \left( 1 + \frac{r}{100} \right) = \text{CP} \times \left( \frac{100 + r}{100} \right)$$
   - Selling price given loss $r\%$:
     $$\text{SP} = \text{CP} \times \left( 1 - \frac{r}{100} \right) = \text{CP} \times \left( \frac{100 - r}{100} \right)$$
