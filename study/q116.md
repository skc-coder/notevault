# Question 116: Total Distance Covered by a Rebounding Ball (Infinite GP)

A elastic ball is dropped from a height of 100 meters. Every time it hits the ground, it rebounds to $\frac{3}{5}$ of its previous height. What is the total distance traveled by the ball before coming to rest?

(a) 250 m  
(b) 400 m  
(c) 300 m  
(d) 500 m  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Shortcut Formula:**  
> For a ball dropped from height $H$ that rebounds to fraction $r$ of height each bounce:
> $$\text{Total Distance} = H \times \left(\frac{1 + r}{1 - r}\right)$$
> 
> **Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
> 1. Initial drop $= H = 100\text{ m}$.
> 2. First bounce: Up $100 \times \frac{3}{5}$ + Down $100 \times \frac{3}{5} = 2 \times 100 \times \frac{3}{5}$.
> 3. Total distance $= H + 2H(r + r^2 + r^3 + \dots)$
>    $$\text{Total Distance} = H + 2H \left(\frac{r}{1 - r}\right) = H \left(1 + \frac{2r}{1 - r}\right) = H \left(\frac{1 + r}{1 - r}\right)$$
> 
> Substitute $H = 100$ and $r = \frac{3}{5}$:
> $$\text{Total Distance} = 100 \times \frac{1 + \frac{3}{5}}{1 - \frac{3}{5}} = 100 \times \frac{8/5}{2/5} = 100 \times 4 = 400\text{ m}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 400 m**
