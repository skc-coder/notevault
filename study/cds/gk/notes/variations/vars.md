# General Knowledge Conceptual Variations

## Variations

### Variation 1: Missile Classification & Launch Platforms
* **Trap**: Confusing air-to-air missiles with surface-to-surface or anti-tank missiles.
* **Key Distinctions**:
  - **Prithvi / Agni**: Surface-to-Surface Ballistic Missiles.
  - **Trishul / Akash**: Surface-to-Air Defense Missiles.
  - **Nag**: Anti-tank Guided Missile (Air-launched variant: *Helina*).
  - **Astra**: Beyond-Visual-Range (BVR) Air-to-Air Missile.
  - **BrahMos**: Supersonic Cruise Missile (Land, sea, sub-sea, and air launch capability).

---

### Variation 2: Award Hierarchy: Wartime vs. Peacetime
* **Wartime Gallantry (In the face of enemy)**:
  1. Param Vir Chakra (PVC)
  2. Maha Vir Chakra (MVC)
  3. Vir Chakra (VrC)
* **Peacetime Gallantry (Other than face of enemy)**:
  1. Ashoka Chakra (Equivalent to PVC in rank)
  2. Kirti Chakra
  3. Shaurya Chakra

---

### Variation 3: Governor-General & Constitutional Transitions
* **1773**: Warren Hastings became the **First Governor-General of Bengal** (Regulating Act 1773).
* **1833**: Lord William Bentinck became the **First Governor-General of India** (Charter Act 1833).
* **1858**: Lord Canning became the **First Viceroy of India** (Government of India Act 1858).
* **1947**: Lord Mountbatten became **First Governor-General of Free India**.
* **1948**: C. Rajagopalachari became **First & Only Indian Governor-General of Free India**.
