# Q3: IGMDP Missile Classifications

## Question
Which one of the following Indian missiles developed under the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) is a 3rd-generation "Fire-and-Forget" anti-tank guided missile?

- (a) Trishul
- (b) Akash
- (c) Nag
- (d) Agni

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: IGMDP Overview
The Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP) managed five primary missile development projects:
1. **Prithvi**: Surface-to-surface ballistic missile.
2. **Agni**: Intermediate/Intercontinental ballistic missile.
3. **Trishul**: Short-range surface-to-air missile.
4. **Akash**: Medium-range surface-to-air missile.
5. **Nag**: Anti-tank guided missile.

### Step 2: Nag Properties
- **Nag** is a 3rd-generation **Fire-and-Forget** anti-tank guided missile equipped with imaging infrared (IIR) guidance.
- Its air-launched version is known as **Helina** (Helicopter-launched Nag).

**Correct Answer**: **(c) Nag**
