# Civilian, Gallantry & Literary Awards

## Honors & Hierarchy Matrix

### 1. Highest Civilian Awards
* **Bharat Ratna**: Highest national award (Instituted 2nd January 1954).
  - First recipients: C. Rajagopalachari, Dr. S. Radhakrishnan, Dr. C.V. Raman (1954).
  - First Posthumous recipient: Lal Bahadur Shastri (1966).
  - Non-Indian recipients: Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan (1987), Nelson Mandela (1990).
* **Padma Awards**: Padma Vibhushan (2nd highest), Padma Bhushan (3rd highest), Padma Shri (4th highest).

### 2. Gallantry Awards (Military)

#### Wartime Gallantry Awards (Face of the Enemy)
1. **Param Vir Chakra (PVC)**: Highest military decoration.
   - First recipient: **Major Somnath Sharma** (Posthumous, 1st Battalion Kumaon Regiment, 1947 Badgam Operation).
2. **Maha Vir Chakra (MVC)**: 2nd highest wartime bravery award.
3. **Vir Chakra (VrC)**: 3rd highest wartime bravery award.

#### Peacetime Gallantry Awards (Other than face of the Enemy)
1. **Ashoka Chakra**: Highest peacetime gallantry award (Equivalent to PVC).
2. **Kirti Chakra**: 2nd highest peacetime bravery award.
3. **Shaurya Chakra**: 3rd highest peacetime bravery award.

### 3. Literary & Cinema Awards
* **Jnanpith Award**: Highest Indian literary award. Instituted 1961. First awarded in 1965 to G. Sankara Kurup (Malayalam). First woman recipient: Ashapoorna Devi (Bengali, 1976).
* **Sahitya Akademi Award**: Conferred annually for 24 languages.
* **Dadasaheb Phalke Award**: Highest honor in Indian Cinema (Instituted 1969). First recipient: Devika Rani.
