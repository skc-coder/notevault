# Firsts in India & National Milestones

## Key Principles & Historical Milestones

### Executive & Judicial Firsts
1. **Governor-General of Independent India**: Lord Mountbatten (August 1947 – June 1948).
2. **First & Only Indian Governor-General**: C. Rajagopalachari (June 1948 – January 1950).
3. **First President of India**: Dr. Rajendra Prasad (1950 – 1962).
4. **First Prime Minister of India**: Jawaharlal Nehru (1947 – 1964).
5. **First Vice-President**: Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan.
6. **First Chief Justice of Supreme Court**: Justice H.J. Kania (1950).
7. **First Chief Election Commissioner**: Sukumar Sen (1950).
8. **First Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG)**: V. Narahari Rao.

### Armed Forces Firsts
1. **First Commander-in-Chief of Indian Army**: General K.M. Cariappa (15th January 1949 — celebrated as Army Day).
2. **First Field Marshal**: S.H.F.J. Manekshaw (1973). Second Field Marshal: K.M. Cariappa (1986).
3. **First Chief of Naval Staff**: Vice Admiral R.D. Katari (1958).
4. **First Indian Chief of Air Staff**: Air Marshal Subroto Mukherjee (1954).
5. **First Marshal of the Air Force**: Arjan Singh (2002).

### Women Firsts in India
1. **First Woman President**: Pratibha Devisingh Patil (2007–2012).
2. **First Woman Prime Minister**: Indira Gandhi (1966–1977, 1980–1984).
3. **First Woman Governor**: Sarojini Naidu (Governor of United Provinces / UP).
4. **First Woman Chief Minister**: Sucheta Kripalani (UP, 1963).
5. **First Woman Speaker of Lok Sabha**: Meira Kumar (2009).
6. **First Woman Judge of Supreme Court**: M. Fathima Beevi (1989).
7. **First Woman IPS Officer**: Kiran Bedi (1972).
8. **First Indian Woman to win Nobel Peace Prize**: Mother Teresa (1979).
9. **First Indian Woman in Space**: Kalpana Chawla (1997, STS-87).
