# Defense, Armed Forces & Space Technology

## Operational Structures & Missile Programs

### 1. Tri-Services Commands & Commands Location
* **Tri-Services Commands**:
  - **ANC**: Andaman and Nicobar Command (Port Blair) — First integrated tri-service command (2001).
  - **SFC**: Strategic Forces Command (New Delhi) — Manages nuclear arsenal assets.
* **Army Commands**: Northern (Udhampur), Southern (Pune), Eastern (Kolkata), Western (Chandimandir), Central (Lucknow), Training (ARTRAC, Shimla), South-Western (Jaipur).
* **Navy Commands**: Western (Mumbai), Eastern (Visakhapatnam), Southern (Kochi).
* **Air Force Commands**: Western (New Delhi), Eastern (Shillong), Central (Prayagraj), Southern (Thiruvananthapuram), South-Western (Gandhinagar), Maintenance (Nagpur), Training (Bengaluru).

### 2. Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP)
Conceived by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam (1983) under DRDO:
* **P**: **Prithvi** — Short-range surface-to-surface ballistic missile.
* **A**: **Agni** — Medium-to-Intercontinental ballistic missile series.
* **T**: **Trishul** — Short-range surface-to-air missile (12 km range).
* **A**: **Akash** — Medium-range surface-to-air missile (25 km range, multi-target engagement).
* **N**: **Nag** — 3rd-generation anti-tank missile ("Fire and Forget").

### 3. Submarines & Naval Fleets
* **Nuclear Submarines**: INS Arihant (First indigenous nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine), INS Arighat.
* **Aircraft Carriers**: INS Vikramaditya, INS Vikrant (First indigenous aircraft carrier - IAC-1).

### 4. Space & Satellite Milestones
* **Aryabhata**: First Indian satellite launched on 19th April, 1975 from Kapustin Yar (USSR).
* **Rohini (RS-1)**: First satellite launched by an indigenous launch vehicle (SLV-3) in 1980.
* **Chandrayaan-1**: Launched 2008 (Discovered water molecules on Moon).
* **Mangalyaan (MOM)**: Mars Orbiter Mission launched 5th November, 2013 (First Asian nation to enter Martian orbit).
