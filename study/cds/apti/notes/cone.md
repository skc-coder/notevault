When a cone is cut by a plane **parallel to the base**:
- The **small cone** formed at the top is **similar** to the original cone.
- By **similar triangles**:
  $$ \frac{r_{small}}{r_{big}} = \frac{h_{small}}{h_{big}} $$

### Volume Ratio
Since volume $V \propto r^2 h$, and $r \propto h$:
$$ \frac{V_{new}}{V_{old}} = \left( \frac{h_1}{h_2} \right)^3 $$

Where:
- $h_1$ = height of small cone
- $h_2$ = height of original cone
