A multi-term ratio like $a : b : c : d = x_1 : x_2 : x_3 : x_4$ simply states that all quantities share a common scaling factor. It is shorthand for the simultaneous pairwise proportions:

$$\frac{a}{x_1} = \frac{b}{x_2} = \frac{c}{x_3} = \frac{d}{x_4} = k \quad (k \neq 0)$$


> [!example] The Shortcut Method
> Given $A:B = 2:3$, $B:C = 4:5$, $C:D = 6:11$, $D:E = 12:17$.
> 
> To find $A:B:C:D:E$:
> 1. **A:** Product of all numerators ($2 \times 4 \times 6 \times 12$)
> 2. **B:** First denom $\times$ remaining numerators ($3 \times 4 \times 6 \times 12$)
> 3. **C:** First two denoms $\times$ last two numerators ($3 \times 5 \times 6 \times 12$)
> 4. **D:** First three denoms $\times$ last numerator ($3 \times 5 \times 11 \times 12$)
> 5. **E:** Product of all denominators ($3 \times 5 \times 11 \times 17$)

### Transformations

If $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$, then:
- **Componendo:** $\frac{a+b}{b} = \frac{c+d}{d}$
- **Dividendo:** $\frac{a-b}{b} = \frac{c-d}{d}$
- **Compenendo Dividendo:** $\frac{a+b}{a-b} = \frac{c+d}{c-d}$
