
$$\text{Profit} = \text{Selling Price} - \text{Cost Price}$$

$$\text{Profit \%} = \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{Cost Price}} \times 100$$
$$ SP = (1 + k) \cdot CP \quad \text{when } k \text{ is profit fraction} $$

$$ SP = (1 - k) \cdot CP \quad \text{when } k \text{ is loss fraction} $$
