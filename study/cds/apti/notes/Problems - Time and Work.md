31  In a fort there was sufficient food for 200 soldiers
for 31 days. After 27 days 120 soldiers left the fort.
For how many extra days will the rest of the food
last for the remaining soldiers?
(a) 12 days
(b) 10 days
(c) 8 days
(d) 6 days
- The rest of the food will last for **6 extra days** for the remaining soldiers. 

- **Initial Food Supply**: The fort had enough food for 200 soldiers for 31 days, totaling 6,200 soldier-days. 
    
- **Consumption**: After 27 days, the 200 soldiers consumed 5,400 soldier-days ($200 \times 27$), leaving 800 soldier-days of food. 
    
- **Remaining Soldiers**: With 120 soldiers leaving, 80 soldiers remained ($200 - 120 = 80$). 
    
- **Duration Calculation**: The remaining 800 soldier-days of food will last the 80 soldiers for 10 days ($800 \div 80 = 10$). 
    
- **Extra Days**: Since 4 days were originally remaining in the schedule ($31 - 27$), the **extra** days the food lasts due to the reduced population is $10 - 4 = \mathbf{6}$ **days**.
