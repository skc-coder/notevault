## Rate and time
rate (efficiency) = 1/time taken to get the work done (1 unit)
Suppose two people work. Their individual rates be r1, r2 and time t1, t2.
Then time taken when they work together = t1 || t2 = 1/r1 || 1/r2.

$$
a || b = \frac{ab}{a+b} \quad \text{days}
$$

$$
\text{Work} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Time} = \text{Man} \times \text{Days}

## Percentage Method (Avoid LCM)

Convert work per day into percentages for faster mental math.

**Example:**  
- A: 10 days → **10%** work/day  
- B: 12 days → **8.33%** work/day  

Together:  
$$
10\% + 8.33\% = 18.33\% \ \text{per day}
$$

To complete 100%:  
$$
\frac{100}{18.33} \approx 5.45 \ \text{days}
$$

**Mental check:**  
5 days = \( 18.33 \times 5 = 91.66\% \)  
Remaining: \( 8.33\% \)  
Time for remainder: \( \frac{8.33}{18.33} = \frac{5}{11} \) day  

**Total:** \( 5\frac{5}{11} \) days.
