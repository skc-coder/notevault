# Fundamental Simple Interest Formulas & Rate Dynamics

Simple Interest ($SI$) is calculated strictly on the original Principal ($P$) throughout the entire loan duration ($T$) at a constant rate ($R\%$) per annum.

## Core Formulae & Invariants

1. **Standard Interest Equation**:
   $$SI = \frac{P \cdot R \cdot T}{100}$$

2. **Maturity Amount Equation**:
   $$A = P + SI = P \left(1 + \frac{R \cdot T}{100}\right)$$

3. **Principal from Amount & Rate-Time**:
   $$P = \frac{100 \cdot A}{100 + R \cdot T}$$

4. **Multiplication Time Factor**:
   If a principal doubles ($A = 2P$, so $SI = P$), the required rate is:
   $$R = \frac{100(n - 1)}{T}$$
   where $n$ is the growth multiple ($n = 2$ for doubling).
