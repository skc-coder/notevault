# Remainders

## Theory & Properties

### 1. Chinese Remainder Theorem Basics
If a number $N$ leaves remainder $r_1$ when divided by $m_1$, and remainder $r_2$ when divided by $m_2$:
- General term: $N = a + k \cdot \operatorname{LCM}(m_1, m_2)$, where $a$ is the smallest positive integer satisfying both congruences.
