# Dual Maturity Amounts & Linear Rate System

When a constant principal $P$ grows to amount $A_1$ in time $t_1$ and to amount $A_2$ in time $t_2$ at simple interest rate $R\%$:

## System Equations & Invariants

1. **Difference in Amounts (Interest Difference)**:
   Since principal is constant, interest per year is strictly constant:
   $$I_{\text{annual}} = \frac{A_2 - A_1}{t_2 - t_1}$$

2. **Principal Formula**:
   $$P = \frac{A_1 t_2 - A_2 t_1}{t_2 - t_1}$$

3. **Rate Percentage Formula**:
   $$R = \frac{100(A_2 - A_1)}{A_1 t_2 - A_2 t_1} \times 100\%$$
