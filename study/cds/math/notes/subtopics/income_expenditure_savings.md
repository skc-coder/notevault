# Income, Expenditure, and Savings Models

## Theory & Proofs

### 1. Fundamental Accounting Identity
For any individual:
$$\text{Income } I = \text{Expenditure } E + \text{Savings } S$$

### 2. Differential Percentage Shifts
If Income increases by $\Delta I\%$, Expenditure increases by $\Delta E\%$, then the change in savings $\Delta S$ satisfies:
$$I_0 \cdot \Delta I\% = E_0 \cdot \Delta E\% + S_0 \cdot \Delta S\%$$
Dividing by $S_0$:
$$\Delta S\% = \frac{I_0 \cdot \Delta I\% - E_0 \cdot \Delta E\%}{S_0}$$
