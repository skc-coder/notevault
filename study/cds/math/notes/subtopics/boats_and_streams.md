# Boats, Streams & Upstream/Downstream Motion

## 1. Key Definitions

- **Speed of Boat in Still Water ($u$ or $x$)**: Speed of boat when stream velocity is zero.
- **Speed of Stream / Current ($v$ or $y$)**: Speed at which river water flows.
- **Downstream Speed ($S_d$)**: Motion along the flow of the stream.
  $$S_d = u + v$$
- **Upstream Speed ($S_u$)**: Motion against the flow of the stream.
  $$S_u = u - v \quad (\text{where } u > v)$$

---

## 2. Derived Formulas

### Speed of Boat in Still Water ($u$)
$$u = \frac{S_d + S_u}{2}$$

### Speed of Stream / Current ($v$)
$$v = \frac{S_d - S_u}{2}$$

---

## 3. Round-Trip River Journey Invariant

If a boat goes a distance $D$ downstream and returns the same distance $D$ upstream:

$$\text{Total Time } T_{\text{total}} = T_{\text{down}} + T_{\text{up}} = \frac{D}{u + v} + \frac{D}{u - v} = \frac{2uD}{u^2 - v^2}$$
