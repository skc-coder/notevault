# Average Speed & Equal Distance Harmonics

## 1. General Definition

Average speed is the total distance covered divided by total time taken over the entire journey:

$$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Total Time}} = \frac{D_1 + D_2 + \dots + D_n}{T_1 + T_2 + \dots + T_n} = \frac{D_1 + D_2 + \dots + D_n}{\frac{D_1}{S_1} + \frac{D_2}{S_2} + \dots + \frac{D_n}{S_n}}$$

---

## 2. Special Case: Equal Distance Segments

### Two Equal Distances
When a distance $D$ is covered at speed $x$ and another equal distance $D$ is covered at speed $y$:

$$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2D}{\frac{D}{x} + \frac{D}{y}} = \frac{2}{\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y}} = \frac{2xy}{x + y}$$

*(This is the harmonic mean of the two speeds $x$ and $y$)*.

### Three Equal Distances
When three equal distances $D$ are covered at speeds $x, y, z$ respectively:

$$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{3}{\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} + \frac{1}{z}} = \frac{3xyz}{xy + yz + zx}$$

---

## 3. Special Case: Equal Time Segments

When a person travels for time $T$ at speed $x$ and then for another equal time $T$ at speed $y$:

$$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{x T + y T}{T + T} = \frac{x + y}{2}$$

*(This is the simple arithmetic mean of the speeds)*.
