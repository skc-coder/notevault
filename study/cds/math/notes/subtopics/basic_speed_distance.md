# Basic Speed, Distance, Time & Unit Conversions

## 1. Mathematical Definitions & Invariants

### fundamental Relation
$$\text{Speed } (S) = \frac{\text{Distance } (D)}{\text{Time } (T)}$$
$$\implies D = S \times T \quad \text{and} \quad T = \frac{D}{S}$$

- **Distance ($D$)**: Length of path traversed (in meters $\text{m}$ or kilometers $\text{km}$).
- **Time ($T$)**: Time duration spent (in seconds $\text{s}$ or hours $\text{h}$).
- **Speed ($S$)**: Rate of change of distance per unit time (in $\text{m/s}$ or $\text{km/h}$).

---

## 2. Unit Conversion Constants

### $\text{km/h}$ to $\text{m/s}$
$$1\text{ km/h} = \frac{1000\text{ m}}{3600\text{ s}} = \frac{5}{18}\text{ m/s}$$
$$\text{Speed in m/s} = \text{Speed in km/h} \times \frac{5}{18}$$

### $\text{m/s}$ to $\text{km/h}$
$$1\text{ m/s} = \frac{\frac{1}{1000}\text{ km}}{\frac{1}{3600}\text{ h}} = \frac{18}{5}\text{ km/h}$$
$$\text{Speed in km/h} = \text{Speed in m/s} \times \frac{18}{5}$$

---

## 3. Constant Proportionality Invariants

1. **If Time ($T$) is Constant**:
   $$\frac{D_1}{D_2} = \frac{S_1}{S_2}$$
   *(Distance covered is directly proportional to speed)*.

2. **If Distance ($D$) is Constant**:
   $$\frac{S_1}{S_2} = \frac{T_2}{T_1} \implies S_1 T_1 = S_2 T_2$$
   *(Speed is inversely proportional to time)*.

3. **If Speed ($S$) is Constant**:
   $$\frac{D_1}{D_2} = \frac{T_1}{T_2}$$
