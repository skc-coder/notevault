# Compound & Multiple Angles

## Theory & Intuition

Compound angle formulas express trigonometric functions of sums or differences of angles $(A \pm B)$ in terms of individual angles $A$ and $B$.

### Addition & Subtraction Formulas
$$\sin(A + B) = \sin A \cos B + \cos A \sin B$$
$$\sin(A - B) = \sin A \cos B - \cos A \sin B$$
$$\cos(A + B) = \cos A \cos B - \sin A \sin B$$
$$\cos(A - B) = \cos A \cos B + \sin A \sin B$$
$$\tan(A + B) = \frac{\tan A + \tan B}{1 - \tan A \tan B}$$
$$\tan(A - B) = \frac{\tan A - \tan B}{1 + \tan A \tan B}$$

### High-Yield Quotient Transformation
$$\frac{\cos \theta + \sin \theta}{\cos \theta - \sin \theta} = \frac{1 + \tan \theta}{1 - \tan \theta} = \tan(45^\circ + \theta)$$
$$\frac{\cos \theta - \sin \theta}{\cos \theta + \sin \theta} = \frac{1 - \tan \theta}{1 + \tan \theta} = \tan(45^\circ - \theta)$$

### Double & Triple Angle Formulas
- **Double Angles**:
  $$\sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta = \frac{2\tan\theta}{1 + \tan^2\theta}$$
  $$\cos 2\theta = \cos^2\theta - \sin^2\theta = 2\cos^2\theta - 1 = 1 - 2\sin^2\theta = \frac{1 - \tan^2\theta}{1 + \tan^2\theta}$$
  $$\tan 2\theta = \frac{2\tan\theta}{1 - \tan^2\theta}$$

- **Triple Angles**:
  $$\sin 3\theta = 3\sin\theta - 4\sin^3\theta \implies \sin^3\theta = \frac{3\sin\theta - \sin 3\theta}{4}$$
  $$\cos 3\theta = 4\cos^3\theta - 3\cos\theta \implies \cos^3\theta = \frac{3\cos\theta + \cos 3\theta}{4}$$
  $$\tan 3\theta = \frac{3\tan\theta - \tan^3\theta}{1 - 3\tan^2\theta}$$
