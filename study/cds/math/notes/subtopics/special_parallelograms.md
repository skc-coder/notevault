# Rectangle, Rhombus & Square

## 1. Rectangle
A parallelogram with all interior angles equal to $90^\circ$.
- **Diagonals**: Equal in length ($AC = BD$) and bisect each other.
- **Midpoint Quad**: Joining midpoints of a rectangle yields a **rhombus**.
- **Circle Inscription**: Diagonals equal the diameter of the circumscribed circle ($2R = d = \sqrt{L^2 + B^2}$).
- **British Flag Theorem / Interior Point Relation**: For any point $O$ inside rectangle $ABCD$:
  $$OA^2 + OC^2 = OB^2 + OD^2$$
- **Formulas**:
  - $\text{Area} = L \times B$
  - $\text{Perimeter} = 2(L + B)$
  - $\text{Diagonal } d = \sqrt{L^2 + B^2}$

## 2. Rhombus
A parallelogram with all four sides equal ($a$).
- **Diagonals**: Perpendicular bisectors of each other ($\angle AOB = 90^\circ$). They bisect vertex angles.
- **Midpoint Quad**: Joining midpoints of a rhombus yields a **rectangle**.
- **Diagonal-Side Identity**:
  $$d_1^2 + d_2^2 = 4a^2 \implies a = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{d_1^2 + d_2^2}$$
- **Formulas**:
  - $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} d_1 d_2 = a \cdot h$
  - $\text{Perimeter} = 4a$

## 3. Square
A regular quadrilateral with 4 equal sides ($a$) and 4 right angles ($90^\circ$).
- **Diagonals**: Equal ($d = a\sqrt{2}$), perpendicular bisectors, and bisect vertex angles ($45^\circ$).
- **Midpoint Quad**: Joining midpoints of a square yields another **square**.
- **Circumradius & Inradius**:
  - Inradius $r = \frac{a}{2}$ (Inscribed circle diameter $= a$)
  - Circumradius $R = \frac{a}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{d}{2}$ (Circumscribed circle diameter $= d$)
- **Formulas**:
  - $\text{Area} = a^2 = \frac{1}{2} d^2$
  - $\text{Perimeter} = 4a$
