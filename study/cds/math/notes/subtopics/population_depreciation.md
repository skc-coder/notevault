# Population Growth & Compound Depreciation

## Theory & Proofs

### 1. Multiplicative Compounding Principle
If population or value $P$ grows at rate $R\%$ annually:
- Year 1: $P_1 = P_0 (1 + r)$ where $r = \frac{R}{100}$.
- Year 2: $P_2 = P_1 (1 + r) = P_0 (1 + r)^2$.
- Year $n$: $P_n = P_0 (1 + r)^n = P_0 \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^n$.

### 2. Reversing Time (Population $n$ Years Ago)
To find past population $P_{\text{past}}$ given current population $P_0$:
$$P_0 = P_{\text{past}} \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^n \implies P_{\text{past}} = \frac{P_0}{\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^n}$$
