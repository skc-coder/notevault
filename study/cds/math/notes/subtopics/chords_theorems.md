# Chords, Angles & Perpendicular Theorems

## 1. Core Theorems & Intuition

### Theorem 1: Perpendicular Bisector of a Chord
The perpendicular drawn from the centre $O$ of a circle to a chord $AB$ bisects the chord at $M$.
$$OM \perp AB \implies AM = MB = \frac{1}{2} AB$$

- **Converse**: The line joining the centre to the midpoint of a chord is perpendicular to the chord.
- **Pythagorean Relation**:
  In right-angled triangle $\Delta OMA$:
  $$r^2 = OM^2 + \left(\frac{AB}{2}\right)^2$$
  where $r = OA$ is the radius, $OM = d$ is the distance from the centre, and $AB$ is the chord length.

### Theorem 2: Equal Chords & Distance from Centre
- Equal chords of a circle are equidistant from the centre.
  $$AB = CD \iff OM = ON$$
- Out of two chords, the one closer to the centre is longer:
  $$OM < ON \iff AB > CD$$

### Theorem 3: Angle Subtended at Centre vs Circumference
The angle subtended by an arc at the centre of a circle is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circumference.
$$\angle AOB = 2 \angle APB$$

### Theorem 4: Angle in a Semi-Circle & Same Segment
- **Semi-circle Angle**: The angle in a semi-circle is a right angle ($90^\circ$).
  If $AB$ is a diameter, then $\angle APB = 90^\circ$.
- **Angles in Same Segment**: Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal.
  $$\angle APB = \angle AQB$$
