# Locus & Concentric Circles

## 1. Core Concepts & Properties

### Basic Locus Theorems in Circles
1. **Locus of points at a fixed distance $r$ from a fixed point $O$**:
   - A circle of radius $r$ centered at $O$.
2. **Locus of the center of a circle passing through two fixed points $A$ and $B$**:
   - The perpendicular bisector of the line segment $AB$.
3. **Locus of the midpoints of equal chords in a circle**:
   - A concentric circle of radius equal to the distance of the equal chords from the center.
4. **Locus of a point $P$ such that the length of tangents from $P$ to a circle $S$ of radius $r$ and center $C$ is constant ($L$)**:
   - A concentric circle centered at $C$ with radius $R = \sqrt{r^2 + L^2}$.

### Concentric Circles & Annulus
Concentric circles are two or more circles having the same center $O$ but different radii ($R > r$).
- **Chords of Outer Circle Touching Inner Circle**:
  A chord $AB$ of the outer circle which touches the inner circle at $P$:
  - Is bisected at $P$ ($AP = PB$).
  - Length of chord $AB = 2\sqrt{R^2 - r^2}$.
- **Area of Annulus (Ring)**:
  $$\text{Area} = \pi (R^2 - r^2) = \pi \left(\frac{AB}{2}\right)^2$$
