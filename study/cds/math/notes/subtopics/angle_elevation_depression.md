# Angle of Elevation & Depression Fundamentals

## Theory & Intuitive Foundations

### Line of Sight & Reference Axes
1. **Horizontal Reference Line**:
   - Drawn parallel to the horizontal surface (ground plane) passing through the observer's eye.
2. **Angle of Elevation ($\theta$)**:
   - Formed when looking upwards at an object elevated above eye level.
   - $\tan \theta = \frac{\text{Perpendicular Height}}{\text{Base Distance}}$
3. **Angle of Depression ($\phi$)**:
   - Formed when looking downwards at an object below eye level.
   - Alternate interior angles ensure that $\phi$ equals the angle of elevation of the observer from the target object.
