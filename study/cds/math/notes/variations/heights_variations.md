# Heights and Distances Master Variations

## Tier 1 Topic & Subtopic Variations

### Variation 1: Equal Height and Shadow Ratio
- **Concept**: When $h = s$, $\tan \theta = 1 \implies \theta = 45^\circ$.
- **Novel Variant**: If height of a building is $\sqrt{3}$ times its shadow, the Sun's altitude is $\arctan(\sqrt{3}) = 60^\circ$. If shadow is $\sqrt{3}$ times height, Sun's altitude is $\arctan(1/\sqrt{3}) = 30^\circ$.

---

### Variation 2: Two-Point Shift Along Line
- **Formula**: $d = h(\cot \alpha - \cot \beta)$
- **Novel Variant**: If moving $40\text{ m}$ towards a tower changes elevation from $30^\circ$ to $45^\circ$:
  $$40 = h(\cot 30^\circ - \cot 45^\circ) = h(\sqrt{3} - 1) \implies h = \frac{40}{\sqrt{3} - 1} = 20(\sqrt{3} + 1)\text{ m}$$

---

### Variation 3: Complementary Angles Geometric Mean Theorem
- **Theorem**: If two points at distances $a$ and $b$ from tower base form complementary elevation angles to the top, height $h = \sqrt{ab}$.
- **Proof**: $\tan \theta = h/b$ and $\tan(90^\circ - \theta) = \cot \theta = h/a \implies \tan \theta \cdot \cot \theta = h^2 / (ab) = 1 \implies h = \sqrt{ab}$.

---

### Variation 4: Mounted Flagstaff/Antenna General Ratio
- **Formula**: Tower height $H = \frac{p \tan \alpha}{\tan \beta - \tan \alpha}$
- **Novel Variant**: Antenna height $p = 50\text{ m}$, $\alpha = 30^\circ$, $\beta = 60^\circ$:
  $$H = \frac{50 \cdot (1/\sqrt{3})}{\sqrt{3} - 1/\sqrt{3}} = \frac{50/\sqrt{3}}{2/\sqrt{3}} = 25\text{ m}$$

---

### Variation 5: Window-Level Opposite Building Height
- **Formula**: $H = h(1 + \tan \theta \cot \phi)$
- **Novel Variant**: Window height $h = 15\text{ m}$, elevation $\theta = 45^\circ$, depression $\phi = 30^\circ$:
  $$H = 15(1 + 1 \cdot \sqrt{3}) = 15(1 + \sqrt{3})\text{ m}$$
