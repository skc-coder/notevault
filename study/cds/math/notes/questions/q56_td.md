# CDS Practice Question 56

## Question
The speed of a boat upstream and downstream are $7\text{ km/h}$ and $13\text{ km/h}$ respectively. Then, the speed of the stream and the speed of the boat in still water are:
- (a) $3\text{ km/h}$ and $10\text{ km/h}$
- (b) $6\text{ km/h}$ and $20\text{ km/h}$
- (c) $9\text{ km/h}$ and $15\text{ km/h}$
- (d) $12\text{ km/h}$ and $40\text{ km/h}$

---

## Detailed Solution & Proof

### Step 1: Given Values
- Downstream Speed $S_d = 13\text{ km/h}$
- Upstream Speed $S_u = 7\text{ km/h}$

### Step 2: Apply Speed Equations
1. **Speed of Boat in Still Water ($u$)**:
   $$u = \frac{S_d + S_u}{2} = \frac{13 + 7}{2} = \frac{20}{2} = 10\text{ km/h}$$

2. **Speed of Stream ($v$)**:
   $$v = \frac{S_d - S_u}{2} = \frac{13 - 7}{2} = \frac{6}{2} = 3\text{ km/h}$$

**Correct Option**: **(a) $3\text{ km/h}$ and $10\text{ km/h}$**
