# CDS Practice Question 54

## Question
Kiran covers a certain distance at $80\text{ km/h}$ and returns back to the same point at $20\text{ km/h}$. Then, the average speed during the whole journey is:
- (a) $35\text{ km/h}$
- (b) $32\text{ km/h}$
- (c) $30\text{ km/h}$
- (d) $28\text{ km/h}$

---

## Detailed Solution & Proof

### Step 1: Identify Distance Condition
The distance for the outward journey is equal to the distance for the return journey ($D_1 = D_2 = D$).

### Step 2: Apply Harmonic Mean Formula
When two equal distances are covered at speeds $x$ and $y$, average speed is:
$$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2xy}{x + y}$$

Substitute $x = 80\text{ km/h}$ and $y = 20\text{ km/h}$:
$$S_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 \times 80 \times 20}{80 + 20} = \frac{3200}{100} = 32\text{ km/h}$$

**Correct Option**: **(b) $32\text{ km/h}$**
