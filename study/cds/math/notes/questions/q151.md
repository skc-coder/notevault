# CDS Percentage Q151

## Question
The price of an article is first increased by $20\%$ and later on the price was decreased by $25\%$ due to reduction in sales. Find the net percentage change in final price of the article.
- (a) $20\%$ decrease
- (b) $10\%$ decrease
- (c) $30\%$ increase
- (d) $15\%$ decrease

---

## Detailed Solution

### Method 1: Using Net Percentage Change Formula
Using [Net Percentage Change Formula](/cds/math/notes/subtopics/successive_percentage):
$$\text{Net Change \%} = a + b + \frac{a \cdot b}{100}$$

Where:
- $a = +20\%$ (increase)
- $b = -25\%$ (decrease)

Substituting values:
$$\text{Net Change \%} = 20 + (-25) + \frac{20 \times (-25)}{100}$$
$$\text{Net Change \%} = -5 + \frac{-500}{100} = -5 - 5 = -10\%$$

Since the sign is negative, it represents a net decrease of $10\%$.

---

## Correct Answer
**(b) $10\%$ decrease**
