# CDS Practice Question 38

## Question Statement
$A$ is thrice as efficient as $B$ and hence completes a work in $40\text{ days}$ less than the number of days taken by $B$. What will be the number of days taken by both of them when working together?

- (a) $22.5\text{ days}$
- (b) $15\text{ days}$
- (c) $20\text{ days}$
- (d) $18\text{ days}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

1. **Formulate Efficiency and Days Invariant**:
   Since efficiency $E$ is inversely proportional to time $D$ taken for a constant work:
   $$\frac{E_A}{E_B} = \frac{3}{1} \implies \frac{D_A}{D_B} = \frac{1}{3}$$

2. **Set up Equations for Days**:
   Let $D_A = x$ days and $D_B = 3x$ days.
   Given that $A$ completes the work in $40\text{ days}$ less than $B$:
   $$D_B - D_A = 40$$
   $$3x - x = 40 \implies 2x = 40 \implies x = 20$$

3. **Find Individual Days**:
   - $D_A = 20\text{ days}$
   - $D_B = 60\text{ days}$

4. **Calculate Combined Days Working Together**:
   $$D_{A+B} = \frac{D_A \cdot D_B}{D_A + D_B} = \frac{20 \times 60}{20 + 60} = \frac{1200}{80} = 15\text{ days}$$

- **Correct Option**: **(b) 15 days**
