# CDS Practice Q14

## Question
What is $\log(\tan 1^\circ) + \log(\tan 2^\circ) + \log(\tan 3^\circ) + \dots + \log(\tan 89^\circ)$ equal to?

- (a) $0$
- (b) $1$
- (c) $2$
- (d) $-1$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Use Logarithmic Sum Identity
Using $\log a + \log b = \log(ab)$:
$$S = \log(\tan 1^\circ \cdot \tan 2^\circ \cdot \tan 3^\circ \dots \tan 89^\circ)$$

### Step 2: Apply Complementary Angle Property
Recall $\tan(90^\circ - \theta) = \cot\theta$, so:
$$\tan 89^\circ = \cot 1^\circ$$
$$\tan 88^\circ = \cot 2^\circ$$
$$\dots$$
$$\tan 46^\circ = \cot 44^\circ$$

Pairs simplify as:
$$\tan 1^\circ \cdot \tan 89^\circ = \tan 1^\circ \cdot \cot 1^\circ = 1$$
$$\tan 2^\circ \cdot \tan 88^\circ = 1$$
$$\dots$$
$$\tan 44^\circ \cdot \tan 46^\circ = 1$$
Middle unpaired term: $\tan 45^\circ = 1$.

### Step 3: Compute Overall Product & Logarithm
$$\text{Product inside log} = 1 \times 1 \times \dots \times 1 = 1$$
$$S = \log(1) = 0$$

---

## Key Takeaway & Atomic Theorem Reference
- Complementary pair property: $\tan\theta \cdot \tan(90^\circ - \theta) = 1$.
- Reference Note: [Fundamental Identities & Ratios](/cds/math/notes/subtopics/trig_identities)

**Correct Answer**: **(a) $0$**
