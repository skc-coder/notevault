# CDS Pathfinder Q11.9

## Problem Statement

A discount series of $10\%$, $20\%$ and $40\%$ is equal to a single equivalent discount of:

A. $50\%$  
B. $60\%$  
C. $56.8\%$  
D. $70.28\%$  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. Multipliers Formula
- For successive discounts $d_1 = 10\%$, $d_2 = 20\%$, $d_3 = 40\%$:
  $$\text{Net Multiplier} = \left(1 - \frac{10}{100}\right) \times \left(1 - \frac{20}{100}\right) \times \left(1 - \frac{40}{100}\right)$$

### 2. Multiplier Evaluation
  $$\text{Net Multiplier} = 0.90 \times 0.80 \times 0.60 = 0.72 \times 0.60 = 0.432$$

### 3. Single Equivalent Discount ($D_{\text{eq}}$)
  $$D_{\text{eq}}\% = (1 - 0.432) \times 100\% = 0.568 \times 100\% = 56.8\%$$

---

## Correct Answer
**C. 56.8%**
