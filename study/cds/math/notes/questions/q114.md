# Question 114: Sum of Inserted Arithmetic Means

If 5 arithmetic means are inserted between 3 and 17, then what is the sum of these 5 inserted arithmetic means?

(a) 40  
(b) 50  
(c) 60  
(d) 100  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Shortcut Formula:**  
> The sum of $k$ arithmetic means inserted between two numbers $a$ and $b$ is given by:
> $$\text{Sum of } k \text{ AMs} = k \times \left(\frac{a + b}{2}\right)$$
> 
> **Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
> Here $a = 3$, $b = 17$, and $k = 5$.
> 
> $$\text{Sum} = 5 \times \left(\frac{3 + 17}{2}\right) = 5 \times \left(\frac{20}{2}\right) = 5 \times 10 = 50$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 50**
