# CDS Pathfinder Q11.4

## Problem Statement

A man sold two watches, each for ₹495. If he gained $10\%$ on one watch and suffered a loss of $10\%$ on the other, what is the loss or gain percentage in the transaction?

A. $1\%$ gain  
B. $1\%$ loss  
C. $\frac{100}{99}\%$ loss  
D. No gain no loss  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. Invariant Application
- When two articles are sold at equal selling prices ($\text{SP}_1 = \text{SP}_2$), with profit $x\%$ on one and loss $x\%$ on the other, the combined transaction **always results in a loss**.

### 2. Loss Percentage Formula
- Applying the standard invariant:
  $$\text{Loss } \% = \left( \frac{x}{10} \right)^2 \% = \left( \frac{10}{10} \right)^2 \% = 1\% \text{ loss}$$

### 3. Numerical Verification
- $\text{SP}_1 = 495 \Rightarrow \text{CP}_1 = \frac{495}{1.10} = \text{Rs. }450$.
- $\text{SP}_2 = 495 \Rightarrow \text{CP}_2 = \frac{495}{0.90} = \text{Rs. }550$.
- Total $\text{CP} = 450 + 550 = \text{Rs. }1000$.
- Total $\text{SP} = 495 + 495 = \text{Rs. }990$.
- $\text{Net Loss} = 1000 - 990 = \text{Rs. }10$.
- $\text{Loss } \% = \frac{10}{1000} \times 100\% = 1\%$.

---

## Correct Answer
**B. 1% loss**
