# Question 13: Large Exponent Modulo Prime (2^1000 mod 17)

What is the remainder when $2^{1000}$ is divided by 17?

(a) 1  
(b) 2  
(c) 8  
(d) 16  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> 1. **Check conditions for Fermat's Little Theorem**:
>    - Modulus $p = 17$ (17 is prime).
>    - Base $a = 2$, $\operatorname{GCD}(2, 17) = 1$.
> 2. **Apply FLT ($2^{17-1} = 2^{16} \equiv 1 \pmod{17}$)**.
> 3. **Reduce exponent $1000 \pmod{16}$**:
>    $$1000 = 16 \times 62 + 8 \implies 1000 \equiv 8 \pmod{16}$$
> 4. **Calculate $2^{1000} \equiv 2^8 \pmod{17}$**:
>    $$2^8 = 256$$
>    $$256 = 17 \times 15 + 1 \implies 256 \equiv \mathbf{1 \pmod{17}}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(a) 1**
