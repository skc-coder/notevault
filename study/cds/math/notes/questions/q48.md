# CDS Chapter 14 Q48

## Problem Statement
The remainder when $12x^3 - 13x^2 - 5x + 9$ is divided by $(3x + 2)$ is:
- (a) $1$
- (b) $2$
- (c) $3$
- (d) $0$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Apply Remainder Theorem
Let $P(x) = 12x^3 - 13x^2 - 5x + 9$.
To find the remainder when divided by $3x + 2$, set the divisor to zero:
$$3x + 2 = 0 \implies x = -\frac{2}{3}$$

According to the Remainder Theorem:
$$R = P\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)$$

### Step 2: Substitute $x = -2/3$ into $P(x)$
$$P\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) = 12\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)^3 - 13\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 - 5\left(-\frac{2}{3}\right) + 9$$

- Compute $(-2/3)^3 = -8/27$:
  $$12 \times \left(-\frac{8}{27}\right) = -\frac{96}{27} = -\frac{32}{9}$$

- Compute $(-2/3)^2 = 4/9$:
  $$-13 \times \left(\frac{4}{9}\right) = -\frac{52}{9}$$

- Compute $-5 \times (-2/3)$:
  $$+\frac{10}{3} = +\frac{30}{9}$$

- Convert constant $9$:
  $$+9 = +\frac{81}{9}$$

### Step 3: Sum the Terms Over Common Denominator 9
$$R = \frac{-32 - 52 + 30 + 81}{9}$$
$$R = \frac{-84 + 111}{9} = \frac{27}{9} = 3$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(c) 3**
