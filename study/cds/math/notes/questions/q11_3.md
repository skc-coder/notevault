# CDS Pathfinder Q11.3

## Problem Statement

Person $A$ sells a table costing ₹2000 to person $B$ earning a profit of $6\%$. Person $B$ sells it to another person $C$ at a loss of $5\%$. At what price did $B$ sell the table to $C$?

A. ₹2054  
B. ₹2050  
C. ₹2024  
D. ₹2014  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. Sequential Multipliers
- Cost price for $A$: $\text{CP}_A = \text{Rs. }2000$.
- Selling price by $A$ (which becomes Cost Price for $B$, $\text{CP}_B$):
  $$\text{CP}_B = \text{CP}_A \times \left(1 + \frac{6}{100}\right) = 2000 \times 1.06 = \text{Rs. }2120$$

### 2. Transaction from $B$ to $C$
- $B$ sells to $C$ at a loss of $5\%$:
  $$\text{SP}_B = \text{CP}_B \times \left(1 - \frac{5}{100}\right) = 2120 \times 0.95$$

- Calculating explicitly:
  $$\text{SP}_B = 2120 \times \frac{19}{20} = 106 \times 19 = \text{Rs. }2014$$

---

## Correct Answer
**D. ₹2014**
