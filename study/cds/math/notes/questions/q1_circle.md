# Q1: Perpendicular Distance from Centre to Chord

## Question
In a circle with centre $O$ and radius $r = 5\text{ cm}$, $AB$ is a chord of length $8\text{ cm}$. If $OM \perp AB$, what is the length of $OM$?

- (a) $2\text{ cm}$
- (b) $2.5\text{ cm}$
- (c) $3\text{ cm}$
- (d) $4\text{ cm}$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Apply Perpendicular Bisector Theorem
Since $OM \perp AB$ from centre $O$, $OM$ bisects chord $AB$:
$$AM = MB = \frac{AB}{2} = \frac{8}{2} = 4\text{ cm}$$

### Step 2: Use Pythagoras Theorem in Right-Angled Triangle $\Delta OMA$
In right-angled triangle $\Delta OMA$:
$$OA^2 = OM^2 + AM^2$$

Substitute known values ($OA = r = 5\text{ cm}$, $AM = 4\text{ cm}$):
$$5^2 = OM^2 + 4^2$$
$$25 = OM^2 + 16$$
$$OM^2 = 9 \implies OM = 3\text{ cm}$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(c) $3\text{ cm}$**
