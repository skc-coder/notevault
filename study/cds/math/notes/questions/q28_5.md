# CDS Practice Question 28.5

## Question
If in a moderately skewed frequency distribution, the mean and median are 20 and 21 respectively, then its mode is approximately:
- (a) 24
- (b) 23
- (c) 25
- (d) 22

---

## Detailed Solution & Derivation

### 1. Karl Pearson's Empirical Identity
$$\text{Mode} = 3(\text{Median}) - 2(\text{Mean})$$

### 2. Parameter Substitution
- Mean $\bar{x} = 20$
- Median $M_e = 21$

$$\text{Mode} = 3(21) - 2(20) = 63 - 40 = 23$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(b) 23**
