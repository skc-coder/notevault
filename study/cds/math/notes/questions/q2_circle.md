# Q2: Tangent Length & Secant Power of a Point

## Question
From an external point $P$, a tangent $PT$ and a secant $PAB$ are drawn to a circle. If $PT = 6\text{ cm}$ and $PA = 4\text{ cm}$, what is the length of chord $AB$?

- (a) $5\text{ cm}$
- (b) $4.5\text{ cm}$
- (c) $9\text{ cm}$
- (d) $5\text{ cm}$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Apply Secant-Tangent Theorem (Power of a Point)
By the Secant-Tangent Theorem for an external point $P$:
$$PT^2 = PA \cdot PB$$

### Step 2: Calculate Total Secant Length $PB$
Substitute $PT = 6\text{ cm}$ and $PA = 4\text{ cm}$:
$$6^2 = 4 \cdot PB$$
$$36 = 4 \cdot PB \implies PB = 9\text{ cm}$$

### Step 3: Find Chord Length $AB$
Since $P, A, B$ lie on a straight secant line ($P-A-B$):
$$PB = PA + AB$$
$$9 = 4 + AB \implies AB = 5\text{ cm}$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(a) $5\text{ cm}$**
