# Question 111: Geometric Progression Terms Relationship

If the 4th, 10th and 16th terms of a GP are $x, y$ and $z$ respectively, then $x, y, z$ are in:

(a) AP  
(b) GP  
(c) AGP  
(d) HP  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
> Let the first term of the GP be $a$ and common ratio be $r$.  
> 
> - 4th term: $T_4 = a r^3 = x$  
> - 10th term: $T_{10} = a r^9 = y$  
> - 16th term: $T_{16} = a r^{15} = z$  
> 
> **Checking Geometric Mean Relationship:**  
> Multiply $x$ and $z$:  
> $$x \cdot z = (a r^3) \cdot (a r^{15}) = a^2 r^{18}$$  
> $$x \cdot z = (a r^9)^2 = y^2$$  
> 
> Since $y^2 = x z$, $y$ is the Geometric Mean of $x$ and $z$.  
> Therefore, $x, y, z$ are in Geometric Progression (GP).  
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) GP**
