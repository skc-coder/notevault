# Question 24: HCF of Linear Expressions m = 2n + 1 and k = 9n + 4

For any integer $n$, what is the HCF of integers $m = 2n + 1$ and $k = 9n + 4$?

(a) 3  
(b) 1  
(c) 2  
(d) 4  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Euclidean Algorithm Elimination Method:
> We know that $\operatorname{HCF}(A, B) = \operatorname{HCF}(A, \, B - q A)$.
> 
> Let $g = \operatorname{HCF}(9n + 4, \, 2n + 1)$.
> 1. Eliminate $n$ by linear combination:
>    $$\text{Subtract } 4 \times (2n + 1) \text{ from } (9n + 4):$$
>    $$(9n + 4) - 4(2n + 1) = 9n + 4 - 8n - 4 = \mathbf{n}$$
> 2. Now $g = \operatorname{HCF}(2n + 1, \, n)$.
> 3. Eliminate $n$ again:
>    $$(2n + 1) - 2(n) = \mathbf{1}$$
> 
> $$\implies \operatorname{HCF}(2n + 1, 9n + 4) = \operatorname{HCF}(1, n) = \mathbf{1}$$
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Verification with Test Cases:
> - Put $n = 1 \implies m = 3, k = 13 \implies \operatorname{HCF}(3, 13) = \mathbf{1}$.
> - Put $n = 2 \implies m = 5, k = 22 \implies \operatorname{HCF}(5, 22) = \mathbf{1}$.
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 1**
