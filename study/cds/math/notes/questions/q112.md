# Question 112: AP, HP, GP Combined Transformation

If $a, b, c$ are in AP, $p, q, r$ are in HP, and $ap, bq, cr$ are in GP, then $\frac{p}{r} + \frac{r}{p}$ is equal to:

(a) $\frac{a}{c} - \frac{c}{a}$  
(b) $\frac{a}{c} + \frac{c}{a}$  
(c) $\frac{b}{q} + \frac{q}{b}$  
(d) $\frac{b}{q} - \frac{q}{b}$  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
> 1. Since $a, b, c$ are in AP:  
>    $$b = \frac{a + c}{2}$$  
> 2. Since $p, q, r$ are in HP:  
>    $$q = \frac{2pr}{p + r}$$  
> 3. Since $ap, bq, cr$ are in GP:  
>    $$(bq)^2 = (ap)(cr) \implies b^2 q^2 = ac pr$$  
> 
> Substitute expressions for $b$ and $q$:  
> $$\left(\frac{a+c}{2}\right)^2 \left(\frac{2pr}{p+r}\right)^2 = ac pr$$  
> $$\frac{(a+c)^2}{4} \cdot \frac{4 p^2 r^2}{(p+r)^2} = ac pr$$  
> $$\frac{(a+c)^2 (pr)}{(p+r)^2} = ac$$  
> $$\frac{(a+c)^2}{ac} = \frac{(p+r)^2}{pr}$$  
> 
> Expand both sides:  
> $$\frac{a^2 + 2ac + c^2}{ac} = \frac{p^2 + 2pr + r^2}{pr}$$  
> $$\frac{a}{c} + 2 + \frac{c}{a} = \frac{p}{r} + 2 + \frac{r}{p}$$  
> 
> Subtract 2 from both sides:  
> $$\frac{p}{r} + \frac{r}{p} = \frac{a}{c} + \frac{c}{a}$$  
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) $\frac{a}{c} + \frac{c}{a}$**
