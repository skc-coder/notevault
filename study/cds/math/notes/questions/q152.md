# CDS Percentage Q152

## Question
The population of a town is $352800$. If it increases at the rate of $5\%$ per annum:
1. What will be its population $2$ years hence?
2. What was its population $2$ years ago?

---

## Detailed Solution

### Part 1: Population 2 Years Hence ($P_{+2}$)
Using [Population Growth Formula](/cds/math/notes/subtopics/population_depreciation):
$$P_{+2} = P_0 \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^2$$

Given $P_0 = 352800$ and $R = 5$:
$$1 + \frac{5}{100} = \frac{21}{20}$$
$$P_{+2} = 352800 \times \left(\frac{21}{20}\right)^2 = 352800 \times \frac{441}{400}$$

Divide $352800$ by $400$:
$$\frac{352800}{400} = 882$$
$$P_{+2} = 882 \times 441 = 388962$$

### Part 2: Population 2 Years Ago ($P_{-2}$)
Using the inverse past formula:
$$P_{-2} = \frac{P_0}{\left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^2} = P_0 \times \left(\frac{20}{21}\right)^2$$
$$P_{-2} = 352800 \times \frac{400}{441}$$

Divide $352800$ by $441$:
$$\frac{352800}{441} = 800$$
$$P_{-2} = 800 \times 400 = 320000$$

---

## Final Result
- **Population 2 Years Hence**: $388,962$
- **Population 2 Years Ago**: $320,000$
