# Question 120: Sum of Consecutive Pair Products

What is the value of the sum $S = 1 \cdot 2 + 2 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot 4 + \dots + 10 \cdot 11$?

(a) 440  
(b) 385  
(c) 500  
(d) 480  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Shortcut Formula:**  
> The sum of products of 2 consecutive integers up to $n$ terms is given by:
> $$\sum_{k=1}^n k(k+1) = \frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{3}$$
> 
> **Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
> Here $n = 10$.
> 
> $$S = \frac{10 \times 11 \times 12}{3} = 10 \times 11 \times 4 = 440$$
> 
> **Alternative Derivation via Sigma Rules:**  
> $$T_k = k(k+1) = k^2 + k$$
> $$\sum T_k = \sum_{k=1}^{10} k^2 + \sum_{k=1}^{10} k = \frac{10 \times 11 \times 21}{6} + \frac{10 \times 11}{2}$$
> $$S = 385 + 55 = 440$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(a) 440**
