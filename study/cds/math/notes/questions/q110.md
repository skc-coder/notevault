# Question 110: Sum of 11 Terms of an Arithmetic Progression

Find the sum of 11 terms of the AP: $-7, -2, 3, 8, \dots$

(a) 200  
(b) 198  
(c) 326  
(d) 137  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
> - First term $a = -7$.  
> - Common difference $d = -2 - (-7) = 5$.  
> - Number of terms $n = 11$.  
> 
> **Applying AP Summation Formula:**  
> $$S_n = \frac{n}{2} [2a + (n-1)d]$$  
> 
> Substitute values:  
> $$S_{11} = \frac{11}{2} [2(-7) + (11-1)(5)]$$  
> $$S_{11} = \frac{11}{2} [-14 + 10 \times 5]$$  
> $$S_{11} = \frac{11}{2} [-14 + 50]$$  
> $$S_{11} = \frac{11}{2} \times 36 = 11 \times 18 = 198$$  
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 198**
