# CDS Practice Question 45

## Question
At what rate percent per annum simple interest will a sum of money double itself in 8 years?
- (a) $12\%$
- (b) $12\frac{1}{2}\%$
- (c) $13\%$
- (d) $15\%$

---

## Detailed Solution & Proof

- Let initial principal be $P$.
- Final maturity amount $A = 2P$.
- Simple interest earned:
  $$SI = A - P = 2P - P = P$$

- Time period $T = 8\text{ years}$.
- Apply $SI = \frac{P \cdot R \cdot T}{100}$:
  $$P = \frac{P \cdot R \cdot 8}{100}$$

- Divide both sides by $P$:
  $$1 = \frac{8R}{100}$$
  $$R = \frac{100}{8} = 12.5\% = 12\frac{1}{2}\%$$

**Correct Option**: **(b) $12\frac{1}{2}\%$**
