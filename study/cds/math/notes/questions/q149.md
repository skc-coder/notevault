# CDS Percentage Q149

## Question
If the salary of $X$ is $20\%$ more than the salary of $Y$, by what percentage is the salary of $Y$ less than that of $X$?
- (a) $25\%$
- (b) $20\%$
- (c) $16\frac{2}{3}\%$ ($\frac{50}{3}\%$)
- (d) $16\frac{1}{4}\%$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Method 1: Using Asymmetric Percentage Theorem
Using [Asymmetric Comparison Theorem](/cds/math/notes/subtopics/asymmetric_percentage):
If $A$ is $x\%$ more than $B$, then $B$ is less than $A$ by:
$$\left(\frac{x}{100 + x} \times 100\right)\%$$

Here $x = 20$:
$$\text{Percentage Less} = \left(\frac{20}{100 + 20} \times 100\right)\% = \left(\frac{20}{120} \times 100\right)\% = \frac{100}{6}\% = \frac{50}{3}\% = 16\frac{2}{3}\%$$

### Method 2: Direct Base Valuation
Let $Y$'s salary be $100$.
- $X$'s salary $= 100 + 20 = 120$.
- Absolute difference between $X$ and $Y = 120 - 100 = 20$.
- Percentage by which $Y$ is less than $X$ (taking $X$ as reference base):
  $$\text{Percentage Less} = \frac{20}{120} \times 100\% = \frac{50}{3}\%$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(c) $\frac{50}{3}\%$ (or $16\frac{2}{3}\%$)**
