# Question 25: Number of Possible Pair Sets Given Product & HCF (CDS 2014 II)

The HCF of two natural numbers $m$ and $n$ is 24 and their product is 552. How many sets of values of $m$ and $n$ are possible?

(a) 1  
(b) 2  
(c) 4  
(d) No set of $m$ and $n$ is possible satisfying the given conditions  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Concept: Co-prime Product Identity Check
> Let the two numbers be $m = 24x$ and $n = 24y$, where $\operatorname{GCD}(x, y) = 1$.
> 
> Product relation:
> $$m \cdot n = (24x)(24y) = 576xy$$
> 
> Given $m \cdot n = 552$:
> $$576 xy = 552 \implies xy = \frac{552}{576} < 1$$
> 
> Since $m$ and $n$ are natural numbers ($x, y \ge 1$), $xy$ MUST be a positive integer!  
> Since $552$ is not divisible by $24^2 = 576$ (in fact $552 < 576$), **no natural numbers $m$ and $n$ can exist**.
> 
> > 💡 **General Rule:** Product of two numbers MUST be divisible by $\operatorname{HCF}^2$! Here $552$ is not even divisible by $24^2 = 576$.
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(d) No set of $m$ and $n$ is possible satisfying the given conditions**
