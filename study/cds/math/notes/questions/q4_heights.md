# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q4

## Question Statement

A radio transmitter antenna of height $100\text{ m}$ stands at the top of a tall building. At a point on the ground, the angle of elevation of bottom of the antenna is $45^\circ$ and that of top of antenna is $60^\circ$. What is the height of the building?

- (a) $100\text{ m}$
- (b) $50\text{ m}$
- (c) $50(\sqrt{3} + 1)\text{ m}$
- (d) $25\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Conceptual Model
Let building height be $h$ and ground distance be $d$. Antenna height $p = 100\text{ m}$.
- Angle of elevation to bottom of antenna = $45^\circ$.
- Angle of elevation to top of antenna = $60^\circ$.

### 2. Equations
- Bottom of antenna:
  $$\tan 45^\circ = \frac{h}{d} \implies 1 = \frac{h}{d} \implies d = h$$
- Top of antenna:
  $$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{h + 100}{d}$$
  $$\sqrt{3} = \frac{h + 100}{h} \implies h\sqrt{3} = h + 100$$
  $$h(\sqrt{3} - 1) = 100 \implies h = \frac{100}{\sqrt{3} - 1}$$

### 3. Rationalize Denominator
$$h = \frac{100(\sqrt{3} + 1)}{(\sqrt{3} - 1)(\sqrt{3} + 1)} = \frac{100(\sqrt{3} + 1)}{3 - 1} = 50(\sqrt{3} + 1)\text{ m}$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(c) $50(\sqrt{3} + 1)\text{ m}$**
