# CDS Percentage Q148

## Question
If $x\%$ of $y$ is $13x$, then what is the value of $y$?
- (a) $880$
- (b) $1300$
- (c) $1200$
- (d) $700$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Write equation in percentage notation
$$x\% \text{ of } y = 13x$$
$$\frac{x}{100} \cdot y = 13x$$

### Step 2: Solve for $y$
Assuming $x \neq 0$, divide both sides by $x$:
$$\frac{y}{100} = 13$$

Multiply both sides by $100$:
$$y = 1300$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(b) 1300**
