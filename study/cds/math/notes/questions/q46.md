# CDS Practice Question 46

## Question
A certain sum at simple interest amounts to ₹1,586 in 2 years and to ₹1,729 in 3 years. Find the sum and the rate per cent per annum.
- (a) $8\%$, ₹1,200
- (b) $9\%$, ₹1,300
- (c) $10\%$, ₹1,000
- (d) $11\%$, ₹1,300

---

## Detailed Solution & Proof

- Given maturity amounts:
  - $A_1 = \text{Rs. }1,586$ at $t_1 = 2\text{ years}$
  - $A_2 = \text{Rs. }1,729$ at $t_2 = 3\text{ years}$

- Annual interest is linear:
  $$I_{\text{annual}} = \frac{A_2 - A_1}{t_2 - t_1} = \frac{1729 - 1586}{3 - 2} = \text{Rs. }143\text{ per year}$$

- Total interest earned in 2 years:
  $$SI_2 = 2 \times 143 = \text{Rs. }286$$

- Calculate Principal $P$:
  $$P = A_1 - SI_2 = 1586 - 286 = \text{Rs. }1,300$$

- Calculate Rate of interest $R\%$:
  $$R = \frac{SI_{\text{annual}}}{P} \times 100\% = \frac{143}{1300} \times 100\% = 11\%$$

**Correct Option**: **(d) 11%, ₹1,300**
