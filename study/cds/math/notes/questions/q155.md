# CDS Percentage Q155

## Question
A sample of $5\text{ L}$ of glycerine is found to be adulterated to the extent of $20\%$. How much pure glycerine should be added to bring down the percentage of impurity to $5\%$?
- (a) $10\text{ L}$
- (b) $25\text{ L}$
- (c) $15\text{ L}$
- (d) $20\text{ L}$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Calculate original volume of impurity
Initial mixture volume $V = 5\text{ L}$.
Impurity concentration $= 20\%$.
Volume of impurity:
$$\text{Impurity} = 5 \times \frac{20}{100} = 1\text{ L}$$

### Step 2: Set up equation with added pure glycerine
Let $x\text{ L}$ of pure glycerine be added.
Adding pure glycerine increases total volume to $(5 + x)\text{ L}$, but the volume of impurity remains constant at $1\text{ L}$.

We want the new impurity concentration to be $5\%$:
$$\frac{\text{Impurity}}{\text{New Total Volume}} = \frac{5}{100} = \frac{1}{20}$$
$$\frac{1}{5 + x} = \frac{1}{20}$$

### Step 3: Solve for $x$
$$5 + x = 20 \implies x = 15\text{ L}$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(c) $15\text{ L}$**
