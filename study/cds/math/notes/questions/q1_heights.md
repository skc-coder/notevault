# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q1

## Question Statement

The length of shadow of a tree is $16\text{ m}$ when the angle of elevation of the Sun is $60^\circ$. What is the height of the tree?

- (a) $8\text{ m}$
- (b) $16\text{ m}$
- (c) $16\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$
- (d) $3\text{ m}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Conceptual Setup & Diagram Model
Let the height of the tree be $h$ and the length of its shadow along the ground be $d = 16\text{ m}$. The angle of elevation of the Sun is $\theta = 60^\circ$.

### 2. Trigonometric Ratio
In the right-angled triangle formed by the tree, its shadow, and the Sun's ray:
$$\tan \theta = \frac{\text{Height of tree}}{\text{Length of shadow}}$$

### 3. Calculation
- Substitute $\theta = 60^\circ$ and shadow length $16\text{ m}$:
  $$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{h}{16}$$
- Since $\tan 60^\circ = \sqrt{3}$:
  $$\sqrt{3} = \frac{h}{16} \implies h = 16\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(c) $16\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$**
