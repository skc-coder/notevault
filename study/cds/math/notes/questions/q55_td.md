# CDS Practice Question 55

## Question
Walking at $3\text{ km/h}$, Rajeev reaches his school $15\text{ min}$ late. If he travels at $4\text{ km/h}$, he reaches $15\text{ min}$ earlier. The distance he has to travel is:
- (a) $4.5\text{ km}$
- (b) $6\text{ km}$
- (c) $7.2\text{ km}$
- (d) $12\text{ km}$

---

## Detailed Solution & Proof

### Step 1: Identify Early/Late Arrival Parameters
- Speed 1 ($x$) = $3\text{ km/h}$
- Speed 2 ($y$) = $4\text{ km/h}$
- $t_1 = 15\text{ min} = \frac{15}{60}\text{ h} = \frac{1}{4}\text{ h}$ (Late)
- $t_2 = 15\text{ min} = \frac{15}{60}\text{ h} = \frac{1}{4}\text{ h}$ (Early)

### Step 2: Apply Early/Late Arrival Formula
$$\text{Distance } D = \frac{x \cdot y}{y - x} \times (t_1 + t_2)$$

$$D = \frac{3 \times 4}{4 - 3} \times \left(\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{4}\right) = 12 \times \frac{1}{2} = 6\text{ km}$$

**Correct Option**: **(b) $6\text{ km}$**
