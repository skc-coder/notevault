# CDS Percentage Q146

## Question
If $90\%$ of $A = 30\%$ of $B$ and $B = x\%$ of $A$, find the value of $x$.
- (a) $700$
- (b) $600$
- (c) $300$
- (d) $1100$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Formulate equation from given relation
Convert percentages into fractional form:
$$\frac{90}{100} A = \frac{30}{100} B$$

Multiply both sides by $100$:
$$90 A = 30 B$$

Divide both sides by $30$:
$$B = 3A$$

### Step 2: Substitute $B = 3A$ into the second percentage definition
We are given $B = x\%$ of $A$:
$$B = \frac{x}{100} A$$

Substitute $B = 3A$:
$$3A = \frac{x}{100} A$$

Since $A \neq 0$, cancel $A$ from both sides:
$$3 = \frac{x}{100} \implies x = 300$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(c) 300**
