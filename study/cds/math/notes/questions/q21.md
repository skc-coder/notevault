# CDS Practice Question 21

## Question Statement
Ravi and Sneha working separately can finish a job in $8\text{ h}$ and $12\text{ h}$, respectively. If they work for an hour alternately, Ravi beginning at 9:00 am, when will the job be finished?

- (a) 7:30 pm
- (b) 7:00 pm
- (c) 6:30 pm
- (d) 6:00 pm

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

1. **Determine Individual Hourly Rates**:
   Let Total Work $W = \text{LCM}(8, 12) = 24\text{ units}$.
   - Ravi's hourly rate:
     $$E_R = \frac{24}{8} = 3\text{ units/h}$$
   - Sneha's hourly rate:
     $$E_S = \frac{24}{12} = 2\text{ units/h}$$

2. **Calculate Work per 2-Hour Alternating Cycle**:
   - Hour 1 (Ravi, 9:00 am - 10:00 am): $3\text{ units}$
   - Hour 2 (Sneha, 10:00 am - 11:00 am): $2\text{ units}$
   - Combined work in 1 cycle ($2\text{ h}$):
     $$W_{\text{cycle}} = 3 + 2 = 5\text{ units}$$

3. **Determine Completed Full Cycles**:
   Total work is $24\text{ units}$.
   Number of complete 2-hour cycles:
   $$\text{Cycles} = \lfloor \frac{24}{5} \rfloor = 4\text{ cycles}$$
   - Time elapsed in 4 cycles:
     $$T_{\text{cycles}} = 4 \times 2 = 8\text{ hours}$$
   - Work completed in 4 cycles:
     $$W_{\text{completed}} = 4 \times 5 = 20\text{ units}$$
   - Remaining work:
     $$W_{\text{rem}} = 24 - 20 = 4\text{ units}$$

4. **Process Remaining Work Step-by-Step**:
   - Hour 9 (Ravi's turn, 5:00 pm - 6:00 pm):
     Ravi does $3\text{ units}$ in 1 hour.
     Remaining work after Hour 9:
     $$W_{\text{rem2}} = 4 - 3 = 1\text{ unit}$$
   - Hour 10 (Sneha's turn, starting at 6:00 pm):
     Sneha's rate is $2\text{ units/h}$.
     Time needed for $1\text{ unit}$:
     $$t = \frac{1}{2}\text{ hour} = 30\text{ minutes}$$

5. **Calculate Final Clock Completion Time**:
   - Total time taken:
     $$T_{\text{total}} = 8\text{ h} + 1\text{ h} + 0.5\text{ h} = 9.5\text{ hours} = 9\text{ h } 30\text{ min}$$
   - Start time: 9:00 am.
   - Completion time:
     $$9:00\text{ am} + 9\text{ h } 30\text{ min} = 6:30\text{ pm}$$

- **Correct Option**: **(c) 6:30 pm**
