# CDS Pathfinder Q11.7

## Problem Statement

If selling price of 8 articles is equal to the cost price of 10 articles, then the percent gain or loss is:

A. $20\%$ gain  
B. $25\%$ gain  
C. $30\%$ loss  
D. $35\%$ gain  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. Equation Formulation
- Given:
  $$8 \cdot \text{SP} = 10 \cdot \text{CP}$$
- Form ratio of $\text{SP}$ to $\text{CP}$:
  $$\frac{\text{SP}}{\text{CP}} = \frac{10}{8} = \frac{5}{4}$$

### 2. Profit Calculation
- Since $\text{SP} > \text{CP}$, there is a profit.
- Profit ratio:
  $$\frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{CP}} = \frac{\text{SP} - \text{CP}}{\text{CP}} = \frac{5 - 4}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$$

### 3. Percentage Conversion
  $$\text{Gain } \% = \frac{1}{4} \times 100\% = 25\% \text{ gain}$$

---

## Correct Answer
**B. 25% gain**
