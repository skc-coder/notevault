# CDS Practice Q5

## Question
The angle between the minute hand and the hour hand of a clock when the time is $7:35\text{ PM}$ is:

- (a) $16^\circ 45'$
- (b) $17^\circ 30'$
- (c) $18^\circ 15'$
- (d) $19^\circ 30'$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Use Clock Angle Formula
For time $H:M$, the angle $\theta$ is given by:
$$\theta = |30H - 5.5M|^\circ$$

Here $H = 7$ and $M = 35$.

### Step 2: Calculate Positions
- Angle of Hour Hand from 12:
  $$\text{Angle}_{H} = 30 \times 7 + 0.5 \times 35 = 210^\circ + 17.5^\circ = 227.5^\circ$$
- Angle of Minute Hand from 12:
  $$\text{Angle}_{M} = 6 \times 35 = 210^\circ$$

### Step 3: Find Angle Difference
$$\theta = 227.5^\circ - 210^\circ = 17.5^\circ = 17^\circ 30'$$

---

## Key Takeaway & Atomic Theorem Reference
- Clock hand relative motion speed is $5.5^\circ/\text{min}$.
- Reference Note: [Angle Measurements & Clocks](/cds/math/notes/subtopics/angle_measurements)

**Correct Answer**: **(b) $17^\circ 30'$**
