# CDS Practice Q35: Polynomial Recovery from HCF and LCM

## Question Text

The HCF of two expressions is $3x^2 + 4x - 4$ and their LCM is $3x^4 + 4x^3 - 7x^2 - 4x + 4$.
Find the two original expressions.

**Options:**
- (a) $(x - 1)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$ and $(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$
- (b) $(x + 1)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$ and $(x + 2)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$
- (c) $(x + 2)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$ and $(x - 1)$
- (d) $(x + 1)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$ and $(x - 1)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$

---

## Detailed Mathematical Solution

### Step 1: Factorize HCF
$$\text{HCF} = 3x^2 + 4x - 4 = 3x^2 + 6x - 2x - 4 = 3x(x + 2) - 2(x + 2) = (3x - 2)(x + 2)$$

### Step 2: Divide LCM by HCF
Since $\text{LCM} = \text{HCF} \cdot a(x) \cdot b(x)$ where $\operatorname{gcd}(a(x), b(x)) = 1$:
$$\frac{\text{LCM}}{\text{HCF}} = \frac{3x^4 + 4x^3 - 7x^2 - 4x + 4}{3x^2 + 4x - 4}$$
Performing polynomial division:
$$3x^4 + 4x^3 - 7x^2 - 4x + 4 = x^2(3x^2 + 4x - 4) - 3x^2 - 4x + 4 = x^2(3x^2 + 4x - 4) - 1(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$$
$$\frac{\text{LCM}}{\text{HCF}} = x^2 - 1 = (x - 1)(x + 1)$$

### Step 3: Assign Co-Prime Factor Pair
The factors $a(x) = (x + 1)$ and $b(x) = (x - 1)$ are coprime.
Therefore, the two original expressions $P(x)$ and $Q(x)$ are:
$$P(x) = (x + 1) \cdot \text{HCF} = (x + 1)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$$
$$Q(x) = (x - 1) \cdot \text{HCF} = (x - 1)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$$

**Correct Answer**: **(d) $(x + 1)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$ and $(x - 1)(3x^2 + 4x - 4)$**

---

## Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy
Just like integer arithmetic $L / H = x \cdot y$ where $\operatorname{gcd}(x,y) = 1$, polynomial quotient $\frac{\operatorname{LCM}}{\operatorname{HCF}}$ splits into coprime algebraic multipliers $a(x)$ and $b(x)$!
