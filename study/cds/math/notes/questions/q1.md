# CDS 2024 Q1

## Question Statement
If $\frac{\sqrt{x + 5} + \sqrt{x - 16}}{\sqrt{x + 5} - \sqrt{x - 16}} = \frac{7}{3}$, find the value of $x$.

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Apply Componendo and Dividendo
Let $A = \sqrt{x + 5}$ and $B = \sqrt{x - 16}$. The equation is given as:
$$\frac{A + B}{A - B} = \frac{7}{3}$$

Applying C&D:
$$\frac{(A + B) + (A - B)}{(A + B) - (A - B)} = \frac{7 + 3}{7 - 3}$$

Simplifying both numerator and denominator:
$$\frac{2A}{2B} = \frac{10}{4} \implies \frac{A}{B} = \frac{5}{2}$$

Substituting back $A$ and $B$:
$$\frac{\sqrt{x + 5}}{\sqrt{x - 16}} = \frac{5}{2}$$

---

### Step 2: Square Both Sides & Solve for $x$
Squaring both sides to eliminate square roots:
$$\frac{x + 5}{x - 16} = \frac{25}{4}$$

Cross-multiplying:
$$4(x + 5) = 25(x - 16)$$
$$4x + 20 = 25x - 400$$
$$21x = 420 \implies x = 20$$

---

## Key Takeaway
Applying C&D directly collapses complicated symmetric sum/difference fractions of radical terms into simple mono-term ratios $\frac{A}{B}$, preventing lengthy quadratic expansions.
