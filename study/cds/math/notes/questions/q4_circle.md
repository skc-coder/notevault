# Q4: Cyclic Quadrilateral Exterior & Opposite Angles

## Question
$ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral. Side $AB$ is produced to point $E$. If $\angle CBE = 95^\circ$ and $\angle BAC = 40^\circ$, what is the measure of $\angle ACD$?

- (a) $45^\circ$
- (b) $55^\circ$
- (c) $65^\circ$
- (d) $75^\circ$

---

## Detailed Solution

### Step 1: Apply Exterior Angle Property of Cyclic Quadrilateral
In cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, when side $AB$ is produced to $E$, exterior angle $\angle CBE$ equals interior opposite angle $\angle ADC$:
$$\angle ADC = \angle CBE = 95^\circ$$

### Step 2: Analyze Triangle $\Delta ADC$
In $\Delta ADC$, angles are subtended by chords and arcs.
Alternatively, in cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$:
$$\angle ADC + \angle ABC = 180^\circ$$
$$\angle ABC = 180^\circ - 95^\circ = 85^\circ$$

In $\Delta ABC$:
$$\angle ACB = 180^\circ - (\angle BAC + \angle ABC) = 180^\circ - (40^\circ + 85^\circ) = 55^\circ$$

Also, $\angle BCD = 180^\circ - \angle BAD$.
Since $\angle ADC = 95^\circ$, in $\Delta ADC$, $\angle ACD + \angle CAD = 180^\circ - 95^\circ = 85^\circ$.
Angles in same segment: $\angle CAD = \angle CBD$.
Using interior opposite angle sum:
$$\angle ACD = \angle ADC - \angle BAC = 95^\circ - 40^\circ = 55^\circ$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(b) $55^\circ$**
