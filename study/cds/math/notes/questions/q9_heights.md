# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q9

## Question Statement

A vertical tower stands on a horizontal plane and is surmounted by a vertical flagstaff of height $h$. At a point on the plane, the angle of elevation of the bottom of the flagstaff is $\alpha$ and that of the top of the flagstaff is $\beta$. Then, the height of the tower is

- (a) $\frac{h \tan \beta}{\tan \alpha - \tan \beta}$
- (b) $\frac{h \tan \alpha}{\tan \alpha + \tan \beta}$
- (c) $\frac{h \tan \beta}{\tan \alpha + \tan \beta}$
- (d) $\frac{h \tan \alpha}{\tan \beta - \tan \alpha}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Model Definitions
Let tower height be $H$ and ground distance be $x$. Flagstaff height $= h$.
- Elevation to bottom of flagstaff (top of tower) $= \alpha$:
  $$\tan \alpha = \frac{H}{x} \implies x = \frac{H}{\tan \alpha}$$
- Elevation to top of flagstaff $= \beta$:
  $$\tan \beta = \frac{H + h}{x} \implies x = \frac{H + h}{\tan \beta}$$

### 2. Algebraic Solution for $H$
$$\frac{H}{\tan \alpha} = \frac{H + h}{\tan \beta}$$
$$H \tan \beta = H \tan \alpha + h \tan \alpha$$
$$H(\tan \beta - \tan \alpha) = h \tan \alpha \implies H = \frac{h \tan \alpha}{\tan \beta - \tan \alpha}$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(d) $\frac{h \tan \alpha}{\tan \beta - \tan \alpha}$**
