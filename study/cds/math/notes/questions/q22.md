# Question 22: Circular Track Concurrency Meeting Time

John, Kate, and Smith at the same time, same point, and in the same direction run around a circular track in 250 seconds, 300 seconds, and 150 seconds respectively. Find after what time will they meet again?

(a) 30 min  
(b) 25 min  
(c) 20 min  
(d) 15 min  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### 💡 Why is this Direct LCM and NOT Type 3 Relative Speed?
> 
> 1. **Question Phrasing**:  
>    The question explicitly states: *"start at the same time, **same point**... Find after what time will they meet again?"*  
>    In standard Indian competitive exam phrasing (Pathfinder / RS Aggarwal / CDS), when a problem asks *"when will they meet again"* without providing a track length $L$, it **implicitly means meeting again at the starting point**!
> 
> 2. **What if we tried Type 3 (Meeting Anywhere)?**
>    Let track length be $L$.
>    - $S_A = L / 250$, $S_B = L / 300$, $S_C = L / 150$.
>    - Relative speed $(A, B) = S_A - S_B = \frac{L}{250} - \frac{L}{300} = \frac{L}{1500} \implies t_{AB} = \mathbf{1500\text{ s}}$.
>    - Relative speed $(B, C) = S_C - S_B = \frac{L}{150} - \frac{L}{300} = \frac{L}{300} \implies t_{BC} = \mathbf{300\text{ s}}$.
>    - $\operatorname{LCM}(t_{AB}, t_{BC}) = \operatorname{LCM}(1500, 300) = \mathbf{1500\text{ seconds} = 25\text{ minutes}}$.
> 
> Notice that for these specific numbers, **the first place all three runners meet anywhere IS AT THE STARTING POINT** after 1500 seconds (25 minutes)!
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 25 min**
