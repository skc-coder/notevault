# Question 113: Ratio of m-th Terms from Ratio of Sums of Two APs

The ratio of the sum of $n$ terms of two arithmetic progressions is $(7n + 1) : (4n + 27)$. Find the ratio of their 11th terms.

(a) 2 : 1  
(b) 4 : 3  
(c) 3 : 2  
(d) 5 : 4  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Shortcut / Key Invariant:**  
> To find the ratio of $m$-th terms $\frac{T_m^{(1)}}{T_m^{(2)}}$ when given the ratio of sums $\frac{S_n^{(1)}}{S_n^{(2)}} = \frac{f(n)}{g(n)}$, substitute:
> $$n = 2m - 1$$
> 
> **Step-by-Step Derivation:**  
> For 11th term ($m = 11$):
> $$n = 2(11) - 1 = 21$$
> 
> Substitute $n = 21$ into the given ratio:
> $$\frac{T_{11}^{(1)}}{T_{11}^{(2)}} = \frac{7(21) + 1}{4(21) + 27} = \frac{147 + 1}{84 + 27} = \frac{148}{111}$$
> 
> Divide numerator and denominator by 37:
> $$\frac{148}{111} = \frac{4 \times 37}{3 \times 37} = \frac{4}{3}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) 4 : 3**
