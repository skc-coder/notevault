# CDS Pathfinder Q11.6

## Problem Statement

A dishonest dealer professes to sell his goods at cost price but uses a false weight and thus gains $25\%$. For a kilogram ($1000\text{ g}$), what weight does he use?

A. $700\text{ g}$  
B. $750\text{ g}$  
C. $800\text{ g}$  
D. $850\text{ g}$  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. Invariant Formula
- Let the false weight used for $1000\text{ g}$ be $W_{\text{false}}$.
- Profit Percentage formula:
  $$\text{Gain } \% = \left( \frac{\text{True Weight} - \text{False Weight}}{\text{False Weight}} \right) \times 100\%$$

### 2. Algebraic Substitution
- Given $\text{Gain } \% = 25\%$ and $\text{True Weight} = 1000\text{ g}$:
  $$25 = \left( \frac{1000 - W_{\text{false}}}{W_{\text{false}}} \right) \times 100$$
  $$\frac{25}{100} = \frac{1}{4} = \frac{1000 - W_{\text{false}}}{W_{\text{false}}}$$

### 3. Solving for False Weight
- Cross-multiplying:
  $$W_{\text{false}} = 4000 - 4 W_{\text{false}}$$
  $$5 W_{\text{false}} = 4000$$
  $$W_{\text{false}} = 800\text{ g}$$

---

## Correct Answer
**C. 800 g**
