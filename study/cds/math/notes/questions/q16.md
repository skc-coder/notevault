# Question 16: HCF of 3 Numbers via Long Division (204, 1190, 1445)

What is the HCF of 204, 1190, and 1445?

(a) 85  
(b) 15  
(c) 17  
(d) 75  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Step 1: Find HCF of any two numbers (1190 and 1445) using Long Division
> 1. $1445 \div 1190 = 1$ remainder $255$  
>    ($1445 = 1190 \times 1 + 255$)
> 2. Divide previous divisor 1190 by remainder 255:  
>    $1190 \div 255 = 4$ remainder $170$  
>    ($1190 = 255 \times 4 + 170$)
> 3. Divide previous divisor 255 by remainder 170:  
>    $255 \div 170 = 1$ remainder $85$  
>    ($255 = 170 \times 1 + 85$)
> 4. Divide previous divisor 170 by remainder 85:  
>    $170 \div 85 = 2$ remainder $0$  
>    ($170 = 85 \times 2 + 0$)
> 
> $\implies \mathbf{\operatorname{HCF}(1190, 1445) = 85}$.
> 
> ---
> 
> ### Step 2: Find HCF of intermediate result (85) and the 3rd number (204)
> 1. Divide 204 by 85:  
>    $204 \div 85 = 2$ remainder $34$  
>    ($204 = 85 \times 2 + 34$)
> 2. Divide previous divisor 85 by remainder 34:  
>    $85 \div 34 = 2$ remainder $17$  
>    ($85 = 34 \times 2 + 17$)
> 3. Divide previous divisor 34 by remainder 17:  
>    $34 \div 17 = 2$ remainder $0$  
>    ($34 = 17 \times 2 + 0$)
> 
> $\implies \mathbf{\operatorname{HCF}(85, 204) = 17}$.
> 
> **Final Answer:** **(c) 17**
