# CDS Pathfinder Q11.2

## Problem Statement

By selling an article for ₹110, a man loses $12\%$. For how much should he sell it to gain $8\%$?

A. ₹120  
B. ₹125  
C. ₹135  
D. ₹140  

---

## Detailed Solution & Step-by-Step Derivation

### 1. Multiplier Invariant Analysis
- Let the Cost Price of the article be $\text{CP}$.
- When sold at a loss of $12\%$:
  $$\text{SP}_1 = \text{CP} \times \frac{100 - 12}{100} = \text{CP} \times \frac{88}{100} = \text{Rs. }110$$

### 2. Direct Ratio Scale
- We need the target selling price $\text{SP}_2$ to earn a gain of $8\%$:
  $$\text{SP}_2 = \text{CP} \times \frac{100 + 8}{100} = \text{CP} \times \frac{108}{100}$$

### 3. Ratio Cross-Multiplication
- Dividing $\text{SP}_2$ by $\text{SP}_1$:
  $$\frac{\text{SP}_2}{\text{SP}_1} = \frac{108}{88}$$
  $$\text{SP}_2 = 110 \times \frac{108}{88} = 110 \times \frac{27}{22} = 5 \times 27 = \text{Rs. }135$$

---

## Correct Answer
**C. ₹135**
