# Question 28: Algebraic Identities of HCF & LCM (CDS 2016 I)

Consider the following in respect of natural numbers $a, b,$ and $c$:
I. $\operatorname{LCM}(ab, ac) = a \cdot \operatorname{LCM}(b, c)$  
II. $\operatorname{HCF}(ab, ac) = a \cdot \operatorname{HCF}(b, c)$  
III. $\operatorname{HCF}(a, b) < \operatorname{LCM}(a, b)$  
IV. $\operatorname{HCF}(a, b)$ divides $\operatorname{LCM}(a, b)$  

Which of the above statement(s) is/are correct?

(a) III and II  
(b) III and IV  
(c) I, II and IV  
(d) All of these  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> ### Statement Analysis:
> 1. **Statement I ($\operatorname{LCM}(ab, ac) = a \cdot \operatorname{LCM}(b, c)$)**:  
>    **TRUE.** (Homogeneity property of LCM. Factoring out scalar $a$ scales the LCM by $a$).
> 2. **Statement II ($\operatorname{HCF}(ab, ac) = a \cdot \operatorname{HCF}(b, c)$)**:  
>    **TRUE.** (Distributive property of HCF over scalar multiplication).
> 3. **Statement III ($\operatorname{HCF}(a, b) < \operatorname{LCM}(a, b)$)**:  
>    **FALSE!** If $a = b$ (e.g. $a=5, b=5$), $\operatorname{HCF}(5,5) = 5$ and $\operatorname{LCM}(5,5) = 5$. Thus $\operatorname{HCF} = \operatorname{LCM}$, not strictly $<$. (The correct weak inequality is $\operatorname{HCF} \le \operatorname{LCM}$).
> 4. **Statement IV ($\operatorname{HCF}(a, b)$ divides $\operatorname{LCM}(a, b)$)**:  
>    **TRUE.** HCF always divides LCM for any pair of natural numbers because $L = H \cdot x \cdot y$.
> 
> Statements **I, II, and IV** are strictly correct.
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(c) I, II and IV**
