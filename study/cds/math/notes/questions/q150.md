# CDS Percentage Q150

## Question
If the price of cooking gas rises by $15\%$, by what percentage should a family reduce its consumption so as not to exceed the budget on cooking gas?
- (a) $12\frac{1}{23}\%$
- (b) $13\frac{1}{23}\%$
- (c) $14\frac{1}{23}\%$
- (d) None of these

---

## Detailed Solution

### Method 1: Applying Price-Consumption Invariance Formula
Using [Price-Consumption Balancing Formula](/cds/math/notes/subtopics/asymmetric_percentage):
$$\text{Reduction \%} = \left(\frac{x}{100 + x} \times 100\right)\%$$

Given price increase rate $x = 15$:
$$\text{Reduction \%} = \left(\frac{15}{100 + 15} \times 100\right)\% = \left(\frac{15}{115} \times 100\right)\%$$

Divide numerator and denominator by $5$:
$$\text{Reduction \%} = \frac{3 \times 100}{23}\% = \frac{300}{23}\%$$

Performing mixed fraction division:
$$300 = 23 \times 13 + 1 \implies \frac{300}{23}\% = 13\frac{1}{23}\%$$

---

## Correct Answer
**(b) $13\frac{1}{23}\%$**
