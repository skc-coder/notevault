# CDS Practice Q30: Multi-Polynomial Numerical & Variable HCF

## Question Text

What is the HCF of the three polynomials?
$$P(x) = 36(3x^4 + 5x^3 - 2x^2)$$
$$Q(x) = 9(6x^3 + 4x^2 - 2x)$$
$$R(x) = 54(27x^4 - x)$$

**Options:**
- (a) $9x(x + 1)$
- (b) $9x(3x - 1)$
- (c) $18x(3x - 1)$
- (d) $18x(x + 1)$

---

## Detailed Mathematical Solution

### Step 1: Factorize Each Polynomial Completely

#### Polynomial 1: $P(x)$
Factor out $x^2$:
$$P(x) = 36x^2(3x^2 + 5x - 2)$$
Split middle term $5x = 6x - x$:
$$P(x) = 36x^2(3x - 1)(x + 2)$$

#### Polynomial 2: $Q(x)$
Factor out $2x$ from inner expression:
$$Q(x) = 9 \cdot 2x(3x^2 + 2x - 1)$$
$$Q(x) = 18x(3x^2 + 2x - 1)$$
Split middle term $2x = 3x - x$:
$$Q(x) = 18x(3x - 1)(x + 1)$$

#### Polynomial 3: $R(x)$
Factor out $x$:
$$R(x) = 54x(27x^3 - 1)$$
Apply cubic difference identity $a^3 - b^3 = (a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2)$:
$$R(x) = 54x(3x - 1)(9x^2 + 3x + 1)$$

---

### Step 2: Extract Highest Common Factors

1. **Numerical Scalar Factors**:
   $$\operatorname{gcd}(36, 18, 54) = 18$$

2. **Common Monomial Factors ($x^k$)**:
   $$\min(x^2, x, x) = x$$

3. **Common Polynomial Factors**:
   $$\text{Common linear factor} = (3x - 1)$$

Combining all three parts:
$$\operatorname{HCF}(P, Q, R) = 18x(3x - 1)$$

**Correct Answer**: **(c) $18x(3x - 1)$**

---

## Key Takeaway & Exam Strategy
Extract scalar factors and variable powers ($x^k$) first! Do not forget to factor out numeric common terms inside parentheses (such as $2$ in $6x^2+4x-2$).
