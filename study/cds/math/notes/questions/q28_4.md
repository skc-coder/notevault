# CDS Practice Question 28.4

## Question
Calculate the mode of the following distribution:

| Class Interval | Frequency ($f$) |
| :--- | :--- |
| 0 – 10 | 5 |
| 10 – 20 | 8 |
| 20 – 30 | 7 |
| 30 – 40 | 12 |
| 40 – 50 | 28 |
| 50 – 60 | 20 |
| 60 – 70 | 10 |
| 70 – 80 | 10 |

- (a) 46.00
- (b) 46.67
- (c) 48.33
- (d) 50.00

---

## Detailed Solution & Derivation

### 1. Identifying Modal Class
- Maximum frequency is **28**.
- Thus, the **Modal Class** is **40 – 50**.

### 2. Identifying Parameters
- Lower limit of modal class $l = 40$
- Frequency of modal class $f_1 = 28$
- Preceding frequency $f_0 = 12$
- Succeeding frequency $f_2 = 20$
- Class size $h = 10$

### 3. Applying Continuous Mode Formula
$$M_o = l + \left( \frac{f_1 - f_0}{2f_1 - f_0 - f_2} \right) \cdot h$$
$$M_o = 40 + \left( \frac{28 - 12}{2(28) - 12 - 20} \right) \times 10$$
$$M_o = 40 + \left( \frac{16}{56 - 32} \right) \times 10$$
$$M_o = 40 + \left( \frac{16}{24} \right) \times 10 = 40 + \frac{2}{3} \times 10 = 40 + 6.6667 = 46.67$$

---

## Final Answer
- **Correct Option**: **(b) 46.67**
