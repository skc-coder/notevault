# Question 117: Sum of Telescoping Fractional Series

What is the sum of the first 20 terms of the series $\frac{1}{1 \times 2} + \frac{1}{2 \times 3} + \frac{1}{3 \times 4} + \dots$?

(a) $\frac{19}{20}$  
(b) $\frac{20}{21}$  
(c) $\frac{21}{22}$  
(d) $\frac{1}{21}$  

> [!faq]- View Solution
> **Telescoping Method / Partial Fractions:**  
> The $k$-th term of the series can be split as:
> $$T_k = \frac{1}{k(k+1)} = \frac{1}{k} - \frac{1}{k+1}$$
> 
> **Sum of 20 Terms:**  
> $$S_{20} = \left(1 - \frac{1}{2}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\right) + \left(\frac{1}{3} - \frac{1}{4}\right) + \dots + \left(\frac{1}{20} - \frac{1}{21}\right)$$
> 
> All intermediate terms cancel out:
> $$S_{20} = 1 - \frac{1}{21} = \frac{20}{21}$$
> 
> **General Shortcut Formula:**  
> $$\frac{1}{1 \cdot 2} + \frac{1}{2 \cdot 3} + \dots + \frac{1}{n(n+1)} = \frac{n}{n+1}$$
> 
> **Correct Answer:** **(b) $\frac{20}{21}$**
