# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q6

## Question Statement

The angle of elevation of a moon when the length of the shadow of a pole is equal to its height is

- (a) $60^\circ$
- (b) $45^\circ$
- (c) $90^\circ$
- (d) $30^\circ$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Equality Ratio
Let pole height be $h$ and shadow length be $s$. Given $s = h$.

### 2. Trigonometric Angle
$$\tan \theta = \frac{h}{s} = \frac{h}{h} = 1$$
$$\theta = \arctan(1) = 45^\circ$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(b) $45^\circ$**
