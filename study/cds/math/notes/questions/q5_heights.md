# CDS Pathfinder Heights & Distances Q5

## Question Statement

The shadow of a tower is $15\text{ m}$ when the Sun's altitude is $30^\circ$. What is the length of the shadow when the Sun's altitude is $60^\circ$?

- (a) $3\text{ m}$
- (b) $4\text{ m}$
- (c) $5\text{ m}$
- (d) $6\text{ m}$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### 1. Tower Height Calculation
Let tower height be $h$. When Sun's altitude $\theta_1 = 30^\circ$, shadow length $s_1 = 15\text{ m}$:
$$\tan 30^\circ = \frac{h}{15} \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{h}{15} \implies h = \frac{15}{\sqrt{3}} = 5\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$

### 2. New Shadow Length
When Sun's altitude becomes $\theta_2 = 60^\circ$, let shadow length be $s_2$:
$$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{h}{s_2}$$
$$\sqrt{3} = \frac{5\sqrt{3}}{s_2} \implies s_2 = 5\text{ m}$$

---

## Correct Answer

**(c) $5\text{ m}$**
