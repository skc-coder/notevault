# CDS Practice Q4

## Question
A railway train is moving on a circular curve of radius $1500\text{ m}$ at a speed of $90\text{ km/h}$. Through what angle (in degrees/minutes) has it turned in $11\text{ seconds}$?

- (a) $12^\circ$
- (b) $16^\circ 30'$
- (c) $10^\circ 30'$
- (d) $11^\circ 40'$

---

## Detailed Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Convert Speed to m/s
Speed $v = 90\text{ km/h}$.
$$v = 90 \times \frac{5}{18} = 25\text{ m/s}$$

### Step 2: Calculate Distance Covered (Arc Length $l$)
Time $t = 11\text{ s}$.
$$l = v \times t = 25 \times 11 = 275\text{ m}$$

### Step 3: Calculate Angle in Radians ($\theta = l/r$)
Radius $r = 1500\text{ m}$.
$$\theta = \frac{l}{r} = \frac{275}{1500} = \frac{11}{60}\text{ radians}$$

### Step 4: Convert Radians to Degrees and Minutes
Multiply by $\frac{180^\circ}{\pi}$ (using $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$):
$$\theta = \frac{11}{60} \times \frac{180}{\pi} = \frac{11}{60} \times 180 \times \frac{7}{22}$$
$$\theta = \frac{11 \times 3 \times 7}{22} = \frac{3 \times 7}{2} = \frac{21^\circ}{2} = 10.5^\circ = 10^\circ 30'$$

---

## Key Takeaway & Atomic Theorem Reference
- Circular arc formula:
  $$l = r\theta \quad (\theta \text{ in radians})$$
- Reference Note: [Angle Measurements & Clocks](/cds/math/notes/subtopics/angle_measurements)

**Correct Answer**: **(c) $10^\circ 30'$**
