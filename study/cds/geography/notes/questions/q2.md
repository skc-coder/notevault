# CAPF 2024 Q2

## Question Statement
In the Sun-Earth system, the distance between Lagrange points $L_2$ and $L_3$ is about? (CAPF 2024)

- **(a)** 15 lakh km
- **(b)** 30 lakh km
- **(c)** 16 crore km
- **(d)** 32 crore km

---

## Direct Answer & Core Logic

**Correct Option: (d) 32 crore km**

### First-Principles Derivation & Mathematical Intuition
1. **Definition of Lagrange Points**:
   - Points of gravitational equilibrium in a two-body system (Sun & Earth).
   - $L_1$ lies between Sun and Earth, $\approx 1.5 \times 10^6 \text{ km} = 15 \text{ lakh km}$ from Earth towards the Sun.
   - $L_2$ lies directly behind Earth (opposite side from Sun), $\approx 1.5 \times 10^6 \text{ km} = 15 \text{ lakh km}$ behind Earth.
   - $L_3$ lies on the exact opposite side of the Sun behind the Sun!

2. **Distance Calculation**:
   - Distance from Earth to Sun $= 1 \text{ AU} \approx 1.496 \times 10^8 \text{ km} \approx 15 \text{ crore km}$.
   - Distance from Sun to $L_3 \approx 1 \text{ AU} \approx 15 \text{ crore km}$.
   - Position of $L_2$ relative to Sun $= 1 \text{ AU} + 0.01 \text{ AU} = 1.01 \text{ AU}$.
   - Position of $L_3$ relative to Sun $= -1.00 \text{ AU}$.
   - Total Distance between $L_2$ and $L_3$:
     $$\text{Distance}(L_2, L_3) = 1.01 \text{ AU} - (-1.00 \text{ AU}) = 2.01 \text{ AU}$$
   - Converting $2.01 \text{ AU}$ to kilometers:
     $$\text{Distance} \approx 2 \times 1.5 \times 10^8 \text{ km} = 3.0 \times 10^8 \text{ km} = 30 \text{ crore km} \text{ to } 32 \text{ crore km}$$

> [!IMPORTANT] **Exam Trap / Quick Mental Math Rule**:
> - Distance $L_1$ to Earth $= 15 \text{ lakh km}$ ($0.01 \text{ AU}$).
> - Distance $L_2$ to Earth $= 15 \text{ lakh km}$ ($0.01 \text{ AU}$).
> - Distance $L_2$ to $L_3 = 2 \text{ AU} \approx 2 \times 16 \text{ crore km} = 32 \text{ crore km}$!
