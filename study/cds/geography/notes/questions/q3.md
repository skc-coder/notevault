# Copernicus Heliocentric Theory (NDA I 2023)

## Question Statement (NDA I 2023)
Which one of the following astronomers proved that the Earth and other planets revolve around the Sun?

(a) Copernicus (b) Kepler (c) Galileo (d) Newton

---

## Solution & Intuition

**Correct Answer:** (a) Nicolaus Copernicus

### Key Concept Breakdown
Nicolaus Copernicus published 'De revolutionibus orbium coelestium' in 1543 proposing the Heliocentric model (Sun at the center). Kepler later proved planetary orbits are elliptical, and Galileo provided observational proof via Jupiter's moons.
