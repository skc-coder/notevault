# Cirque vs Fjords Landform Classification (CDS 2 2025)

## Question Statement (CDS 2 2025)
Which one of the following is an erosional glacial landform shaped like an armchair or bowl?

(a) Cirque (b) Moraine (c) Esker (d) Drumlin

---

## Solution & Intuition

**Correct Answer:** (a) Cirque

### Key Concept Breakdown
Cirques are arm-chair shaped steep-sided depressions carved out by mountain glaciers. Moraines, Eskers, and Drumlins are depositional glacial landforms.
