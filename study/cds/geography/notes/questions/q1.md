# CAPF 2019 Q1

## Question Statement
Which one of the following represents the inner planets? (CAPF 2019)

- **(a)** Planets between the Sun and the Earth
- **(b)** Planets between the Sun and the Belt of Asteroids
- **(c)** Planets in gaseous state
- **(d)** Planets without satellites

---

## Direct Answer & Core Logic

**Correct Option: (b) Planets between the Sun and the Belt of Asteroids**

### Explanation & Intuition
- The Solar System is divided into two distinct zones separated by the **Asteroid Belt** (located between Mars and Jupiter).
- **Inner Planets (Terrestrial Planets)**: Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars. They are located inside the Asteroid Belt, closer to the Sun. They are dense, rocky, metallic, and have high densities.
- **Outer Planets (Jovian / Gas Giants)**: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. They lie outside the Asteroid Belt and are predominantly gaseous/ice compositions with lower densities.

> [!WARNING] **Trap / Distractor Analysis**:
> - Option (a) refers specifically to *Inferior Planets* (Mercury & Venus), NOT Inner Planets!
> - Option (c) describes Jovian / Outer planets, not inner planets.
> - Option (d) is incorrect because Earth has 1 satellite (Moon) and Mars has 2 satellites (Phobos & Deimos).
