# Seismic Wave Properties & S-Wave Shadow Zone (CAPF 2021)

## Question Statement (CAPF 2021)
Which one of the following statements regarding S-waves is NOT correct?

(a) S-waves are slower than P-waves (b) S-waves can travel through liquids (c) S-waves create a larger shadow zone beyond 103° (d) S-waves are transverse body waves

---

## Solution & Intuition

**Correct Answer:** (b) S-waves can travel through liquids

### Key Concept Breakdown
S-waves (Secondary / Shear waves) cannot travel through liquids or gases because liquids lack shear strength. P-waves (Primary / Compressional waves) travel through solids, liquids, and gases. Liquid outer core blocks S-waves completely, creating a massive shadow zone beyond 103°.
