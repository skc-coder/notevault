# Rock Cycle & Lithification (CAPF 2024)

## Question Statement (CAPF 2024)
Which of the following processes are part of the Rock Cycle?

1. Lithification 2. Metamorphism 3. Weathering and Erosion 4. Crystallization

---

## Solution & Intuition

**Correct Answer:** All 1, 2, 3, and 4

### Key Concept Breakdown
The Rock Cycle is a continuous process where Igneous, Sedimentary, and Metamorphic rocks transform into one another through Weathering, Lithification, Metamorphism, Melting, and Crystallization.
