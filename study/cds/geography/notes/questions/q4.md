# International Date Line & GMT Time Difference (CAPF 2024)

## Question Statement (CAPF 2024)
If it is 31st Dec 2023 in New Zealand, at the same time what will be the date in the Hawaiian Islands and Alaska?

(a) Both have 01 Jan 2024 (b) Both have 30 Dec 2023 (c) New Zealand is 01 Jan 2024 while Hawaii is 31st Dec 2023 (d) Both have 31st Dec 2023

---

## Solution & Intuition

**Correct Answer:** (b) / (d) Date difference across International Date Line

### Key Concept Breakdown
Crossing the International Date Line (180° Meridian) from West to East (New Zealand to Hawaii/Alaska) subtracts 24 hours (1 day). Thus, when it is Dec 31 in NZ, Hawaii is still on Dec 30 / early Dec 31 depending on exact UTC offset.
