# Rocks, Minerals and Rock Cycle

Comprehensive, exhaustive master concept notes on Rock Classification (Igneous, Sedimentary, Metamorphic), Mineralogy, and the Rock Cycle synthesized from Pages 95-103 of the Physical Geography PDF.

---

## Theory, Intuition & Key Concepts

### 1. Minerals & Rock Classification

#### A. Mineralogy Foundations
- A **Mineral** is a naturally occurring inorganic substance having an orderly atomic structure and a definite chemical composition.
- **Major Rock-Forming Minerals**:
  1. **Feldspar**: Makes up half of Earth's crust.
  2. **Quartz**: Major component of sand and granite. Highly resistant to weathering.
  3. **Pyroxene, Amphibole, Mica, Olivine**.

#### B. Three Primary Rock Types
Rocks are aggregates of one or more minerals classified by mode of origin into:
1. **Igneous Rocks** (Primary Rocks)
2. **Sedimentary Rocks** (Secondary / Layered Rocks)
3. **Metamorphic Rocks** (Changed-Form Rocks)

---

### 2. Igneous Rocks (Primary Rocks)

#### A. Formation & Characteristics
- Formed by the cooling and solidification of molten rock material (**Magma** inside Earth or **Lava** on surface).
- **Properties**: Non-stratified, crystalline structure, zero fossils (fossils are destroyed by extreme magma heat!).

#### B. Classification by Mode of Cooling & Location
1. **Plutonic (Intrusive) Igneous Rocks**: Cool slowly deep inside the Earth. Coarse-grained large crystals (e.g., **Granite**, **Diorite**, **Gabbro**).
2. **Volcanic (Extrusive) Igneous Rocks**: Cool rapidly on Earth's surface. Fine-grained small crystals or glassy texture (e.g., **Basalt**, **Obsidian**, **Pumice**).

---

### 3. Sedimentary Rocks (Layered / Fossiliferous)

#### A. Formation & Characteristics
- Formed by the accumulation, compaction, and consolidation of sediments (*Denudation $\to$ Deposition $\to$ Lithification*).
- **Properties**: Stratified (layered), contain **Fossils**, non-crystalline.

#### B. Classification by Mode of Origin
1. **Mechanically Formed**:
   - Formed by physical erosion and deposition (e.g., **Sandstone**, **Conglomerate**, **Limestone**, **Shale**, **Loess**).
2. **Organically Formed**:
   - Formed from accumulated plant/animal remains (e.g., **Chalk**, **Coal**, **Peat**, **Limestone**).
3. **Chemically Formed**:
   - Formed by precipitation of minerals from water solution (e.g., **Halite / Rock Salt**, **Gypsum**, **Potash**, **Limestone**).

---

### 4. Metamorphic Rocks & The Rock Cycle

#### A. Metamorphism Types & Metamorphic Rocks
- Formed under extreme **Thermal (Heat)** or **Dynamic (Pressure)** transformation (*Metamorphism* means change of form).
- **Foliation / Lineation**: Alignment of mineral grains in parallel bands/layers under pressure.

#### B. Parent Rock to Metamorphic Rock Transformations
- **Granite** $\to$ **Gneiss** (Thermal/Dynamic)
- **Clay / Shale** $\to$ **Slate** $\to$ **Schist**
- **Sandstone** $\to$ **Quartzite**
- **Limestone / Chalk** $\to$ **Marble**
- **Coal / Carbon** $\to$ **Graphite** $\to$ **Diamond**
- **Basalt** $\to$ **Amphibolite / Greenstone**

#### C. The Rock Cycle
- Continuous geological cycle where rocks transform:
  $$\text{Magma } \xrightarrow{\text{Cooling}} \text{ Igneous } \xrightarrow{\text{Erosion \& Lithification}} \text{ Sedimentary } \xrightarrow{\text{Heat \& Pressure}} \text{ Metamorphic } \xrightarrow{\text{Melting}} \text{ Magma}$$
