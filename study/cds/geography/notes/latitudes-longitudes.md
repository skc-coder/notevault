# Latitudes, Longitudes and Time Systems

Comprehensive, exhaustive master concept notes on Latitudes, Parallels, Longitudes, Meridians, Standard Time Systems, International Date Line, and Solstices/Equinoxes synthesized from Pages 35-55 of the Physical Geography PDF.

## Theory, Intuition & Core Concepts

### 1. Shape of Earth & Geographic Grid

#### A. Geoid Shape & Dimensions
- Earth is an **Oblate Spheroid** / **Geoid** (bulges at the equator, flattened at the poles due to axial rotation).
- **Equatorial Radius ($r_e$)**: $6,378.1 \text{ km}$ ($12,756 \text{ km}$ diameter).
- **Polar Radius ($r_p$)**: $6,356.8 \text{ km}$ ($12,714 \text{ km}$ diameter).
- **Difference**: Equatorial diameter is $\approx 42-43 \text{ km}$ larger than polar diameter.

#### B. Axis Inclination & Orbit Mechanics
- **Axial Tilt**: Earth's axis is inclined at an angle of **$23.5^\circ$** to the vertical (perpendicular to the orbital plane).
- **Angle with Orbital Plane**: Earth's axis makes an angle of **$66.5^\circ$** ($90^\circ - 23.5^\circ$) with its orbital plane.
- **Rotation**: Rotates from **West to East** (anticlockwise viewed from North Pole). Takes $23\text{h } 56\text{m } 4\text{s}$ (Sidereal Day).
- **Revolution**: Revolves around the Sun in an elliptical orbit taking $365.25 \text{ days}$.

### 2. Parallels of Latitude

#### A. Definition & Properties
- **Latitude**: Angular distance of a point north or south of the Equator, measured in degrees from the center of Earth.
- **Parallels**: Imaginary circles running parallel to the Equator East-West.
- **Key Characteristics**:
  - Distance between two consecutive $1^\circ$ parallels of latitude is approximately **$111 \text{ km}$** ($69 \text{ miles}$).
  - Circumference of latitude circles **decreases** from Equator ($0^\circ$) to the Poles ($90^\circ\text{N} / 90^\circ\text{S}$), where they shrink to a point.
  - Total number of latitude lines: $89 + 1 + 89 = 179$ parallels (if poles are counted as points).

#### B. Important Parallels of Latitude
1. **Equator ($0^\circ$)**: Great circle dividing Earth into Northern and Southern Hemispheres.
2. **Tropic of Cancer ($23.5^\circ\text{ N}$)**: Northernmost limit where Sun can be directly overhead.
3. **Tropic of Capricorn ($23.5^\circ\text{ S}$)**: Southernmost limit where Sun can be directly overhead.
4. **Arctic Circle ($66.5^\circ\text{ N}$)**: Limit of continuous 24-hour daylight/darkness in North.
5. **Antarctic Circle ($66.5^\circ\text{ S}$)**: Limit of continuous 24-hour daylight/darkness in South.

#### C. Tropic of Cancer Passing Countries & Indian States
- **Passes through 16 Countries**:
  - *North America*: Mexico, Bahamas.
  - *Africa*: Western Sahara, Mauritania, Mali, Algeria, Niger, Libya, Egypt.
  - *Asia*: Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman, India, Bangladesh, Myanmar, China, Taiwan.
- **Passes through 8 Indian States (West to East)**:
  $$\text{Gujarat} \to \text{Rajasthan} \to \text{Madhya Pradesh} \to \text{Chhattisgarh} \to \text{Jharkhand} \to \text{West Bengal} \to \text{Tripura} \to \text{Mizoram}$$
  - **Mnemonic**: *G*u*R*u *M*a*C*h*J*h*W* *T*r*M* $\to$ **GRMCJ WTM** (Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, WB, Tripura, Mizoram).
  - River **Mahi** crosses Tropic of Cancer twice in India!

### 3. Meridians of Longitude & Time Zones

#### A. Definition & Properties
- **Longitude**: Angular distance east or west of the Prime Meridian ($0^\circ$).
- **Meridians**: Semi-circles connecting the North Pole to the South Pole.
- **Key Characteristics**:
  - All meridians are of **equal length**.
  - Distance between meridians is maximum at the Equator ($\approx 111.3 \text{ km}$) and converges to **$0 \text{ km}$ at the Poles**.
  - **Prime Meridian ($0^\circ$)**: Passes through Royal Observatory at **Greenwich, London, UK**.
  - **Great Circles**: Any pair of opposite meridians (e.g., $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$) forms a Great Circle.

#### B. Standard Time Calculation Rules
Earth rotates $360^\circ$ in 24 hours:
$$360^\circ = 24 \text{ hours} \implies 15^\circ = 1 \text{ hour} \implies 1^\circ = 4 \text{ minutes}$$

- **East Gain, West Lose (E.G.W.L)**:
  - Places **East** of Greenwich are **Ahead** in time ($+$).
  - Places **West** of Greenwich are **Behind** in time ($-$).

#### C. Indian Standard Time (IST)
- **Standard Meridian of India**: **$82.5^\circ\text{ E}$** ($82^\circ 30'\text{ E}$) passing through **Mirzapur (near Prayagraj/Allahabad, UP)**.
- **IST Offset**: IST is **$5.5 \text{ hours ahead of GMT}$** ($+5\text{h } 30\text{m}$).
  $$\text{Calculation: } 82.5^\circ \times 4 \text{ min} = 330 \text{ min} = 5.5 \text{ hours}$$
- **Passes through 5 Indian States**:
  $$\text{Uttar Pradesh (UP)}, \text{Madhya Pradesh (MP)}, \text{Chhattisgarh}, \text{Odisha}, \text{Andhra Pradesh (AP)}$$
  - **Mnemonic**: **OUCAM** (Odisha, UP, Chhattisgarh, AP, MP).

### 4. International Date Line (IDL) & Seasonality

#### A. International Date Line ($180^\circ$ Meridian)
- Located along the $180^\circ$ longitude line across the Pacific Ocean.
- **Zig-Zag Nature**: Deviates from $180^\circ$ line to prevent splitting islands/countries (Bering Strait, Aleutian Islands, Fiji, Tonga) into two calendar dates!
- **Date Adjustment Rules**:
  - **Crossing IDL from West to East (Asia to America)**: **GAIN a day / Subtract 24 hours** (e.g., Sunday becomes Saturday).
  - **Crossing IDL from East to West (America to Asia)**: **LOSE a day / Add 24 hours** (e.g., Saturday becomes Sunday).

#### B. Solstices and Equinoxes
1. **Summer Solstice ($21\text{st}$ June)**:
   - Sun directly overhead at Tropic of Cancer ($23.5^\circ\text{ N}$).
   - Longest day in Northern Hemisphere. 24-hour daylight north of Arctic Circle.
2. **Winter Solstice ($22\text{nd}$ December)**:
   - Sun directly overhead at Tropic of Capricorn ($23.5^\circ\text{ S}$).
   - Longest day in Southern Hemisphere.
3. **Equinoxes ($21\text{st}$ March - Vernal / $23\text{rd}$ September - Autumnal)**:
   - Sun directly overhead at the Equator ($0^\circ$).
   - Equal day and night ($12 \text{ hours}$ each) across the entire Earth.

## Detailed PYQ Analysis (Pages 35-50)

### PYQ 1. Greenwich Time Calculation (CAPF 2021)
- **Question**: It is 12:00 Noon in India ($82.5^\circ\text{ E}$). On which meridian will it be 7:00 a.m. of the same day?
  - Time Difference $= 12:00 \text{ PM} - 7:00 \text{ AM} = 5 \text{ hours} = 300 \text{ minutes}$.
  - Longitude Difference $= 300 / 4 = 75^\circ$.
  - Target Longitude $= 82.5^\circ\text{ E} - 75^\circ = \mathbf{7.5^\circ\text{ E}}$.
