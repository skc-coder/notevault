# Variation 2: Lagrange Points Gravitational Equilibrium & Distance Variations

## Tricky Novel Variation Statement

Regarding the Sun-Earth Lagrange points ($L_1$ to $L_5$) used for space observatory placement, consider the following statements:

1. Lagrange point $L_1$ is located approximately $1.5 \text{ million km}$ ($15 \text{ lakh km}$) from Earth directly towards the Sun.
2. The distance between Lagrange point $L_1$ and Lagrange point $L_2$ is approximately equal to the Earth-Sun distance ($1 \text{ AU}$).
3. The Aditya-L1 satellite is positioned at $L_1$ to observe the Sun continuously without any eclipses or occultations.
4. $L_4$ and $L_5$ form stable equilateral triangles with the Sun and Earth, leading the Earth and trailing the Earth by $60^\circ$ in its orbit.

Which of the statements given above are **CORRECT**?

- **(a)** 1 and 3 only
- **(b)** 2 and 4 only
- **(c)** 1, 3, and 4 only
- **(d)** 1, 2, 3, and 4

---

## Detailed Solution & Trap Analysis

**Correct Option: (c) 1, 3, and 4 only**

### Statement Breakdown:
1. **Statement 1 is CORRECT**:
   - $L_1$ lies along the Sun-Earth line $\approx 1.5 \text{ million km}$ ($15 \text{ lakh km}$) inside Earth's orbit.
2. **Statement 2 is INCORRECT**:
   - Distance from $L_1$ to Earth $= 1.5 \text{ million km}$. Distance from Earth to $L_2 = 1.5 \text{ million km}$.
   - Therefore, distance between $L_1$ and $L_2 = 1.5 + 1.5 = 3.0 \text{ million km}$ ($30 \text{ lakh km} \approx 0.02 \text{ AU}$).
   - It is NOT $1 \text{ AU}$ ($150 \text{ million km}$ / $15 \text{ crore km}$)!
3. **Statement 3 is CORRECT**:
   - $L_1$ provides an unobstructed, continuous view of the solar atmosphere and solar flares.
4. **Statement 4 is CORRECT**:
   - $L_4$ and $L_5$ are stable points located $60^\circ$ ahead and behind Earth along its orbit.
