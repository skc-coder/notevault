# Variation 4: Longitude Time Difference Multi-City Derivation

## Novel Tricky Variation

If it is 12:00 Noon at 82.5° E (Indian Standard Time), calculate the exact local solar time at 7.5° W (Greenwich West longitude):
- Longitude Difference = 82.5° + 7.5° = 90°
- Time Difference = 90° × 4 min = 360 min = 6 hours
- Local Time = 12:00 PM - 6 hours = 6:00 AM of the same day!
