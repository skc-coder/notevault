# Koeppen Climate Classification and Indian Climate

Comprehensive, exhaustive master concept notes on Wladimir Köppen's World Climate Classification Scheme, Biomes, and Indian Monsoon Dynamics synthesized from Pages 205-228 of the Physical Geography PDF.

---

## Theory, Intuition & Key Concepts

### 1. Köppen Climate Classification Scheme

Wladimir Köppen (1900 / 1918) devised an empirical climate classification based on **temperature, precipitation, and vegetation zones**.

#### A. 5 Major Climate Groups (Capital Letters)
1. **A - Tropical Humid Climates**: Average temperature of coldest month is $\ge 18^\circ\text{C}$. Winterless!
2. **B - Dry Climates**: Evapotranspiration exceeds precipitation. Subdivided into Arid Desert ($W$) and Semi-Arid Steppe ($S$).
3. **C - Warm Temperate (Mesothermal) Climates**: Average temperature of coldest month is between $-3^\circ\text{C}$ and $18^\circ\text{C}$.
4. **D - Cold Snow Forest (Microthermal) Climates**: Average temperature of coldest month is $<-3^\circ\text{C}$; warmest month $>10^\circ\text{C}$.
5. **E - Polar Climates**: Average temperature of warmest month is $<10^\circ\text{C}$. Summerless!
6. **H - Highland Climates**: Cold climates due to high elevation.

---

#### B. Sub-Group Precipitation Letters (Lowercase)
- **f**: Fully humid / No dry season (*feucht* = moist).
- **m**: Monsoon climate (short dry season).
- **w**: Dry season in winter (*winter-dry*).
- **s**: Dry season in summer (*summer-dry*).

---

#### C. Summary Table of Köppen Climate Types

| Code | Climate Name | Key Characteristics & World Vegetation Regions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Af** | Tropical Wet (Rainforest) | High temp & rainfall all year round. **Selvas** (Amazon Basin, Congo, Indo-Malaysia). |
| **Am** | Tropical Monsoon | Heavy monsoon rainfall, short dry season (India, Myanmar, SE Asia). |
| **Aw** | Tropical Savanna | Distinct wet summer and dry winter. Tall grass with scattered trees (**Llanos, Campos, Sudan type**). |
| **BWh** | Subtropical Arid Desert | Hot desert ($h=$ hot). Sahara, Arabian, Thar, Atacama, Namib, Great Australian Deserts. |
| **BWk** | Mid-Latitude Cold Desert | Cold desert ($k=$ cold). Gobi, Taklamakan, Patagonia Deserts. |
| **BSk** | Mid-Latitude Steppe | Temperate grasslands (**Steppes** - Eurasia, **Prairies** - N. America, **Pampas** - Argentina, **Veldt** - S. Africa, **Downs** - Australia). |
| **Csa** | Mediterranean Climate | **Dry hot summer ($s$) and wet mild winter ($w$)**. Citrus fruits, olives, evergreen oak! |
| **Cfb** | Marine West Coast | Mild winter, cool summer, humid all year (Western Europe, NZ). |
| **Cwg** | Humid Subtropical (Ganga Plain) | Dry winter, monsoon summer ($g=$ Ganga type in India). |
| **ET** | Tundra Climate | Mosses, lichens, flowering plants; permafrost. Warmest month $<10^\circ\text{C}$. |
| **EF** | Ice Cap Climate | Permanent ice and snow ($<0^\circ\text{C}$ all year). Greenland, Antarctica. |

### 2. Köppen Classification of Indian Climate

Köppen divided India into **8 Major Climatic Regions**:

```text
Amw  ---> Monsoon with short dry season (West Coast of India / Western Ghats)
As   ---> Monsoon with dry summer (Coromandel / Tamil Nadu coast - rains in winter via NE Monsoon!)
Aw   ---> Tropical Savanna (Most of Peninsular India south of Tropic of Cancer)
BWhw ---> Hot Desert (Extreme Western Rajasthan / Thar Desert)
BShw ---> Semi-Arid Steppe (Rain shadow zone of Western Ghats & NW India)
Cwg  ---> Damodar / Ganga Plain type (Northern Plains of India - UP, Bihar, WB)
Dfc  ---> Cold Humid with short summer (Arunachal Pradesh & Eastern Himalayas)
E / ET -> Polar / Tundra type (High Himalayas - Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Himachal)
```

> [!IMPORTANT] **High-Yield Exam Trap**:
> - **Tamil Nadu Coast (Coromandel Coast)** is classified as **As** (dry summer), because it receives its maximum rainfall during **Winter (Nov-Dec)** from the **Retreating North-East Monsoon**!
