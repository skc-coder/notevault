- **Luminous Intensity:** Measures visible light emitted in a specific direction, weighted for human eye sensitivity; unit is the **candela (cd)**.
    
- **Parsec:** Astronomical distance unit equal to about $3.26$ light-years, defined by a trigonometric parallax of one arcsecond.
- **Pressure Units:** $1\text{ atm} = 101,325\text{ Pa} = 1.01325\text{ bar} = 760\text{ mmHg} = 14.696\text{ psi}$. The SI unit is the **Pascal ($\text{Pa}$)**.
    
      
    
- **Electromotive Force (EMF):** Work done per unit charge, dimension $[ML^2T^{-2}Q^{-1}]$, measured in volts ($\text{V}$).
    
      
    
- **Impulse:** Change in momentum, measured in Newton-seconds ($\text{N}\cdot\text{s}$).
    
      
    
- **Viscosity:** Fluid internal resistance to flow, measured in Pascal-seconds ($\text{Pa}\cdot\text{s}$).
    
      
    
- **Stress:** Deforming force applied per unit area; unit is the Pascal ($\text{Pa}$).
    
      
    
- **Strain:** Geometric deformation ratio; it is a dimensionless quantity with no units.

- **[Relative Density (R.D.)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_density)**, or specific gravity, is a unitless ratio comparing the density of a substance to the density of a reference fluid (usually pure water at \(4^{\circ }\text{C}\)).
- A **[hydrometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrometer)** operates on the principle of buoyancy (**Archimedes' Principle**). It is built with a graduated scale designed to read the **Relative Density (R.D.)** directly off the surface level of the liquid without requiring separate mass or volume calculations.
- Do not confuse a _hydrometer_ (measures liquid relative density) with a _hygrometer_ (measures air humidity).
- - **Calorie (cal):** Defined as the amount of heat energy required to raise the temperature of **1 gram of water by $1^{\circ }\text{C}$** (specifically from $14.5^{\circ }\text{C}$ to $15.5^{\circ }\text{C}$)
- **Joule (J):** The absolute mechanical and **SI standard unit** for heat energy. The conversion factor between the two is:  
    $1\text{\ calorie}\approx 4.184\text{\ Joules}$
