# Indian Economy Cheat Sheet

## Theory, Intuition & Formulas

The Indian Economy is a **mixed, agrarian, and developing economy**. It combines private initiative with public sector planning and intervention (concept coined by **J.M. Keynes**, adopted in India's **Industrial Policy of 1948**).

### Economic Sectors
- **Primary Sector**: Direct exploitation of natural resources (Agriculture, Forestry, Fishing, Mining, Dairy).
- **Secondary Sector**: Manufacturing & industrial transformation of primary inputs (Construction, Manufacturing, Electricity, Gas, Water supply).
- **Tertiary Sector**: Service provision supporting economic activities (Banking, Insurance, Transport, Telecom, Real Estate).
- **Quaternary Sector**: Knowledge-based activities (R&D, IT consulting, Financial planning).
- **Quinary Sector**: Top-level strategic decision-making (Highest government officials, media executive leadership).

## Important Historical Plans & Five-Year Plans (FYPs)

| Plan / Policy | Period | Model / Focus | Key Milestones & Initiatives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st FYP** | 1951–56 | **Harrod-Domar Model** | Focus on Agriculture, Irrigation, Power. Bhakra-Nangal, Hirakud, Damodar dams initiated. Target GDP: 2.1%, Achieved: 3.6%. |
| **2nd FYP** | 1956–61 | **Mahalanobis Model** | Focus on Heavy Industry & Rapid Industrialization. Steel plants established at Bhilai, Durgapur, Rourkela. |
| **3rd FYP** | 1961–66 | **Gadgil Yojana** | Focus on self-reliant & self-generating economy. Hindered by 1962 Sino-Indian War, 1965 Indo-Pak War & severe drought. |
| **Plan Holidays** | 1966–69 | Annual Plans | Devaluation of Rupee (1966), Green Revolution launched (HYV seeds, Norman Borlaug & M.S. Swaminathan). |
| **4th FYP** | 1969–74 | Ashok Rudra & Manne | Growth with Stability & Progressive Self-Reliance. **14 Major Banks Nationalised (1969)**, Pokhran-1 (1974). |
| **5th FYP** | 1974–78 | D.D. Dhar Model | **Garibi Hatao** (Poverty Eradication) & Self-Reliance. 20-Point Programme. Terminated early by Janata Party. |
| **Rolling Plan** | 1978–80 | Gunnar Myrdal concept | Initiated by Janata Government under Morarji Desai. |
| **6th FYP** | 1980–85 | Direct Attack on Poverty | **NABARD established (1982)**, EXIM Bank (1982), **6 more banks nationalised (1980)**. Target: 5.2%, Achieved: 5.7%. |
| **7th FYP** | 1985–90 | Pranab Mukherjee Model | Food, Work & Productivity. Introduction of Jawahar Rozgar Yojana (JRY 1989). Rapid growth of service sector. |
| **Annual Plans** | 1990–92 | Economic Crisis | Severe BOP Crisis, Foreign Exchange reserves plummeted. **LPG Reforms (1991)** launched. |
| **8th FYP** | 1992–97 | Rao-Manmohan Model | Human Resource Development, Employment, Infrastructure. India joined WTO (1st Jan 1995). Target: 5.6%, Achieved: 6.8%. |
| **9th FYP** | 1997–2002 | Growth with Social Justice | Focused on 7 basic minimum services. Target: 6.5%, Achieved: 5.6%. |
| **10th FYP** | 2002–07 | Target 8% GDP | Doubling per capita income in 10 years, reducing poverty by 5%. |
| **11th FYP** | 2007–12 | C. Rangarajan | **Faster and More Inclusive Growth**. Target: 9%, Achieved: 8%. |
| **12th FYP** | 2012–17 | Final FYP | **Faster, Sustainable and More Inclusive Growth**. NITI Aayog replaced Planning Commission in 2015. |

## Important Institutions, Committees & Regulatory Bodies

- **Planning Commission**: Formed 15th March 1950 (Extra-constitutional, advisory body). Advisory chair: Prime Minister.
- **National Development Council (NDC)**: Established 6th August 1952. Ultimate approval body for FYPs.
- **NITI Aayog (National Institution for Transforming India)**: Replaced Planning Commission on **1st January 2015**. Think-tank emphasizing **Cooperative Federalism**. PM is Chairperson.
- **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)**:
  - Established: **1st April 1935** under RBI Act, 1934 (Hilton Young Commission recommendation).
  - Nationalised: **1st January 1949**.
  - Central Bank, issuer of currency, lender of last resort, manager of Forex reserves.
- **NABARD (National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development)**: Established 12th July 1982 on **B. Sivaraman Committee** recommendation.
- **SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)**: Set up in 1988, given statutory powers via SEBI Act on 30th January 1992 (Narasimham Committee).
- **IRDAI**: Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority (Malhotra Committee recommendations, 1999).
- **CSO / NSO**: Central Statistics Office (established 1951) merged with NSSO into **NSO (National Statistical Office)** under Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) to calculate National Income / GDP.

## Key Terms, Concepts & Indicators

- **GDP (Gross Domestic Product)**: Total monetary value of all final goods and services produced *within the geographic boundary* of a country in a financial year.
- **GNP (Gross National Product)**: $\text{GNP} = \text{GDP} + \text{NFIA}$ (Net Factor Income from Abroad).
- **NNP at Factor Cost (National Income)**: $\text{NNP}_{\text{FC}} = \text{NNP}_{\text{MP}} - \text{Net Indirect Taxes} (\text{Indirect Taxes} - \text{Subsidies})$.
- **Human Development Index (HDI)**: Evaluates 3 dimensions:
  1. Long and healthy life (Life Expectancy at Birth).
  2. Knowledge (Mean years of schooling & Expected years of schooling).
  3. Decent standard of living (GNI per capita in PPP\$).
- **Inflation Metrics**:
  - **WPI (Wholesale Price Index)**: Released by Office of Economic Adviser (DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce).
  - **CPI (Consumer Price Index)**: Released by NSO (MoSPI), measures retail price movement.
- **Types of Unemployment**:
  - **Disguised Unemployment**: Marginal productivity of labor is zero (predominant in Indian agriculture).
  - **Structural Unemployment**: Mismatch between available jobs and worker skill sets.
  - **Frictional Unemployment**: Temporary unemployment while switching jobs.
