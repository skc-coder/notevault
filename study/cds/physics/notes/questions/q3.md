# Q3: Total Internal Reflection Condition

## Question

Which of the following conditions is mandatory for Total Internal Reflection (TIR) to occur at an interface between two optical media?

A. Light must travel from an optically rarer medium to an optically denser medium.  
B. Light must travel from an optically denser medium to an optically rarer medium, and the angle of incidence must exceed the critical angle.  
C. The angle of incidence must be equal to $90^\circ$.  
D. The refractive indices of both media must be equal.  

---

## Detailed Solution

1. **Physical Principles of TIR**:
   - Total Internal Reflection occurs when a light wave travelling through a denser medium strikes a rarer medium boundary and is completely reflected back into the denser medium.
2. **Two Necessary Conditions**:
   - Light MUST travel from optically denser ($\mu_1$) to optically rarer medium ($\mu_2 < \mu_1$).
   - The angle of incidence $i$ MUST be strictly greater than the critical angle $\theta_c$ ($\sin\theta_c = \frac{\mu_2}{\mu_1}$).

---

## Correct Answer

**B. Light must travel from an optically denser medium to an optically rarer medium, and the angle of incidence must exceed the critical angle.**
