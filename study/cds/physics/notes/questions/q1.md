# Q1: Angle of Projection for Maximum Horizontal Range

## Question

At what angle to the horizontal ground should a projectile be thrown with a given initial speed $u$ so that its horizontal range on the ground is maximum?

A. $30^\circ$  
B. $45^\circ$  
C. $60^\circ$  
D. $90^\circ$  

---

## Detailed Solution

1. **Horizontal Range Formula**:
   $$R = \frac{u^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}$$
2. **Maximization Condition**:
   For a given speed $u$ and acceleration due to gravity $g$, $R$ is maximum when $\sin(2\theta)$ attains its maximum possible value of $+1$:
   $$\sin(2\theta) = 1 \implies 2\theta = 90^\circ \implies \theta = 45^\circ$$
3. **Maximum Range Value**:
   $$R_{\text{max}} = \frac{u^2}{g}$$

---

## Correct Answer

**B. $45^\circ$**
