# Q2: Recoil Velocity of Gun

## Question

A gun of mass $4\text{ kg}$ fires a bullet of mass $20\text{ g}$ with a muzzle velocity of $400\text{ m/s}$. What is the recoil velocity of the gun?

A. $-2\text{ m/s}$  
B. $-4\text{ m/s}$  
C. $-0.5\text{ m/s}$  
D. $-1\text{ m/s}$  

---

## Detailed Solution

1. **Given Data**:
   - Mass of Gun ($M$) = $4\text{ kg}$
   - Mass of Bullet ($m$) = $20\text{ g} = 0.02\text{ kg}$
   - Bullet Velocity ($v$) = $400\text{ m/s}$

2. **Applying Conservation of Linear Momentum**:
   Initial momentum before firing = $0$.
   Final momentum after firing = $M V + m v$.
   $$0 = M V + m v \implies V = -\frac{m v}{M}$$

3. **Calculation**:
   $$V = -\frac{0.02\text{ kg} \times 400\text{ m/s}}{4\text{ kg}} = -\frac{8}{4} = -2\text{ m/s}$$

---

## Correct Answer

**A. $-2\text{ m/s}$**
