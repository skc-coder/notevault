# Optics and Optical Instruments

Geometrical optics, mirror/lens formulas, refraction, total internal reflection, dispersion, and vision correction.

---

## 1. Reflection of Light & Spherical Mirrors

- **Mirror Equation**:
  $$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$$
- **Linear Magnification**:
  $$m = \frac{h_i}{h_o} = -\frac{v}{u}$$
- **Applications**:
  - Concave Mirror: Headlights, solar furnaces, shaving mirrors, dentist mirrors (forms enlarged virtual image when $u < f$).
  - Convex Mirror: Rearview vehicle mirrors (provides wide field of view and erect diminished image).

---

## 2. Refraction & Total Internal Reflection (TIR)

- **Snell's Law**:
  $$\mu_1 \sin i = \mu_2 \sin r \implies \mu = \frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \frac{c}{v}$$
- **Critical Angle ($\theta_c$)**:
  $$\sin\theta_c = \frac{1}{\mu}$$
- **Total Internal Reflection**: Occurs when light travels from dense to rare medium with $i > \theta_c$.
  - Applications: Optical fiber telecommunications, mirage formation in deserts, sparkling of cut diamonds.

---

## 3. Thin Lenses & Human Eye Vision

- **Lens Formula**:
  $$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$$
- **Power of Lens ($P$)**:
  $$P = \frac{1}{f \text{ (meters)}} \quad (\text{Dioptre, D})$$

### Eye Vision Defects & Rectification

| Defect | Description | Cause | Corrective Lens |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Myopia** (Short-sightedness) | Distance vision blurred | Elongated eyeball / High lens curvature | **Concave Lens** ($P < 0$) |
| **Hypermetropia** (Far-sightedness) | Near vision blurred | Short eyeball / Low lens curvature | **Convex Lens** ($P > 0$) |
| **Presbyopia** | Loss of accommodation | Aging ciliary muscles | **Bifocal Lens** |
| **Astigmatism** | Distortion in orientation | Non-spherical cornea curvature | **Cylindrical Lens** |
