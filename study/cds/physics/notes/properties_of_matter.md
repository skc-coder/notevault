# Properties of Matter and Fluid Mechanics

Hydrostatics, Hydrodynamics, Elasticity, Pascal's Principle, Archimedes' Buoyant Force, Surface Tension, and Viscosity.

---

## 1. Elasticity & Stress-Strain

- **Hooke's Law**: Within elastic limit, stress is directly proportional to strain:
  $$\text{Stress} \propto \text{Strain} \implies Y = \frac{\text{Stress}}{\text{Strain}}$$
- **Young's Modulus ($Y$)**: Tensile stress per unit longitudinal strain ($Y = \frac{F/A}{\Delta L/L}$).

---

## 2. Hydrostatics & Pascal's Law

- **Fluid Pressure ($P$)**: Normal force per unit area:
  $$P = \frac{F}{A} \quad (\text{SI Unit: Pascal, Pa})$$
- **Pressure at Depth ($h$)**:
  $$P = P_0 + \rho g h$$
- **Pascal's Principle**: External pressure applied to enclosed static fluid is transmitted undiminished throughout the fluid and container walls.

---

## 3. Buoyancy & Archimedes' Principle

Any body fully or partially submerged in a fluid experiences an upward buoyant force equal to the weight of the fluid displaced:
$$F_B = \rho_{\text{fluid}} V_{\text{submerged}} g$$

- **Laws of Flotation**:
  1. Weight of body = Weight of displaced liquid.
  2. Center of gravity of body and center of buoyancy lie on the same vertical line.

---

## 4. Hydrodynamics & Surface Physics

- **Equation of Continuity**: Mass conservation for incompressible steady fluid:
  $$A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2 = \text{constant}$$
- **Bernoulli's Principle**: Energy conservation for ideal fluid:
  $$P + \frac{1}{2} \rho v^2 + \rho g h = \text{constant}$$
- **Surface Tension ($T$)**: Contractive surface force per unit length ($T = \frac{F}{L}$).
- **Viscosity & Stokes' Law**: Internal fluid friction force:
  $$F_v = 6 \pi \eta r v$$
- **Terminal Velocity ($v_T$)**: Steady maximum speed attained by falling sphere in viscous fluid:
  $$v_T = \frac{2 r^2 (\rho - \sigma) g}{9 \eta}$$
