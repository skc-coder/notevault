# Modern Physics and Communication

Cathode Rays, X-Rays, Photoelectric Effect, Mass-Energy Equivalence, Nuclear Physics, Semiconductors, and Communication Systems.

---

## 1. Photoelectric Effect & Quantum Physics

Einstein's Photoelectric Equation:
$$E = h \nu = W_0 + K_{\text{max}} = h \nu_0 + \frac{1}{2} m v_{\text{max}}^2$$

- **Work Function ($W_0$)**: Minimum photon energy to eject an electron from metal surface.
- **Threshold Frequency ($\nu_0$)**: Minimum light frequency below which no emission occurs.

---

## 2. Mass-Energy Equivalence & Nuclear Physics

- Einstein's Mass-Energy Relation:
  $$E = m c^2 \quad (c = 3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s})$$
- Nuclear Fission: Heavy nucleus splits into lighter nuclei releasing huge energy (basis of nuclear reactors and atomic bomb).
- Nuclear Fusion: Light nuclei combine at ultra-high temperature to form heavier nucleus (energy source of Sun and Stars).

---

## 3. Semiconductors & Modern Electronics

- **Intrinsic Semiconductors**: Pure silicon or germanium.
- **Extrinsic Semiconductors**:
  - **n-type**: Doped with pentavalent impurities (Phosphorus, Arsenic); majority carriers are electrons.
  - **p-type**: Doped with trivalent impurities (Boron, Indium, Gallium); majority carriers are holes.
- **Diode & Transistor**: p-n junction acts as a rectifier (converting AC to DC).
