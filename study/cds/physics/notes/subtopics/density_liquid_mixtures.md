# Density & Liquid Mixtures

Fundamentals of mass density, relative density with respect to water, and mixture density formulas for equal volume vs equal mass mixing.

---

## 1. Density & Relative Density

- **Mass Density ($\rho$ or $d$)**: Mass per unit volume of a substance:
  $$d = \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Volume}} = \frac{m}{V} \quad (\text{SI Unit: } \text{kg/m}^3)$$

- **Relative Density (Specific Gravity)**: Ratio of the density of a substance to the density of pure water at $4^\circ\text{C}$ ($1000 \text{ kg/m}^3$ or $1 \text{ g/cm}^3$):
  $$\text{Relative Density} = \frac{\text{Density of Substance}}{\text{Density of Water at } 4^\circ\text{C}}$$
  - Relative density is a dimensionless pure number (no units).

---

## 2. Density of Liquid Mixtures

When two non-reacting liquids $A$ and $B$ of densities $d_1$ and $d_2$ are mixed together, the overall effective density $D$ of the mixture depends on whether equal volumes or equal masses are combined.

---

### Case 1: Equal Volume Mixing ($V_1 = V_2 = V$)

If equal volumes $V$ of Liquid $A$ (density $d_1$) and Liquid $B$ (density $d_2$) are mixed:

- Total Mass:
  $$M_{\text{total}} = m_1 + m_2 = d_1 V + d_2 V = (d_1 + d_2) V$$

- Total Volume:
  $$V_{\text{total}} = V + V = 2V$$

- Mixture Density ($D$):
  $$D = \frac{M_{\text{total}}}{V_{\text{total}}} = \frac{(d_1 + d_2) V}{2V} = \frac{d_1 + d_2}{2}$$

> **Key Rule**: When equal volumes of two liquids are mixed, the resultant density is the **Arithmetic Mean (AM)** of their individual densities.

---

### Case 2: Equal Mass Mixing ($m_1 = m_2 = m$)

If equal masses $m$ of Liquid $A$ (density $d_1$) and Liquid $B$ (density $d_2$) are mixed:

- Total Mass:
  $$M_{\text{total}} = m + m = 2m$$

- Total Volume:
  $$V_{\text{total}} = V_1 + V_2 = \frac{m}{d_1} + \frac{m}{d_2} = m \left( \frac{1}{d_1} + \frac{1}{d_2} \right) = m \left( \frac{d_1 + d_2}{d_1 d_2} \right)$$

- Mixture Density ($D$):
  $$D = \frac{M_{\text{total}}}{V_{\text{total}}} = \frac{2m}{m \left( \frac{d_1 + d_2}{d_1 d_2} \right)} = \frac{2 d_1 d_2}{d_1 + d_2}$$

> **Key Rule**: When equal masses of two liquids are mixed, the resultant density is the **Harmonic Mean (HM)** of their individual densities.
