# Newton's Laws and Momentum

Fundamental principles governing force interaction, linear momentum conservation, and impulsive forces.

---

## 1. Newton's Three Laws of Motion

### First Law (Law of Inertia)

Every body continues in its state of rest or of uniform motion in a straight line unless compelled by an external force to change that state.

- **Inertia**: Qualitative resistance of a body to change its state of motion. Mass is the quantitative measure of inertia.
- **Types of Inertia**:
  - Inertia of Rest (e.g., dust falling from carpet when beaten with a stick; passenger falling backward when bus suddenly starts).
  - Inertia of Motion (e.g., athlete running before long jump; passenger falling forward when bus suddenly brakes).
  - Inertia of Direction (e.g., mud flying off tangentially from a rotating wheel).

### Second Law (Force and Momentum)

The rate of change of momentum of a body is directly proportional to the applied net force and takes place in the direction of force:
$$\vec{F} = \frac{d\vec{p}}{dt} = \frac{d(m\vec{v})}{dt} = m \vec{a}$$

- **SI Unit**: Newton ($\text{N} = \text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}^2$).
- **CGS Unit**: Dyne ($1\text{ N} = 10^5\text{ dynes}$).
- Practical Examples: Catching a cricket ball by pulling hands backwards increases interaction time $\Delta t$, thereby reducing impact force $F = \frac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$.

### Third Law (Action and Reaction)

To every action force, there is an equal and opposite reaction force acting on different bodies:
$$\vec{F}_{AB} = -\vec{F}_{BA}$$

- Key Concept: Action and reaction act simultaneously on two distinct objects, so they never cancel each other.
- Examples: Walking on ground (pressing earth backward), recoil of gun, rocket propulsion.

---

## 2. Conservation of Linear Momentum

In an isolated system without external forces ($\vec{F}_{\text{ext}} = 0$):
$$\frac{d\vec{P}_{\text{total}}}{dt} = 0 \implies \vec{P}_{\text{initial}} = \vec{P}_{\text{final}}$$

### Gun Recoil Derivation

Let a gun of mass $M$ fire a bullet of mass $m$ with velocity $v$.
- Initial Momentum: $P_i = 0$
- Final Momentum: $P_f = M V + m v$
- Applying Conservation:
  $$M V + m v = 0 \implies V = -\frac{m v}{M}$$
  *(The negative sign indicates recoil opposite to bullet direction.)*

---

## 3. Impulse

An impulse is a large force acting for a very short time interval:
$$\vec{I} = \vec{F} \Delta t = \Delta \vec{p} = \vec{p}_{\text{final}} - \vec{p}_{\text{initial}}$$
SI Unit: $\text{N}\cdot\text{s}$ or $\text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$.
