# Cell Biology & Genetics

## 1. The Cell — Fundamental Unit of Life
The cell is the basic structural, functional, and biological unit of all living organisms.

### Prokaryotes vs. Eukaryotes
| Feature | Prokaryotic Cell | Eukaryotic Cell |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nucleus** | Absent (membrane-bound region: nucleoid) | Present (well-defined with nuclear membrane) |
| **Membrane-bound Organelles** | Absent | Present (Mitochondria, ER, Golgi, etc.) |
| **Ribosomes** | 70S type | 80S type (Cytoplasm), 70S in Mitochondria/Plastids |
| **DNA structure** | Circular, single chromosome | Linear, multiple chromosomes with histones |
| **Examples** | Bacteria, Cyanobacteria (Blue-green algae), Mycoplasma | Plants, Animals, Fungi, Protists |

## 2. Key Cell Organelles & Functions

### 2.1 Cell Wall & Cell Membrane
- **Cell Wall**: Present in plants (cellulose), fungi (chitin), and bacteria (peptidoglycan). Absent in animal cells. Provides rigidity, shape, and protection.
- **Plasma Membrane**: Fluid Mosaic Model (Singer & Nicolson). Semi-permeable lipid bilayer with embedded proteins. Controls transport via diffusion, osmosis, and active transport.

### 2.2 Mitochondria — The Powerhouse
- Double-membraned organelle; inner membrane folded into **cristae** to increase surface area.
- Site of **Aerobic Cellular Respiration** and **ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate)** generation (Krebs Cycle).
- **Semi-autonomous**: Contains its own circular DNA and 70S ribosomes (can synthesize some of its own proteins).

### 2.3 Plastids (Plant Cells Only)
- **Chloroplasts**: Contain chlorophyll pigments for photosynthesis. Possess thylakoids (stacks called grana) and stroma. Semi-autonomous (have own DNA and ribosomes).
- **Chromoplasts**: Contain carotenoid pigments (red, orange, yellow); give color to flowers and fruits.
- **Leucoplasts**: Colorless plastids for storage:
  - *Amyloplasts*: Store starch.
  - *Elaioplasts/Aleuroplasts*: Store oils/fats and proteins.

### 2.4 Endoplasmic Reticulum (ER) & Golgi Apparatus
- **Rough ER (RER)**: Studded with ribosomes; site of **protein synthesis** and folding.
- **Smooth ER (SER)**: Lacks ribosomes; site of **lipid & steroid synthesis**, and **detoxification** of drugs/poisons in liver cells.
- **Golgi Body / Dictyosome**: Packages, modifies, and sorts proteins/lipids received from ER into vesicles for transport.

### 2.5 Lysosomes & Ribosomes
- **Lysosomes ("Suicide Bags")**: Contain hydrolytic digestive enzymes (active at acidic pH). Digest worn-out cell parts, foreign pathogens, or self-destruct cell during cellular damage (autolysis).
- **Ribosomes ("Protein Factories")**: Non-membrane bound organelles. Translate mRNA into proteins.

### 2.6 Cell Division — Mitosis vs. Meiosis
| Feature | Mitosis | Meiosis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Occurs in** | Somatic (body) cells | Germ (reproductive) cells |
| **Daughter Cells** | 2 identical diploid ($2n$) cells | 4 non-identical haploid ($n$) cells |
| **Chromosome Number** | Maintained ($2n \to 2n$) | Halved ($2n \to n$) |
| **Purpose** | Growth, tissue repair, asexual reproduction | Gamete formation (sperm/egg), variation via crossing over |

## 3. Human Physiology & Systems

### 3.1 Digestive System
- **Stomach**: Secretes HCl (kills bacteria, activates pepsinogen to pepsin) and Pepsin (protein digestion).
- **Liver**: Largest gland. Secretes **Bile** (stored in Gallbladder), which **emulsifies fats** (no digestive enzymes in bile itself).
- **Pancreas**: Secretes pancreatic juice (Trypsin for proteins, Lipase for fats, Amylase for carbohydrates).
- **Small Intestine**: Main site of complete digestion and nutrient absorption (via villi and microvilli).

### 3.2 Circulatory & Respiratory Systems
- **Heart**: 4-chambered in mammals/birds (2 atria, 2 ventricles), 3-chambered in amphibians/reptiles, 2-chambered in fish.
- **Blood Components**:
  - *RBCs (Erythrocytes)*: Anucleate (lack nucleus in humans), contain Hemoglobin for $O_2$ transport.
  - *WBCs (Leukocytes)*: Immune defense (Lymphocytes produce antibodies, Phagocytes engulf pathogens).
  - *Platelets (Thrombocytes)*: Blood clotting (Fibrinogen $\to$ Fibrin).
- **Respiration**: Gas exchange ($O_2/CO_2$) occurs in **Alveoli** of lungs via simple diffusion across capillaries.

### 3.3 Endocrine System (Hormones)
| Gland | Hormone | Function / Disorder |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pituitary ("Master Gland")** | Growth Hormone (GH), TSH, ACTH | Controls other endocrine glands; Deficiency $\to$ Dwarfism, Excess $\to$ Gigantism |
| **Thyroid** | Thyroxine (requires Iodine) | Controls Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR). Deficiency $\to$ Goitre, Hypothyroidism |
| **Pancreas** | Insulin & Glucagon | Insulin lowers blood glucose; Deficiency $\to$ Diabetes Mellitus |
| **Adrenal Gland** | Adrenaline ("Fight or Flight") | Increases heart rate, blood pressure, and alertness during emergency |

## 4. Human Diseases & Immunity

### 4.1 Infectious Pathogens
- **Bacterial Diseases**: Tuberculosis (*Mycobacterium tuberculosis*), Typhoid (*Salmonella typhi*), Cholera (*Vibrio cholerae*), Tetanus, Anthrax.
- **Viral Diseases**: Dengue, Rabies, Polio, Influenza, Hepatitis, AIDS (caused by HIV targeting T-helper cells).
- **Protozoan Diseases**: Malaria (caused by *Plasmodium*, transmitted by female *Anopheles* mosquito), Amoebiasis.
- **Fungal Diseases**: Ringworm, Athlete's foot.

### 4.2 Immunity & Vaccines
- **Active Immunity**: Body produces its own antibodies in response to antigen contact/vaccine (long-lasting).
- **Passive Immunity**: Ready-made antibodies directly injected into body (e.g., Anti-tetanus serum, Mother's Colostrum containing IgA antibodies) — instant but temporary protection.
