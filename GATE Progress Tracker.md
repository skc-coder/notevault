# 🎯 GATE Syllabus & Revision Tracker

Track your **PYQ Coverage (Pass 1 & Pass 2)**, **Revision Rounds (Rev 1, 2, 3)**, **Variation Practice (Set 1 & Set 2)**, and **Test Series (GO Overflow & GO Classes)** for every subject and chapter.

---

## 📌 Column Legend Explained

* **PYQ Pass 1 & Pass 2**: First complete solving of previous year questions, followed by second timed/error-review pass.
* **Rev 1, Rev 2, Rev 3**: 3 spaced revision cycles for core concept notes and formulas.
* **Var 1 & Var 2 (Variations)**: Solving modified, tricky, or twisted GATE-level variations of standard PYQs.
* **Test Series 1 (GO Overflow)**: Subject tests and topic tests on GATE Overflow platform.
* **Test Series 2 (GO Classes)**: Full-length tests, subject tests, and weekly quizzes from GO Classes.
