**Clock questions**
Hour and minute hand meeting points
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ebzs2Oaf8Jg
The formula **θ = |30H - 11M/2|** gives the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock.

### 1. **Minute Hand Movement**
- Moves 360° in 60 minutes → **6° per minute**
- So at `M` minutes, minute hand is at **6M°**

### 2. **Hour Hand Movement**
- Moves 360° in 12 hours → **30° per hour**
- Also moves **0.5° per minute** (since 30°/60 = 0.5°)
- So at `H` hours and `M` minutes, hour hand is at **30H + 0.5M°**
### 3. **Angle Between Them**
Difference = |(30H + 0.5M) - 6M| = |30H - 5.5M|

Since 5.5 = 11/2, we get:

> **θ = |30H - 11M/2|**


This gives the smaller angle between the two hands (if > 180°, subtract from 360° for the acute angle).

	Let's think about this per hour of actual travel time.
	**Without stoppage:** In 1 hour, the train covers 75 km.
	
	**With stoppages:** The train covers 60 km in 1 hour (including stop time).
	
	So in 1 hour of clock time, the train travels 60 km. But at its normal speed of 75 km/h, it would need only 60/75 = 4/5 hour to cover 60 km.
	
	Time spent traveling = 4/5 hour = 48 minutes  
	Time spent stopped = 60 - 48 = **12 minutes**
	
	**Answer: (b) 12 minutes**
