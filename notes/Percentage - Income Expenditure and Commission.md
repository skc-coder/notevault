> [!question]
> **Relative Savings Comparison (AFCAT 2 2025)**
> Person $A$ spends $60\%$ of his salary, whereas Person $B$ spends $75\%$ of his salary[cite: 1]. If the saving of $A$ is $20\%$ more than the saving of $B$, by what percentage is the income of $A$ less than the income of $B$?[cite: 1]
> - 1. $16\frac{2}{3}\%$
> - 2. $20\%$
> - 3. $25\%$
> - 4. None of these[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> Let incomes of $A$ and $B$ be $I_A$ and $I_B$.
> - Savings of $A = (100 - 60)\% \times I_A = 40\% \times I_A = \frac{40}{100} I_A$[cite: 1]
> - Savings of $B = (100 - 75)\% \times I_B = 25\% \times I_B = \frac{25}{100} I_B$[cite: 1]
> Given $S_A = S_B \times \left(1 + \frac{20}{100}\right) = \frac{120}{100} S_B$:
> $$\frac{40}{100} I_A = \frac{120}{100} \times \left( \frac{25}{100} I_B \right)$$[cite: 1]
> $$40 I_A = \frac{6}{5} \times 25 I_B = 30 I_B \implies \frac{I_A}{I_B} = \frac{30}{40} = \frac{3}{4}$$[cite: 1]
> Income of $A$ is less than $B$ by:
> $$\frac{4 - 3}{4} \times 100 = \frac{1}{4} \times 100 = 25\%$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: 3**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Dynamic Income and Expenditure Increase (AFCAT 1 2026)**
> A person spends $70\%$ of his income in a month[cite: 1]. If his income increases by $40\%$ and his expenditure increases by $30\%$, his savings increase by Rs. $1216$[cite: 1]. What is the person's original income?[cite: 1]
> - 1. Rs. $3475$
> - 2. Rs. $6400$
> - 3. Rs. $2482$
> - 4. Rs. $8960$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> Let original income $= 100x$[cite: 1].
> - Original Expenditure $= 70x$[cite: 1]
> - Original Savings $= 30x$[cite: 1]
> New conditions:
> - New Income $= 100x \times 1.40 = 140x$[cite: 1]
> - New Expenditure $= 70x \times 1.30 = 91x$[cite: 1]
> - New Savings $= 140x - 91x = 49x$[cite: 1]
> Change in savings:
> $$\Delta S = 49x - 30x = 19x$$[cite: 1]
> Given $19x = 1216$:
> $$x = \frac{1216}{19} = 64$$[cite: 1]
> $$\text{Original Income} = 100x = 100 \times 64 = \text{Rs. } 6400$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: 2**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Agent Commission Problem (Q8)**
> If a sales agent gets $5\%$ commission on his sales and deposits the remainder of Rs. $57000$ into the company account, find his total sales amount[cite: 1].
> - 1. $60000$
> - 2. $50000$
> - 3. $45000$
> - 4. $83000$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> Company deposit $= (100 - 5)\% = 95\%$ of sales[cite: 1].
> Let total sales be $S$[cite: 1]:
> $$95\% \times S = 57000 \implies S = \frac{57000}{95} \times 100 = 600 \times 100 = 60000$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: 1**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Stepwise Remainder Spending (AFCAT 2 2024)**
> Mayank spends $40\%$ of his salary on clothes and $\frac{1}{3}$ of the remaining on food[cite: 1]. If half of the balance after spending on clothes and food is Rs. $450$, find his salary[cite: 1].
> - A. Rs. $2250$
> - B. Rs. $3000$
> - C. Rs. $2750$
> - D. Rs. $5300$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> Let salary be $x$[cite: 1].
> - After clothes ($40\%$), remaining $= 60\% \text{ of } x = \frac{3}{5}x$[cite: 1].
> - Spends $\frac{1}{3}$ of remainder on food $\implies$ fraction left $= 1 - \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{3}$[cite: 1].
> - Remainder after food $= \frac{2}{3} \times \left(\frac{3}{5}x\right) = \frac{2}{5}x$[cite: 1].
> Given half of this balance is $450$[cite: 1]:
> $$\frac{1}{2} \times \left(\frac{2}{5}x\right) = 450 \implies \frac{1}{5}x = 450 \implies x = 450 \times 5 = 2250$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: A**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Homework: Multi-step Remainder Expenditure**
> A man spends $31\%$ and $9\%$ of his monthly income on food and clothing, respectively, and $\frac{1}{3}$ of the remaining on the education of children[cite: 1]. He saves Rs. $4500$ per month, which is equal to half the balance after spending on food, clothing, and education of children[cite: 1]. His monthly salary is:[cite: 1]
> - (A) Rs. $22,500$
> - (B) Rs. $20,650$
> - (C) Rs. $21,000$
> - (D) Rs. $26,700$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> 1. Combined initial spending $= 31\% + 9\% = 40\%$.
> 2. Remaining salary $= 100\% - 40\% = 60\% = \frac{3}{5}I$.
> 3. Education expenditure $= \frac{1}{3} \times \text{remaining} \implies \text{Balance left} = \frac{2}{3} \times \text{remaining} = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{3}{5}I = \frac{2}{5}I$.
> 4. Savings $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Balance left} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{2}{5}I = \frac{1}{5}I$.
> 5. Given savings $= \text{Rs. } 4500$:
>    $$\frac{1}{5}I = 4500 \implies I = 4500 \times 5 = 22500$$
> **Correct Option: (A)**[cite: 1]
