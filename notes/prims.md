**TL;DR — Prism, Dispersion & Rainbows**

  

- **Prism Refraction:** Bends light by an angle called the **Angle of Deviation ($D$)**.
    
      
    
- **Dispersion:** White light splits into 7 colors (VIBGYOR) due to different speeds/wavelengths.
    
      
    - **Violet:** Bends **most** (Shortest $\lambda$, slowest speed, highest $n$).
        
          
        
    - **Red:** Bends **least** (Longest $\lambda$, fastest speed, lowest $n$).
        
          
        
    - **Recombination:** An inverted prism merges the 7 colors back into white light.
        
          
        
- **Rainbows (Always opposite the Sun):**
    
      
    - **Primary (Inner / Brighter):** Dispersion + **1 TIR** + Refraction $\to$ **Red on top**.
        
          
        
    - **Secondary (Outer / Fainter):** Dispersion + **2 TIRs** + Refraction $\to$ **Red on bottom**.f

**TL;DR — Atmospheric Optics & Scattering**

  

- **Atmospheric Refraction:** Bending of light through changing air density layers.
    
      
    - **Star Twinkling:** Continuous refraction shifts point-source light (Planets don't twinkle because they are extended sources).
        
          
        
    - **Advanced Sunrise/Sunset:** Sun seen $2\text{ mins}$ early & $2\text{ mins}$ late ($4\text{ mins}$ total extra daylight).
        
          
        
    - **Mirage & Diamond:** Driven mainly by **Total Internal Reflection (TIR)**.
        
          
        
- **Scattering ($\propto 1/\lambda^4$):** Deflection of light by matter.
    
      
    - **Blue Sky:** Fine air molecules scatter short blue wavelengths more.
        
          
        
    - **Red Sunsets & Danger Signals:** Long red wavelength scatters the **least**, travelling furthest through atmosphere.
        
          
        
    - **Space Sky:** Black due to no atmosphere / no scattering.
        
          
        
- **Tyndall Effect vs. Scattering:** Scattering is the general light deflection; **Tyndall Effect** is the _visible path of light_ caused specifically by **colloidal/larger particles** (e.g., fog, dusty rooms).
