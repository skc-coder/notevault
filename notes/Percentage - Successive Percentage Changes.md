> [!formula]
> **Successive Percentage Formula**:
> For two successive percentage changes $x\%$ and $y\%$:
> $$\text{Net } \% \text{ Change} = \left( x + y + \frac{x \cdot y}{100} \right)\%$$[cite: 1]
> *Sign convention*: Use $+x$ for increase, $-x$ for decrease/discount[cite: 1].
> 
> **For Multi-Factor / 3D Geometry Changes And Proof**:
> Convert individual terms to fractional multipliers:
> $$V_{\text{final}} = V_{\text{initial}} \times \left(\frac{b_1 \pm a_1}{b_1}\right) \times \left(\frac{b_2 \pm a_2}{b_2}\right) \times \left(\frac{b_3 \pm a_3}{b_3}\right)$$

> [!question]
> **Successive Increase (AFCAT 2 2024)**
> If the price of a commodity is first increased by $20\%$ and then again increased by $20\%$, by how much percent has it increased overall?[cite: 1]
> - 1. $44\%$
> - 2. $20\%$
> - 3. $36\%$
> - 4. $48\%$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> - **Formula**: $\text{Net} = 20 + 20 + \frac{20 \times 20}{100} = 40 + 4 = 44\%$[cite: 1].
> - **Ratio**: $20\% = \frac{1}{5} \implies 5 \to 6$.
>   $$5 \times 5 : 6 \times 6 = 25 : 36 \implies \Delta = \frac{11}{25} \times 100 = 44\%$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: 1**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Mixed Two-Dimensional Area Change (Q6)**
> If the length of a rectangle is increased by $30\%$ and its breadth is decreased by $20\%$, find the percentage change in its area[cite: 1].
> - A. $4\%$ increase
> - B. $15\%$ increase
> - C. $10\%$ increase
> - D. $13\%$ increase[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> - $L: 10 \to 13$ (via $+30\% = +\frac{3}{10}$)[cite: 1]
> - $B: 5 \to 4$ (via $-20\% = -\frac{1}{5}$)[cite: 1]
> $$\text{Area}_{\text{initial}} = 10 \times 5 = 50 \quad (\text{or } 25 \text{ by simplifying } 10 \times 5 \text{ vs } 13 \times 4)$$[cite: 1]
> $$\text{Direct}: 25 \to 26 \implies \Delta = \frac{26 - 25}{25} \times 100 = \frac{1}{25} \times 100 = +4\%$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: A**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Commodity Expenditure Shift**
> The price of tomato increases by $10\%$. The consumption of tomato decreases by $15\%$. What is the percentage change in the expenditure on tomatoes?[cite: 1]
> - (A) $6.5\%$ decrease
> - (B) $7\%$ decrease
> - (C) $8\%$ decrease
> - (D) $7.5\%$ decrease[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> Since $\text{Expenditure} = \text{Price} \times \text{Consumption}$[cite: 1]:
> $$\text{Net } \% = +10 - 15 + \frac{(10)(-15)}{100} = -5 - 1.5 = -6.5\%$$[cite: 1]
> A negative value represents a $6.5\%$ decrease[cite: 1].
> **Correct Option: (A)**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Three-Dimensional Volume Variation (CDS 1 2024)**
> The length, breadth, and height of a cuboid are increased by $10\%$, $20\%$, and $50\%$ respectively[cite: 1]. What is the percentage increase in volume of the cuboid?[cite: 1]
> - (a) $100\%$
> - (b) $99\%$
> - (c) $98\%$
> - (d) $50\%$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> - Length: $+10\% = +\frac{1}{10} \implies 10 \to 11$[cite: 1]
> - Breadth: $+20\% = +\frac{1}{5} \implies 5 \to 6$[cite: 1]
> - Height: $+50\% = +\frac{1}{2} \implies 2 \to 3$[cite: 1]
> Multiply baseline and modified dimensions:
> $$\text{Initial Volume} = 10 \times 5 \times 2 = 100$$[cite: 1]
> $$\text{Final Volume} = 11 \times 6 \times 3 = 198$$[cite: 1]
> $$\% \text{ Increase} = \frac{198 - 100}{100} \times 100 = 98\%$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: (c)**[cite: 1]
