**Network address translation** (**NAT**) is a method of mapping an IP [address space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Address_space "Address space") into another by modifying [network address](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_address "Network address") information in the [IP header](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IP_header "IP header") of packets while they are in transit across a traffic [routing device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Router_\(computing\)).

A router at home assigns IP address to the host locally.
And the router communicates over the net on behalf of the local hosts.
The ISP provides IP only to the router.
