To determine the correct option, let's analyze the physics behind weightlessness in orbit:

  

1. **Sensation of Weight:** What a person perceives as "weight" is not the gravitational pull itself, but the contact support force (the normal reaction) exerted upward on them by a floor or surface.
    
      
    
2. **Orbital Motion:** The International Space Station (ISS) and the astronaut inside it are both freely falling around Earth under the influence of Earth's gravity with identical centripetal acceleration:
    
      
    
    $$a = \frac{G M}{r^2}$$
    
    Because both the station and the astronaut accelerate toward Earth at the exact same rate, the floor of the station does not push against the astronaut. Therefore, the normal reaction force $N = 0$.
