> [!formula]
> Multiplier & Percentage Change Formula:
> $$\% \text{ Increase} = \frac{\text{Final Value} - \text{Initial Value}}{\text{Initial Value}} \times 100$$[cite: 1]
> $$\% \text{ Decrease} = \frac{\text{Initial Value} - \text{Final Value}}{\text{Initial Value}} \times 100$$[cite: 1]

> [!trap]
> **Linguistic Trap: "Reduced to" vs. "Reduced by"**
> - **"A is reduced to 30%"**: The final value of $A$ is now $30\%$ of original ($100 \to 30$, decrease is $70\%$)[cite: 1].
> - **"A is reduced by 30%"**: The subtraction amount is $30\%$ of original ($100 \to 100 - 30 = 70$, final value is $70\%$)[cite: 1].

> [!question]
> **Q1: Base Inversion Problem**
> If $A$ is $20\%$ less than $B$, then $B$ is what percentage more than $A$?[cite: 1]
> - A. $25\%$
> - B. $30\%$
> - C. $55\%$
> - D. $75\%$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> - **Method 1 (Base 100)**: Let $B = 100 \implies A = 100 - 20 = 80$[cite: 1].
>   Excess of $B$ over $A = 100 - 80 = 20$[cite: 1].
>   $$\% \text{ more} = \frac{20}{\text{Base } A} \times 100 = \frac{20}{80} \times 100 = 25\%$$[cite: 1]
> - **Method 2 (Ratio Invariant)**: $20\% = \frac{1}{5}$[cite: 1].
>   $B = 5\text{ units} \implies A = 5 - 1 = 4\text{ units}$[cite: 1].
>   Difference $= 1\text{ unit}$.
>   $$\% \text{ more} = \frac{1}{4} \times 100 = 25\%$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: A**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Q2: Reversal to Attain Original Value**
> If $A$ is increased by $30\%$, then by what percentage should it be decreased so that it attains its original value?[cite: 1]
> - A. $\frac{300}{13}\%$
> - B. $\frac{20}{13}\%$
> - C. $\frac{11}{5}\%$
> - D. $\frac{6}{7}\%$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> Let original $A = 100$. After $30\%$ increase, $A' = 130$[cite: 1].
> To revert to $100$, reduction required $= 30$[cite: 1].
> Current base $= 130$[cite: 1].
> $$\% \text{ Reduction} = \frac{30}{130} \times 100 = \frac{300}{13}\%$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: A**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Q3: Comparative Income Shift**
> If the annual income of $X$ is $20\%$ more than that of $Y$, then the income of $Y$ is less than that of $X$ by $P\%$. What is the value of $P$?[cite: 1]
> - A. $36\%$
> - B. $16\frac{2}{3}\%$
> - C. $56\%$
> - D. $23\%$[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> $20\% = \frac{1}{5} \implies Y = 5\text{ units}, X = 5 + 1 = 6\text{ units}$[cite: 1].
> Income of $Y$ is less than $X$ by $1\text{ unit}$ on base $X = 6$ units[cite: 1]:
> $$P\% = \frac{1}{6} \times 100 = 16\frac{2}{3}\%$$[cite: 1]
> **Correct Option: B**[cite: 1]

> [!question]
> **Compound Comparison (AFCAT 2 2025)**
> $A$ is $80\%$ more than $B$, and $C$ is $48\frac{4}{7}\%$ less than the sum of $A$ and $B$ together[cite: 1]. Then $C$ is how much percent less than $A$?[cite: 1]
> - 1. $\frac{220}{9}\%$
> - 2. $30\%$
> - 3. $40\%$
> - 4. None of these[cite: 1]
> 
> *Solution:*
> 1. Ratio of $A$ to $B$:
>    $$A = B \times \left(1 + \frac{80}{100}\right) = B \times \frac{180}{100} \implies \frac{A}{B} = \frac{9}{5}$$[cite: 1]
>    Let $A = 9x$ and $B = 5x \implies A + B = 14x$[cite: 1].
> 2. Value of $C$:
>    $$48\frac{4}{7}\% = \frac{48 \times 7 + 4}{7}\% = \frac{340}{7}\% = \frac{340}{700} = \frac{17}{35}$$[cite: 1]
>    $C$ is $\frac{17}{35}$ less than $(A+B)$[cite: 1]:
>    $$C = (A + B) \times \left(1 - \frac{17}{35}\right) = 14x \times \frac{18}{35} = \frac{2 \times 18x}{5} = \frac{36x}{5} = 7.2x$$[cite: 1]
> 3. Percentage by which $C$ is less than $A$:
>    $$\text{Reduction} = A - C = 9x - \frac{36x}{5} = \frac{9x}{5} = 1.8x$$[cite: 1]
>    $$\% \text{ less} = \frac{A - C}{A} \times 100 = \frac{\frac{9x}{5}}{9x} \times 100 = \frac{1}{5} \times 100 = 20\%$$[cite: 1]
> 
> *(Since $20\%$ is not in options 1, 2, or 3, it corresponds to None)*[cite: 1].
> **Correct Option: 4 (None)**[cite: 1]
